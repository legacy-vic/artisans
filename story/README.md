# Story planning

- `season-1.md` is the working Season 1 plan for Artemis's storyline (1A, the even episodes, and 30A).
- `sources/episode-summaries.md` has condensed summaries of the Drive scripts: Eps 1A, 2 and 4, plus the old drafts of 10 and 14.
- `sources/notion-beats.md` is a snapshot of the CCG – Episode List Notion database. Notion itself is the source of truth.

Where they conflict, follow this order: the author's latest answers, then Notion, then the current scripts (1A, 2, 4), then the old drafts, then the wiki.
