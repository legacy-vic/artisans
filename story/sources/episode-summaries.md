# Episode script summaries (Artemis storyline)

> These are condensed scene-by-scene summaries of the author's Google Drive scripts, made 2026-11-27 for Season 1 planning. The scripts themselves are the source of truth. All the scripts are in one Drive folder.
> - **Current canon:** Ep 1A, Ep 2, Ep 4 (unfinished).
> - **Outdated drafts:** old Ep 10 and old Ep 14. The story still loosely follows them. Notion beats (`notion-beats.md`) take priority over these.

## Ep 1A: "Sic Parvis Magna" (Greatness from small beginnings)
A screenplay-style script covering Artemis from about age 8 to about 14. Time jumps are marked *[Two years later…]* and so on. Each jump opens on Kama's town hall, the zarbuck homestead and the bait shop, tracking Talvar's takeover.

**Kama, age 8**
- **Midori ("Yaya")** is a middle-aged female ringo who runs the "Little Helpers" orphanage by the lake. She stops the baker who is chasing Artemis for stealing bread. Artemis has white hair and immature claws.
- The orphans taunt him as **"Little Oiyas."** Oiyas is the death god, whose colour is white.
- **Ano** is the oldest bully. Artemis rams a door into him.
- **Tibs** is a male ringo who visits now and then. He touches Artemis's hair and says "Call me Uncle Tibs."
- Midori tells Artemis she was also "treated differently" as a child. Artemis asks: "How is not fighting back better?"

**Two years later, about age 10**
- Arvi, the bait-shop owner, throws Artemis into the road. Tibs sees it, and the shop later burns.
- **Afina** is about 8 and is told to keep Artemis out of trouble.
- Tibs serves a fine vex roast that he clearly didn't make locally.
- Artemis finds Midori's letters to the new king, **Onfroi**, begging for money.
- Tibs recruits Artemis for out-of-town jobs ("our secret… do you trust me?"). They go to Sukara, Kilan, Shur'iq, Pa'iq and Wapiti.

**One year later, about age 11**
- Talvar has annexed Kama, and there is graffiti against the takeover.
- A carriage passes carrying a pale princess with opraxi braids.
- Tibs returns after four months with **Azra**: short blonde hair, teal tattoos, from Morowai on Tabla Pisé, which was raided by pirates. Tibs "saved" her. She had a sister she argued with.
- Afina secretly earns tosis. Artemis promises never to leave her and Yaya again.
- A red note from the mayor says the orphanage will no longer be funded because of Talvar's demands.
- Midori confronts Tibs for sending her children on errands. She takes Azra with her: "I'm not leaving another child under Tibs' influence." Tibs: "I'm willing to make this a family business, Midori!"
- They leave Kama.

**One year later, about age 12, in Khamu (tan'we jungle, ytara trees)**
- Midori teaches the villagers from her family's map, written in hyenesh. "Icos… where the fair-skinned atlae come from." She tells Artemis: "do something that makes you feel like you belong."
- Elder Antekano suggests the **Kashif Trials**.
- Elder Azhotep does a double-take when he sees Artemis.
- Artemis, Afina and Azra all volunteer.
- **First trial, the obstacle course, under Marshal T'Akoteh:** Azra is pathfinder and Artemis supports. They win.
- **Four days later:** there have been "disqualifications and complications." The groups are reformed in fives.
  - Azra leaves for her friends, including **Amori**.
  - Artemis and Afina join **Ano, Aurel and Arya**. Aurel and Arya are siblings.
  - Their marshal is **Akkennoten**, who confiscates Artemis's hidden blade after he draws it on Aurel and Ano.
- **The hunt:**
  - Artemis disobeys and hunts with Afina. They kill a **labua**, and Afina finishes it, traumatised.
  - They meet a **tirvaan** with cubs, and Artemis shoots one of the cubs.
  - Akkennoten saves them and is badly wounded.
  - In the gully, Akkennoten stays to fight. Ano, who has changed his mind about Artemis, and Aurel jump back to help him. Artemis drags Arya away, with Afina.
  - **None of them are shown again.**
- **The anointing:**
  - Amori receives the mark of Isi and Azra the mark of Atta.
  - Artemis limps in **alone**, in shock. "Where's Afina?"
  - Midori has **vanished**. Tibs claims she left the kids in his care and says he has "a place in Trion."
  - Artemis is marked with twin jawline stripes.

**Two years later, about age 14, in Kama (now Icosianised)**
- At the abandoned orphanage: "Why did you leave? Why didn't you say goodbye?"
- **Azlan** crashes into him with fruit and calls him "Arty." His grandfather's saying: "A family is like a tree…" He invites Artemis to the harvest feast.
- Artemis and Azra are thieves in Tibs' gang, based in temple ruins east of town.
- A princess with glossy black hair arrives under Talvarian guard. Artemis stares, and Azra is jealous.
- Artemis makes Azra promise not to tell anyone about the zarbuck homestead.
- Tibs gives Artemis a caravan job, **Trion to Suran**, and mentions "the arrival of the kit."
- **That night:** a boy says Azra told the gang about the homestead job, but Azra seems confused, so it could be a frame.
- **The gang burns Azlan's homestead.** Artemis drags Azlan to safety. Azlan's father carries out Azlan's pregnant mother, then is cut down. *[To be continued]*

## Ep 2: "Of Kings & Fools"
*About five years later; Artemis is about 19.*

**The khovis eggs**
- Artemis and Azlan steal khovis eggs and are chased. Both eggs end up lost.

**The temple**
- An old temple with **dholesh** script (an Icos tongue), murals of atlae and ringo dancing with water, and a woman on a stone in the ocean.
- A lever raises a pillar bridge.
- Artemis: "We're not thieves anymore." Azlan: "I was never a thief."

**Dunio and Abasse (abunali, heterochromatic)**
- Villagers call Artemis "the white one… gift from the hidden ones."
- She foretells:
  - "You will enter a **white void**… The **god beyond the cracked wall** … holds immense power that you must take."
  - "You will place your trust in the untrustworthy… A reflection of yourself will surface…"
  - "The being that stops you… will be the last being you see."
  - He will say: "My demand is to be left alone."
- To Azlan: "Keep him on his path, righteous one, and your sacrifices will not go unnoticed."

**Azra in Dunio**
- Now a headhunter for Tibs, with B'Ajit and Altin. "He truly needs you this time."
- She mentions the old Bank of Pearls dream job.
- She shackles Artemis. He tells her Midori should have "seen you for the stray you were."
- **Shaman Ankti** honours Elder A'te, Akkennoten's mother, and orders a **Nalusi Ba'et** duel. Artemis wins by pushing Azra out of the ring, and his demand to be left alone is granted. Ankti quietly lets the boys escape.

**Novon and Trion**
- **Novon:** a poor town under Talvar, with raids, taken daughters, a burned orphanage and rising taxes.
- **Princess Koto Talvo** holds an audience, with Commander Jiro. She picks Artemis ("You, with the white hair") as delegate, over Jiro's "He is marked by death…"
- **Trion:** the Hunter's Guild outpost, Bo Lao Academy, and the Shrine of Ka'at, with murals of atlae and groll dancing.
- Artemis's speech: "if one part of it's hurting, then we're all hurting."

**The palace**
- Lord Commander Kazuro, Jiro's brother. Servants Niles (snobbish) and Cho Abbot (mute).
- Koto's family: her youngest brother fled to find battle, her sisters stopped writing four years ago, and her eldest brother vanished on a shipment to Crage. She has been an emissary since 6 and her parents ignore her.
- On the veranda, Artemis says: "It's not the life you chose."
- They spar, and Artemis flips her.

**Onfroi and Queen Kota**
- Onfroi knew Azlan's father, who sold him zarbuck: "I'm sorry, son."
- He baits Artemis. Artemis: "You feast while they starve." Onfroi: "What would you have me do?" Artemis: "Anything."
- Onfroi sets the test: escort a shipment **Trion to Sulala** in three days, "there and back, untouched." *[To be continued]*

## Ep 4: "The Sands of Bali" (unfinished, about 48%)

**The road and Pa'iq**
- Artemis and Azlan drive a zarbuck wagon of Trion "royal goods" with no escort. Saatra chase them, and Artemis throws jerky to lose them.
- **Pa'iq:** Artemis to a tjarka rider: "I was born in the clouds."

**The stables**
- **Tanzu "the Great"** has a fake bow, a foul mouth and wild hair, and their gender is ambiguous.
- **Akir** is a silent giant, Tanzu's "financial security."
- The stable owner beats a tjarka. Artemis nearly attacks him, and Azlan and Akir hold him back.

**The alehouse**
- Artemis: "I always end up proving people right." A priest tried to drown him at five, calling him "Filth… marked… demon."
- Azlan: "Dude. I don't hate you."
- Artemis distrusts Koto because she said nothing at dinner the night before.
- **Commander Jiro** arrives as a minder: "I'm only here to clean up whatever mess inevitably happens."

**Mandor Canyon**
- **Mirak-Yen'iq Sipa**, an old ringo, has lived there 57 years among the dra'iq bones of **Mephisto**, whom he killed.
- He sells garugala venom and Varrow's Leaf.
- Artemis sneaks into a hidden room and finds the scroll **"Art of Hidrokin: Neophyte II"** (in pumesh).
- Mirak speaks of the **jotuun**, whom the High King erased: "I prefer history not to be erased by an autocratic empire." Artemis shrugs it off.
- Mirak moves sand and the door with kinmatter.

**The massacre and the monastery**
- **Grolls** massacre Pa'iq and take the cargo and Tanzu.
- At the monastery ruins, Azlan's plan is to stampede the tulkai. **Artemis goes for the wagon, not the kid.**
- Jiro's line collapses, and Jiro dies.
- Artemis is gravely hurt (cracked ribs, a slashed shoulder).
- The knight who saved Tanzu and Artemis is **Princess Koto**, who ran away without telling anyone.

**Sulala**
- At the gate, Koto bluffs that she is the caravan's writ: "a gesture of continued trade after the summit."
- Fake customs guards take the wagon. In the chase, slavers corner them in the "Den of Teeth."
- **The cargo is sand:** "Whoever sent you played you for a fool."
- The slavers flee from a man in a keffiyeh with **kohl-ringed eyes** who stares at Artemis. He is **Amari, "the Tirvaan,"** a headhunter Tibs hired.
- Soldiers arrive. Koto steps down from a wagon "accompanied by a—". *[The script breaks off]*

## Old Ep 10: "Joint Efforts" (outdated draft; Notion beats take priority)
- They are fugitives in prison rags who arrived by ship at the tiscola lagoon, in the shanty market outside Suran.
- Aila sells "old world doubloons."
- **Koto, Aila and Tanzu:**
  - They work at a textile shop. Koto calls Aila "a figurehead's" opposite: a robber and killer. Aila: "my family—"
  - Tanzu is revealed as a girl.
  - In the town square Tanzu kills a guard: "Natural born killer."
  - The canals lead to an armory and **Amabi Mahuja**, a tinkerer who recognises Koto.
  - At the harbor they're attacked by wydjet. Tanzu is mauled protecting Koto and is saved by an unseen ballista.
  - Aila says Koto's friends "would still be alive if it weren't for him" (Artemis).
  - In the cathedral and dungeon they find Niles and Cho forced to serve the Keiran warriors. They free **Queen Ai and Prince Amersi**.
  - Explosions; Suran falls. Civilians refuse to help the queen.
- **Artemis, Azlan and Azra:**
  - At the hunters' lodge (keeper Venn), Azra offers a truce: "My gear, my hunt."
  - Farmer **Nirvaad**'s wife was taken by a six-taloned flyer, the "sand snatcher."
  - Artemis wounds an **occi**, which turns out to be the snatcher's prey.
  - In an underground temple: burned skeletons, dead **sacred yris**, and Azra smashes the creature's eggs.
- Marauders attack, and Artemis is thrown onto rocks and blacks out.

## Old Ep 14: "Strangers & Foes" (outdated draft)
- **Departure:**
  - The captain won't sail for Icos because of Nirva.
  - Koto proposes asking the Savvads for help. Ai and Amersi say the Savvads are "controlled by the bank."
  - Artemis is back. He was "carried away" by two figures (a thin one and a fat one; a third is hinted at) and saved by **Mirak**: "shook my faith."
- **At the lodge:** Azra warns Artemis to turn himself in. She is uneasy about the ritual murders, the slaughtered yris and the symbols drawn around corpses.
- **At camp:** Ai says Arvaan and Onfroi asked the "Czaar" to slay Nirva. Aila names herself **Aila Dueval**, and Amersi punches her.
- **Training:** Mirak: "**Kinmatter. The art of the jotuun.**" Artemis lifts and splits a boulder with his mind.
- **The pleiad:** Amabi worked for the Czaar and fled because "he knows far too much about their weaknesses." The pleiad are **Zuul's Hand**, the seven assassins.
- **Yeni, now Orsena:**
  - The guard Elora and Lord Commander Barsoli.
  - King Araeus Savvad praises Anteo Zuul and insults Arvaan. Queen Milera runs a spy network.
  - They promise help, but only after an "esteemed guest" arrives.
  - Amersi confesses his love. Koto and Amersi have been betrothed since childhood.
- **Cliffhanger:** a swordsman in a blue kimono recognises Koto. *[The draft ends]*
