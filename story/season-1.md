# Artisans: Season 1 plan (Artemis storyline)

> **Status:** working draft for discussion.
> - **Canon:** Episodes 1A, 2 and 4 (Google Drive scripts); the beats for Eps 6, 8 and 10 in the **CCG – Episode List** Notion database; the wiki at 211 ABU.
> - **Loose guides:** the old drafts of Eps 10 and 14.
> - **Proposed:** anything else, marked *(proposed)*.

## Structure
- **Season 1 has 30 episodes and two storylines.**
- **Episode 1 is split.** 1A belongs to Artemis. 1B belongs to a second character, whose story is separate from Artemis's events.
- **After Episode 1, Artemis's storyline takes the even-numbered episodes.** He has 15 in all: 1A, then 2 through 28. Some of them are told from another character's point of view; Ep 6 is Azra's.
  - *Open question:* is Ep 30 his, a shared finale, or the other storyline?
- **The odd-numbered episodes (3, 5, 7…) follow the other storyline** and are not covered here.

## Logline
White-haired orphans with nothing to their names start out in the slums of Kama and end up changing the known world. A thief who believes he is cursed is pulled into a royal feud, a war across two continents and a fight against a resurrected tyrant's enforcer. When the dust settles, a new High Queen sits on the High Throne and an era ends.

## Theme: *Sic Parvis Magna* ("greatness from small beginnings")
- **Artemis**, "the seed of Oiyas", believes "nothing about my life means anything." The season argues the opposite.
- **Belonging.** Midori tells him: "I want you to do something that makes you feel like you belong." Azlan's grandfather says: "A family is like a tree… the roots keep them all together." The found family of the season becomes the answer to the family Artemis lost at the Trials.
- **Power and trust.** Abasse warns: "You will place your trust in the untrustworthy." Tibs' love comes "with strings attached." In the end the threat is the very throne everyone defends.

## The endgame (decided)
1. **Augmen** returns and wreaks havoc across both continents. He **levels Karlaine**.
2. The **Blood Empress** uses the chaos to invade, with her **Gurtai** alliance and a blood-magic bond to the dra'iq **Scylla**.
3. The gang fights on **High King Alfor's** side, joined by **Queen Vaenera** and her dra'iq **Luscena**.
4. **Artemis stops Augmen.**
5. Revelations make **Alfor** the final threat, and he is **deposed**.
6. **Vaenera is chosen** to take the High Throne.

### Act I: Small beginnings (1A, 2, 4)

**Ep 1A. "Sic Parvis Magna"** (written)
- Artemis grows up from 8 to 14 in Kama and Khamu.
- At the Kashif Trials he shoots a tirvaan cub. Afina, Ano, Aurel, Arya and Akkennoten never come back.
- Midori vanishes and Tibs takes in Artemis and Azra.
- Artemis meets Azlan, then Tibs' gang burns Azlan's homestead and kills his father.

**Ep 2. "Of Kings & Fools"** (written)
- Five years later Artemis is about 19.
- Abasse's prophecy. Azra hunts Artemis for Tibs ("He truly needs you this time"). Artemis wins the Nalusi Ba'et, with his demand "to be left alone".
- His speech for Novon wins over Princess Koto.
- At the palace, Onfroi baits Artemis ("Anything.") and sets the Sulala shipment test.
- **Off-screen between Eps 2 and 4:** two nights at the palace, a tense dinner where Koto stays silent, and leaving with the "no escort" cargo.

**Ep 4. "The Sands of Bali"** (first half written)
- **Written:**
  - Pa'iq, where Tanzu and Akir are introduced.
  - Mirak and the hidrokin scrolls in Mandor Canyon.
  - Grolls massacre Pa'iq, and Artemis chooses the cargo over the kid. Jiro dies.
  - The knight is revealed as Koto. The cargo turns out to be sand.
  - In Sulala's alleys, **Amari, "the Tirvaan,"** the headhunter Tibs hired, scares off the slavers and stares at Artemis.
- **Still to write** *(proposed; Ep 8 needs these to have happened)*:
  - Who is with Koto.
  - Artemis and Azlan take **Aila Dueval** captive. Ep 8 calls her "previously their captive."
  - The way home to Trion.
  - **Artemis and Azlan are arrested**, and Koto feels she is to blame ("That was not my—").
  - Koto's guilt, and Tanzu's anger at her.

### Act II: Consequences (6, 8, 10, 12, 14)

**Ep 6. "Among Thieves"** (Notion beats). Told from **Azra's** point of view.
- **I:** Azra wakes from the **Cerulean Dream**: she and Artemis lie silent in a field of blue reeds, a peace like death. She takes it as an omen. She keeps a hyper-disciplined routine and clutches **a trinket Artemis gave her** years ago. She stands apart from the gang, and the spark she had in 1A is gone.
- **II:** Tibs pitches a **heist** that will settle everyone's debts, and he will lead point himself. He looks strained. In private he admits he is filling in for Artemis because she failed to bring him in: "It would've been perfect. You ever taste perfect, Azra?"
- **III:** Her crew is a rival who resents Tibs' favour, a Tibs fanatic, and an orphan boy who idolises her and whom she keeps at arm's length. The alarm is raised and someone Tibs promised would be fine dies. Azra saves the boy. Tibs snaps: "If you had done what you were told and brought my boy back!"
- **IV:** The law knows where the gang is hiding. Tibs: "That stubborn kid damned us all…" Azra: "You don't want him back. You want him obedient." Then: "…let him walk free." Tibs' paranoia becomes a full conspiracy: she let Artemis go.
- **V:** A friend of Azra's is killed as "disloyal." A bandit: "your friend, Artemis, will be getting his soon. No loose ends." Azra holds Tibs' gaze. Her loyalty is dead, though she hasn't acted on it yet.

**Ep 8. "Consequences of Conviction"** (Notion beats)
- **I:**
  - Artemis and Azlan are in **Trion's gaol**, where their relationship breaks down. Azlan is fed up with Artemis's recklessness, and Artemis won't take responsibility. They still have to work together.
  - **Tibs is a prisoner there too**, the result of Ep 6's raid. He plays the concerned father figure, and Artemis plays along while planning to leave him behind.
  - **Aila** is in the gaol as well. She looks out only for herself, and Artemis tries to win her trust.
- **II:**
  - Koto, wracked with guilt, pleads with Onfroi, who refuses. She learns that obedience isn't the same as doing right.
  - Tanzu won't wait for permission.
- **III:**
  - Artemis and Azlan clash with the other prisoners. Aila recognises one of them and helps beat him.
  - Tanzu breaks in and starts freeing prisoners. Koto follows her to stop her and gets pulled into the escape.
  - **The group refuses to free Tibs.** He laughs and is *proud* that Artemis finally refused to be manipulated. His last help is a secret passage to the abandoned Trion shipyard.
  - Artemis frees Aila, and they **escape by sea**, leaving Tibs behind.

**Ep 10. "Joint Efforts"** (Notion beats, plus the old draft)
- Suran's gates are closed to them, so they set up in the shanty district outside the walls and split up for money.
- **Koto, Aila and Tanzu:**
  - They work at a textile shop. Koto and Aila argue about privilege, poverty and whether Koto has ever earned anything.
  - Koto goes after the imprisoned prince. The three get in through the canals and learn the royals are held in a chapel-turned-prison. Tanzu accuses Koto of trying to prove something rather than owning the consequences.
  - Tanzu nearly dies protecting Koto from the city's predators. A tinkerer (Amabi) treats her, but she is out of action.
  - With Niles and Cho, Koto frees **Queen Ai and Prince Amersi**. Suran explodes into chaos. The queen is badly hurt, and the civilians refuse to help, which forces Amersi to face what his family's rule has done.
- **Artemis, Azlan and Azra:**
  - Artemis's recklessness nearly gets them thrown out of the hunters' lodge. **Azra** steps in with gear and offers a hunt.
  - They track a flying predator. Artemis provokes it, and they end up trapped in an ancient underground complex full of dead sacred yris and old victims. Azlan is sure they are walking to their deaths.
- **End:** the two halves are still apart but heading for the same place, and their separate aims merge into one effort.

### Act III: The rising storm (16, 18, 20) *(proposed)*
- **The "no loose ends" order and Amari:** proposed, the headhunter's job changes from bringing Artemis back to killing him, and the showdown follows.
- **Azra acts on her choice**, as Ep 6 sets up. She brings back the trinket and the Cerulean Dream.
- **The heist:** what it was, and why Artemis would have made it "perfect".
- **The truth about Afina and Midori.**
- **Augmen returns.** Neru'Augma completes the resurrection.
- **Karlaine falls** (about Ep 20). Augmen levels it, possibly during the Royal Conclave.

### Act IV: War of two continents (22, 24, 26) *(proposed)*
- The Blood Empress strikes, with Khusan's Gurtai and Scylla.
- **Azra meets Vaenera**, her half-sister, and Luscena.
- The gang allies with Alfor, and Kiro and Zuul's Hand fight alongside them.
- **Artemis stops Augmen** (about Ep 26). Payoffs for "the god beyond the cracked wall" and for Azlan's "sacrifices."

### Act V: A new era (28, and 30?) *(proposed)*
- The revelations: Theara's execution turns Kiro against Alfor. The Vessel. Alfor's plans for Vaenera.
- Alfor is **deposed**, and **Vaenera is chosen** High Queen.

## Character arcs

**Artemis** goes from cursed nobody to the one who stopped Augmen.
- **Flaw:** recklessness and grabbing for proof ("I need this"; choosing the cargo over Tanzu). Ep 8 makes him face what it costs.
- **Turn:** in Ep 8 he refuses Tibs' strings, and Tibs is proud of him for it. The rest of the season moves him from refusing to be used to choosing people over prizes.
- **Kinmatter growth:** hidrokin scrolls (Ep 4), then Mirak (Ep 12 or 14), then mastery (Act IV).

**Azlan, the "righteous one"**
- He is the moral spine who finally stands up to Artemis in Ep 8.
- His father's death in Ep 1A is still open. Does he learn that Tibs' gang caused it?

**Azra**
- She goes from Tibs' favourite to someone who chooses for herself.
- **Ep 6 is the turning point:** her loyalty dies.
- The trinket and the Cerulean Dream: the dream of her and Artemis lying still in blue reeds reads as a foreshadowed death or afterlife.
- Then the hunt with Artemis (Ep 10), and later meeting Vaenera.

**Tibs**
- A father figure whose love comes with strings.
- **Ep 6:** his heist collapses and he becomes paranoid, ordering "no loose ends."
- **Ep 8:** jailed, left behind, proud of Artemis. He is both his best and his worst self in one episode.

**Koto**
- She goes from obedient princess to someone who acts on conscience.
- Her guilt in Ep 8, her rescue of Suran's royals in Ep 10, and the pull between Amersi and Artemis.

**Tanzu**
- She acts first and asks later: the jailbreak in Ep 8, saving Koto in Ep 10.
- Her conscience works on Koto.

**Aila Dueval**
- Captive, then cellmate, then a reluctant ally who only looks out for herself.
- A strong candidate for "trust in the untrustworthy."
