# Artisans: Season 1 plan (Artemis storyline)

> **Status:** working draft for discussion.
> - **Canon:** Episodes 1A, 2 and 4 (Google Drive scripts); the beats for Eps 6, 8 and 10 in the **CCG – Episode List** Notion database; the author's answers in planning sessions; the wiki at 211 ABU.
> - **Loose guides:** the old drafts of Eps 10 and 14.
> - **Proposed:** anything else, marked *(proposed)*.

## Structure
- **Season 1 has 30 episodes and two storylines.**
- **Eps 1 and 30 are both split in half.** Artemis has **1A** and **30A**. The B halves belong to the other storyline and are separate from Artemis's events.
- **In between, Artemis's storyline takes the even-numbered episodes**, 2 through 28. Some of them are told from another character's point of view; Ep 6 is Azra's.
  - Including 30A, that's 16 episode slots. *Check:* is 30A the 15th episode (in place of one of 2–28), or does it make 16?
- **The odd-numbered episodes follow the other storyline** and are not covered here.

## Logline
White-haired orphans with nothing to their names start out in the slums of Kama and end up changing the known world. A thief who believes he is cursed is pulled into a royal feud, a war across two continents and a fight against a resurrected tyrant's enforcer. When the dust settles, a new High Queen sits on the High Throne and an era ends.

## Theme: *Sic Parvis Magna* ("greatness from small beginnings")
- **Artemis**, "the seed of Oiyas", believes "nothing about my life means anything." The season argues the opposite.
- **Belonging.** Midori tells him: "I want you to do something that makes you feel like you belong." Azlan's grandfather says: "A family is like a tree… the roots keep them all together." The found family of the season becomes the answer to the family Artemis lost at the Trials.
- **Power and trust.** Abasse warns: "You will place your trust in the untrustworthy." Tibs' love comes "with strings attached." In the end the threat is the very throne everyone defends.

## The endgame (decided)
1. **Augmen** returns and wreaks havoc across both continents. He **levels Karlaine**.
2. The **Blood Empress** uses the chaos to invade, with her **Gurtai** alliance and a blood-magic bond to the dra'iq **Scylla**.
3. The gang fights on **High King Alfor's** side, joined by **Queen Vaenera** and her dra'iq **Luscena**.
4. **Artemis stops Augmen.**
5. Revelations make **Alfor** the final threat, and he is **deposed**.
6. **Vaenera is chosen** to take the High Throne.

### Act I: Small beginnings (1A, 2, 4)

**Ep 1A. "Sic Parvis Magna"** (written)
- Artemis grows up from 8 to 14 in Kama and Khamu.
- At the Kashif Trials he shoots a tirvaan cub. Afina, Ano, Aurel, Arya and Akkennoten never come back.
- Midori vanishes and Tibs takes in Artemis and Azra.
- Artemis meets Azlan, then Tibs' gang burns Azlan's homestead and kills his father.

**Ep 2. "Of Kings & Fools"** (written)
- Five years later Artemis is about 19.
- Abasse's prophecy. Azra hunts Artemis for Tibs ("He truly needs you this time"). Artemis wins the Nalusi Ba'et, with his demand "to be left alone".
- His speech for Novon wins over Princess Koto.
- At the palace, Onfroi baits Artemis ("Anything.") and sets the Sulala shipment test.
- **Off-screen between Eps 2 and 4:** two nights at the palace, a tense dinner where Koto stays silent, and leaving with the "no escort" cargo.

**Ep 4. "The Sands of Bali"** (first half written; the revision will run much longer)
- **Written:**
  - Pa'iq, where Tanzu and Akir are introduced.
  - Mirak and the hidrokin scrolls in Mandor Canyon.
  - Grolls massacre Pa'iq, and Artemis chooses the cargo over the kid. Jiro dies.
  - The knight is revealed as Koto. The cargo turns out to be sand.
  - In Sulala's alleys, **Amari, "the Tirvaan,"** the headhunter Tibs hired, scares off the slavers and stares at Artemis.
- **Still to write:**
  - Who is with Koto.
  - **Aila Dueval is introduced.** She was first introduced in the old Ep 6. *How* they meet is still open and may change completely with the revisions.
  - The way home to Trion.
  - **Artemis and Azlan are arrested**, and Koto feels she is to blame. Ep 8 needs this.

### Act II: Consequences (6, 8, 10, 12, 14)

**Ep 6. "Among Thieves"** (Notion beats). Told from **Azra's** point of view.
- **Chronology:** Ep 6 likely happens **before Ep 4**. Tibs is arrested after the failed heist and has a while in Trion's gaol to think things over before Artemis arrives in Ep 8.
- **I:** Azra wakes from the **Cerulean Dream**: she and Artemis lie silent in a field of blue reeds, a peace like death. She takes it as an omen. She keeps a hyper-disciplined routine and clutches **a trinket Artemis gave her** years ago. She stands apart from the gang, and the spark she had in 1A is gone.
- **II:** Tibs pitches a **heist** that will settle everyone's debts, and he will lead point himself. The target is most likely the **Bank of Pearls**. He looks strained. In private he admits he is filling in for Artemis because she failed to bring him in: "It would've been perfect. You ever taste perfect, Azra?"
- **III:** Her crew is a rival who resents Tibs' favour, a Tibs fanatic, and an orphan boy who idolises her and whom she keeps at arm's length. The alarm is raised and someone Tibs promised would be fine dies. Azra saves the boy. Tibs snaps: "If you had done what you were told and brought my boy back!"
- **IV:** The law knows where the gang is hiding. Tibs: "That stubborn kid damned us all…" Azra: "You don't want him back. You want him obedient." Then: "…let him walk free." Tibs' paranoia becomes a full conspiracy: she let Artemis go.
- **V:** A friend of Azra's is killed as "disloyal." A bandit: "your friend, Artemis, will be getting his soon. No loose ends." Azra holds Tibs' gaze. Her loyalty is dead, though she hasn't acted on it yet.
- **About "no loose ends":** it shows Tibs' darker side, but **deep down he doesn't want Artemis dead**. He has no such attachment to anyone else in the gang.
- **Amari:** the first of three episodes in which he hunts Artemis and fails. *(Proposed: off-screen, or a brief cutaway.)*

**Ep 8. "Consequences of Conviction"** (Notion beats)
- **I:**
  - Artemis and Azlan are in **Trion's gaol**, where their relationship breaks down. Azlan is fed up with Artemis's recklessness, and Artemis won't take responsibility. They still have to work together.
  - **Tibs is a prisoner there too**, the result of Ep 6's raid. He plays the concerned father figure, and Artemis plays along while planning to leave him behind.
  - **The scene between Tibs and Artemis** (the author's beats):
    - Tibs has spent his time inside thinking it over and **learned the wrong lessons**. He pitches Artemis on **one last score**, and Artemis sees right through it.
    - Tibs says he has been planning a way out for about a week, with "his boys" on the outside ready to stage a prison break. "Common folk, prisoners, gaolers, I've got eyes and ears everywhere."
    - Artemis scoffs and asks what that has to do with him. Tibs's confidence cracks and he admits he has been struggling. Artemis: "Yeah, no kidding."
    - Tibs says Artemis **wasn't in the plan at first**, and he would rather not have chased him at all. He understands why Artemis ran off all those years ago and has been trying to change since. But it caught up with him ("…It always does"), and here he is.
    - He has been losing loyal friends: "Truth is, I've been running out of options… and running short on time." Artemis stays quiet and unmoved, sure it's manipulation.
    - "I only told Azra to go find you because I knew she could. She knew you the best out of any of us." This calls back to their confrontation in Ep 2.
    - Artemis, annoyed by the memories, turns to go. Tibs: "Well, do I have to beg, kid? Did you wanna get out of here or not?"
    - Artemis turns back: "All those years ago when I asked what happened to Midori, you said she was arrested… Was I a fool to trust you?" Tibs, stunned, hesitates: "No." Artemis: "Then where is she?"
    - Tibs squints, searching for words. That small hesitation sets Artemis off, and he steps in close: "Let me find out she died and I'll–"
    - Tibs cuts him off: **Midori is alive and has been living in Phylomina for the last few years.**
    - Artemis still has no real reason to trust him and steps back to think.
  - **Aila** is in the gaol as well. She looks out only for herself, and Artemis tries to win her trust.
- **II:**
  - Koto, wracked with guilt, pleads with Onfroi, who refuses. She learns that obedience isn't the same as doing right.
  - Tanzu won't wait for permission.
- **III:**
  - Artemis and Azlan clash with the other prisoners. Aila recognises one of them and helps beat him.
  - Tanzu breaks in and starts freeing prisoners **before Tibs' outside crew can act**, so his own breakout never happens. Koto follows her to stop her and gets pulled into the escape.
  - **The group refuses to free Tibs.** He laughs and is *proud* that Artemis finally refused to be manipulated. His last help is a secret passage to the abandoned Trion shipyard.
  - Artemis frees Aila, and they **escape by sea**, leaving Tibs behind.
  - As he leaves, Artemis repeats: **"Remember, Tibs. Let me find out."**
- **Amari** learns that his client has been arrested and **keeps hunting Artemis anyway**. This is his second failure.

**Ep 10. "Joint Efforts"** (Notion beats, plus the old draft)
- Suran's gates are closed to them, so they set up in the shanty district outside the walls and split up for money.
- **Koto, Aila and Tanzu:**
  - They work at a textile shop. Koto and Aila argue about privilege, poverty and whether Koto has ever earned anything.
  - Koto goes after the imprisoned prince. The three get in through the canals and learn the royals are held in a chapel-turned-prison. Tanzu accuses Koto of trying to prove something rather than owning the consequences.
  - Tanzu nearly dies protecting Koto from the city's predators. A tinkerer (Amabi) treats her, but she is out of action.
  - With Niles and Cho, Koto frees **Queen Ai and Prince Amersi**. Suran explodes into chaos. The queen is badly hurt, and the civilians refuse to help, which forces Amersi to face what his family's rule has done.
- **Artemis, Azlan and Azra:**
  - Artemis's recklessness nearly gets them thrown out of the hunters' lodge. **Azra** steps in with gear and offers a hunt.
  - They track a flying predator. Artemis provokes it, and they end up trapped in an ancient underground complex full of dead sacred yris and old victims. Azlan is sure they are walking to their deaths.
- **Kiro first crosses paths with the gang in Suran.** *(Proposed: Amabi fled the High King, so an Inquisitor sent to Suran after him could explain Kiro being there.)*
- **Amari:** his third failure. He is done with Artemis's games and **decides on his own to kill him**, though he was never hired to.
- **End:** the two halves are still apart but heading for the same place, and their separate aims merge into one effort.

**Ep 12. "Ab Ovo"** *(title may change; WIP in Notion)*

  - Suran's ritual murders and the symbols around corpses (Neru'Augma?).

### Act III: The rising storm (16, 18, 20) *(proposed)*
- **Amari's showdown.** He has gone rogue to kill Artemis, and Tibs, in gaol, doesn't know. Ep 16 or 18. Proposed: if Tibs ever learns of it, he turns against Amari.
- **The Bank of Pearls:** what the heist in Ep 6 was after, and its fallout. Orsena is jointly ruled by the Bank and the Savvad crown, so it is already the setting of Ep 14.
- **Azra acts on her choice**, as Ep 6 sets up. She brings back the trinket and the Cerulean Dream.
- **Midori in Phylomina.** Tibs says she is alive and living there. Phylomina is **Vaenera's** city, and Azra spent part of her childhood there. *(Proposed: Artemis's search for Midori takes the gang to Phylomina, which leads to Azra meeting Vaenera and to Vaenera's entry into the main story. It also tests whether Tibs told the truth: "Let me find out.")*
- **The truth about Afina.**
- **Augmen returns.** Neru'Augma completes the resurrection.
- **Karlaine falls** (about Ep 20). Augmen levels it, possibly during the Royal Conclave.

### Act IV: War of two continents (22, 24, 26) *(proposed)*
- The Blood Empress strikes, with Khusan's Gurtai and Scylla.
- **Azra meets Vaenera**, her half-sister, and Luscena.
- The gang allies with Alfor, and **Kiro**, their attacker in Ep 14, now fights alongside them.
- **Artemis stops Augmen** (about Ep 26). Payoffs for "the god beyond the cracked wall" and for Azlan's "sacrifices."

### Act V: A new era (28, 30A) *(proposed)*
- **Ep 28:** the revelations. Theara's execution turns Kiro against Alfor. The Vessel. Alfor's plans for Vaenera. Alfor becomes the final threat.
- **Ep 30A** is Artemis's half-finale: Alfor is **deposed**, **Vaenera is chosen** High Queen, and the new era begins. Proposed: the A and B halves bring both storylines to a close together.

## Character arcs

**Artemis** goes from cursed nobody to the one who stopped Augmen.
- **Flaw:** recklessness and grabbing for proof ("I need this"; choosing the cargo over Tanzu). Ep 8 makes him face what it costs.
- **Turn:** in Ep 8 he refuses Tibs' strings, and Tibs is proud of him for it. The rest of the season moves him from refusing to be used to choosing people over prizes.
- **Kinmatter growth:** hidrokin scrolls (Ep 4), then Mirak (Ep 12 or 14), then mastery (Act IV).

**Azlan, the "righteous one"**
- He is the moral spine who finally stands up to Artemis in Ep 8.
- His father's death in Ep 1A is still open. Does he learn that Tibs' gang caused it?

**Azra**
- She goes from Tibs' favourite to someone who chooses for herself.
- **Ep 6 is the turning point:** her loyalty dies.
- The trinket and the Cerulean Dream: the dream of her and Artemis lying still in blue reeds reads as a foreshadowed death or afterlife.
- Then the hunt with Artemis (Ep 10), and later meeting Vaenera.

**Tibs**
- A father figure whose love comes with strings.
- **He cares about Artemis**, and only about Artemis. "No loose ends" is his dark side talking; he doesn't truly want Artemis dead.
- **Ep 6:** his heist collapses and he grows paranoid.
- **Ep 8:** jailed after thinking it over, he **learns the wrong lessons** and tries "one last score" on Artemis. When his confidence cracks, the person underneath shows through: he says he never wanted to chase Artemis, has been trying to change, and is running out of friends and time.
- He gives up **Midori's whereabouts** under pressure, then is left behind, still proud of Artemis.
- He doesn't know that Amari has gone rogue.

**Amari, "the Tirvaan"**
- A headhunter known across Bali and Abu, hired by Tibs to bring Artemis in.
- He fails three times (Eps 6–10). His professional pride turns personal, and he **decides on his own to kill Artemis**.
- He keeps hunting after Tibs' arrest.
- His title echoes the tirvaan that took Afina's group in Ep 1A.

**Kiro**
- The High King's ward and the youngest of his Inquisitors, with elektrokin and the Vessel's stun baton.
- He meets the gang in Suran (Ep 10) and attacks them in Orsena (Ep 14), then becomes an ally in the war (Act IV).
- He turns on Alfor when he learns of Theara's execution (Act V).
- Proposed: he is the "reflection of yourself" from Abasse's prophecy, an orphan who was molded the way Tibs tried to mold Artemis.

**Koto**
- She goes from obedient princess to someone who acts on conscience.
- Her guilt in Ep 8, her rescue of Suran's royals in Ep 10, and the pull between Amersi and Artemis.

**Tanzu**
- She acts first and asks later: the jailbreak in Ep 8, saving Koto in Ep 10.
- Her conscience works on Koto.

**Aila Dueval**
- She is introduced in the revised Ep 4, with the circumstances still open.
- Cellmate in Ep 8, then a reluctant ally who only looks out for herself.
- A strong candidate for "trust in the untrustworthy."

## Open questions for the author
1. **The heist leg-work.** How does a Bank of Pearls heist, in Orsena in the Anklan Isles, end with Tibs in **Trion's** gaol? *(proposed options)*
   - **(a)** The target is a Bank of Pearls shipment or branch on Talvarian ground, near Trion.
   - **(b)** Tibs is caught in the Anklan Isles and **handed over to Trion**, because Onfroi wants him for the Kama gang or for Azlan's homestead.
   - **(c)** The gang's hideout is near Trion (Tibs "has a place in Trion"), and the raid at the end of Ep 6 is the arrest. The heist itself can happen anywhere.
   - With Ep 6 now before Ep 4, option (c) fits best: the raid at the end of Ep 6 ends with Tibs in Trion's gaol.
2. **How Aila is introduced** in the revised Ep 4.
3. **Midori.**
   - Why is she in Phylomina, and did she leave by choice?
   - In Ep 1A, Tibs tells the elders she "had things to do before she left her kids in my care." Later he told Artemis she had been **arrested**. Was that a lie on top of a lie, or did the arrest really happen and later lead to Phylomina?
   - What does Tibs still know that he isn't saying?
4. **Afina and Artemis's origin.** These are still open.
5. **Do the two storylines cross** before 30A/B?
6. **Tibs after Ep 8.** His crew is still outside with a breakout ready. Do they spring him later, off-screen or in an odd episode? *(Proposed: Tibs comes back free in Act III, which makes "Let me find out" a live threat.)*
