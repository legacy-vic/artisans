# Artisans: Season 1 plan

> **Status:** working draft for discussion.
> - **Canon:** Episodes 1, 2 and 4 (Google Drive scripts), plus the wiki at 211 ABU.
> - **Loose guides:** the old drafts of Episodes 10 and 14. Their numbering is outdated and they are summarised here only for beats.
> - **Proposed:** anything else, marked *(proposed)*.

## Logline
White-haired orphans with nothing to their names start out in the slums of Kama and end up changing the known world. A thief who believes he is cursed is pulled into a royal feud, a war across two continents and a fight against a resurrected tyrant's enforcer. When the dust settles, a new High Queen sits on the High Throne and an era ends.

## Theme: *Sic Parvis Magna* ("greatness from small beginnings")
- **Artemis**, "the seed of Oiyas", believes "nothing about my life means anything." The season argues the opposite.
- **Belonging.** Midori tells him: "I want you to do something that makes you feel like you belong." Azlan's grandfather says: "A family is like a tree… the roots keep them all together." The found family of the season becomes the answer to the family Artemis lost at the Trials.
- **Power and trust.** Abasse warns: "You will place your trust in the untrustworthy." In the end the threat is the very throne everyone defends.

## The endgame (decided)
1. **Augmen** returns and wreaks havoc across both continents. He **levels Karlaine**.
2. The **Blood Empress** uses the chaos to invade, with her **Gurtai** alliance and a blood-magic bond to the dra'iq **Scylla**.
3. The gang fights on **High King Alfor's** side, joined by **Queen Vaenera** and her dra'iq **Luscena**.
4. **Artemis stops Augmen.**
5. Revelations make **Alfor** the final threat, and he is **deposed**.
6. **Vaenera is chosen** to take the High Throne.

## Episode outline (about 15 episodes)

### Act I: Small beginnings (Eps 1–5)

**Ep 1. "Sic Parvis Magna"** (written)
- Artemis grows up from 8 to 14 in Kama and Khamu.
- At the Kashif Trials he shoots a tirvaan cub. Afina, Ano, Aurel, Arya and Akkennoten never come back.
- Midori vanishes and Tibs takes in Artemis and Azra.
- Artemis meets Azlan, then Tibs' gang burns Azlan's homestead and kills his father.

**Ep 2. "Of Kings & Fools"** (written)
- Five years later Artemis is about 19 and Azlan is his brother in all but blood.
- Abasse's prophecy in Dunio. Azra hunts Artemis for Tibs ("He truly needs you this time"). Artemis wins the Nalusi Ba'et.
- His speech for Novon wins over Princess Koto.
- At the palace, Onfroi baits Artemis ("Anything.") and sets the Sulala shipment test.

**Ep 3. (unwritten)** *(proposed beats; Ep 4 implies these)*
- Two nights at Trion Palace.
- A tense dinner where Onfroi slights Artemis and Koto stays silent. This is the root of Artemis's distrust in Ep 4.
- Azlan insists the papers matter.
- Departure with the "no escort" cargo. Koto secretly slips in among Jiro's knights.
- Tag scene: Tibs hires **Amari, "the Tirvaan."**

**Ep 4. "The Sands of Bali"** (written, unfinished)
- Pa'iq, where Tanzu and Akir are introduced.
- Mirak and the hidrokin scrolls in Mandor Canyon.
- Grolls massacre Pa'iq, and Artemis chooses the cargo over the kid. Jiro dies.
- The knight is revealed as Koto. The cargo turns out to be sand.
- At the end, Amari scares off the slavers and stares at Artemis.

**Ep 5. Sulala** *(proposed)*
- Finish the cliffhanger: who comes down from the wagon with Koto.
- Amari's hunt begins in earnest.
- The rigged sand cargo, together with Koto's disappearance, gets Artemis and Azlan **arrested**. Ep 10's old draft says Koto put them "behind bars", and Koto protests "That was not my—".
- The fallout between Artemis and Koto starts here.

### Act II: Fugitives (Eps 6–10)

**Ep 6. Jailbreak and the crossing** *(proposed, from old-draft clues)*
- Artemis and Azlan escape in prison rags.
- **Aila Dueval** joins them. Her friends died because of Artemis.
- Koto, now Tanzu's guardian, flees too.
- They sail for Suran. At sea they run into pirates or Nirva, then land at the tiscola lagoon.

**Ep 7. "Joint Efforts"** (old Ep 10, loose)
- **Artemis, Azlan and Azra:** Azra arranges a truce and a joint hunt. They track the six-taloned "sand snatcher" below the occi pit, and Azra smashes its eggs.
- **Koto, Aila and Tanzu:**
  - Tanzu is revealed as a girl and makes her first kill. She is badly hurt and Amabi treats her.
  - Koto frees **Queen Ai and Prince Amersi**, plus Niles and Cho, from the Keiran Clan.
- Suran burns.
- Cliffhanger: Artemis is thrown from the wagon and goes missing.

**Ep 8. The white void** *(proposed)*
- Two figures carry Artemis off.
- This connects to Suran's ritual murders, the slaughtered yris and symbols drawn around corpses. Proposed: this is the work of Neru'Augma, which is preparing Augmen's return.
- Mirak saves him, and Artemis's **kinmatter** wakes ("The art of the jotuun").
- Possible payoff of "a white void… the descent into darkness."

**Ep 9. "Strangers & Foes"** (old Ep 14, loose)
- The road to the Savvads. Kinmatter training with Mirak, and the boulder lift.
- Aila's Dueval name comes out and she fights Amersi.
- Amersi confesses his feelings, and the old betrothal surfaces.
- **The pleiad, Zuul's Hand,** emerges as a threat: Amabi fled the High King because he knows "their weaknesses."
- The King and Queen Savvad delay their help for an "esteemed guest."
- The swordsman in the blue kimono recognises Koto. Proposed: he is **Kendo Burinkan**.

**Ep 10. Karlaine falls** *(proposed midpoint)*
- The monarchs gather. Proposed: the Royal Conclave at Karlaine, with the stop at Wietmoire on the way.
- Neru'Augma completes the resurrection, and **Augmen levels Karlaine.**
- The answer to **why Tibs needs Artemis** pays off here. See open questions.

### Act III: War of two continents (Eps 11–13)

**Ep 11. The Blood Empress moves** *(proposed)*
- Oprax and the Gurtai under Khusan strike with Scylla while the realms are broken.
- **Azra meets Vaenera**, her half-sister, and Luscena. The question of Azra's identity (Kade Asher's daughter) comes to the surface.

**Ep 12. An uneasy alliance** *(proposed)*
- The gang joins Alfor's side, and **Kiro** and Zuul's Hand fight alongside them.
- The truth about **Afina and Midori** and Tibs' endgame. Artemis's final confrontation with Amari.
- Payoff for "a reflection of yourself will surface": Kiro (an orphan raised as a weapon) or Augmen.

**Ep 13. Artemis vs Augmen** *(proposed)*
- "The god beyond the cracked wall… holds immense power that you must take."
- **Artemis stops Augmen.** Azlan's "sacrifices" are noticed.
- The Blood Empress's forces are broken.

### Act IV: A new era (Eps 14–15)

**Ep 14. The revelations** *(proposed)*
- Alfor executed Kiro's guardian, Theara, and Kiro turns on him.
- **The Vessel**, a crashed ship in the far north.
- **Alfor's hidden plans for Vaenera.** He learned of her Zuul blood from Dendrilla's letter.
- What Amabi knows about Zuul's Hand.
- Alfor becomes the final threat.

**Ep 15. The High Throne** *(proposed)*
- Alfor is deposed and **Vaenera is chosen** High Queen.
- The gang's place in the new era. Seeds for Season 2.

## Character arcs

**Artemis** goes from cursed nobody to the one who stopped Augmen.
- **Flaw:** he grabs at power and proof ("I need this"; choosing the cargo over Tanzu).
- **Turn:** he chooses people over prizes.
- **Kinmatter growth:** hidrokin scrolls (Ep 4), then awakening (Ep 8), then mastery (Ep 13).

**Azlan, the "righteous one"**
- He goes from a self-described coward to the moral spine of the group.
- He freezes when it counts in Ep 4, and his plan works anyway.
- His father's death in Ep 1 is a wound waiting to open. Does he learn that Tibs' gang, and possibly Azra, caused it?

**Azra**
- She goes from Tibs' hunter to someone who chooses her own family.
- Truce in Ep 7, then meeting Vaenera in Ep 11, then her break with Tibs.

**Koto**
- She goes from caged figurehead to a ruler in her own right.
- Her rift with Artemis opens in Eps 5–6.
- Torn between Amersi and Artemis.

**Tanzu**
- A feral kid who gains a found family, a hidden girl and a would-be student of kinmatter.

**Aila Dueval**
- An untrustworthy ally with her own angle, trying to rebuild the Dueval underworld.
- A strong candidate for "trust in the untrustworthy."

## Open questions for the author
1. **Why does Tibs need Artemis?** Options:
   - Neru'Augma needs a white-haired vessel or catalyst to bring Augmen back. Ayala, Augmen's daughter, had white hair.
   - Tibs owes the Blood Empress.
   - Artemis is the key to the Bank of Pearls job.
2. **Afina's fate.** Did she die at the Trials, or was she taken? If taken, by whom: Tibs, Neru'Augma, or the "missing kids" rumours from the Trials?
3. **Midori.** Did she leave by choice, was she taken, or was she paid off by Tibs?
4. **Artemis's origin.** His white hair, Azhotep's double-take, his swiveling ear, "born in the clouds." Is he connected to the jotuun or the Zuuls?
5. **The cliffhanger in Ep 4.** Who is with Koto in Sulala?
6. **"The arrival of the kit"** (Tibs, Ep 1). What is it?
7. **Who is the "esteemed guest"** at the Savvads' court: Vaenera, Kiro, or Alfor's envoy?
