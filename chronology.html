    Chronology

    Every dated event in the notes. Years count before (BBU) and after (ABU) the Bicontinental Union; the present is 211 ABU.

    173 BBU

    • Athuran Groll Wastings

      War is waged on the entire groll population in the region of Abu by Alusha the Vengeful after her children were mutilated by members of a groll tribe in the then capital city of Sundiri.

    • Groll

      A jungle in central Athura was burned down in 173 BBU to cull a massive groll population, and those that slipped through the wastings simply slipped beneath Alusha's proverbial radar.

    159 BBU

    • Athuran Groll Wastings

      A few years after the wasting, and the division of the Athuran Sultanate, the jotuun started advocating on behalf of all groll to be accepted back into Athuran society.

    153 BBU

    • Athuran Groll Wastings

      Alusha the Vengeful would rise once again, reminding the nations of "what monsters the grolls truly were" in staged events that baited several groll tribes to attack her pawns.

    3 BBU

    • Temple of Khada

      Built about a dozen years ago, this temple stands to be one of the largest in all of Athura, although from the surface you wouldn't get this impression.

    0 ABU

    • Athuran Groll Wastings

      At the time of the Bicontinental Union, the grolls on Athura are few and far between, and the public opinion about them hasn't changed much.

    128 ABU

    • Ayala

      Born ~

    134 ABU

    • Anteo Zuul

      When General Tsavin invaded the Anklan Isles on his own in 134 ABU, Anteo did nothing, glad to see a realm weakened, and watched the jotuun take years to end it.

    • General Tsavin

      In late 134 ABU, while Anteo Zuul was High King, Tsavin invaded the Anklan Isles on his own, not on Anteo's orders, meaning to carve out a realm of his own.

    • Lorence

      It was called Mesui until General Tsavin took it first in his invasion of 134 ABU and renamed it Oricos.

    • The Crisis in Anklan

      General Tsavin launched his invasion on Orsena in late 134 ABU.

    135 ABU

    • Demeia

      It was liberated from General Tsavin's rule in 135 ABU, and the Temple of Yolotl was raised there as a shelter for refugees and jotuun.

    • Temple of Yolotl

      A temple in Demeia, erected in 135 ABU as a shelter for refugees and jotuun during The Crisis in Anklan.

    136 ABU

    • Augmen

      In 136 ABU he went with Prior Ad Regalis to the Anklan Isles.

    • Lorence

      Augmen's impatience led to its liberation in 136 ABU, and after Tsavin's death the isles' Icosi overlords renamed it Lorence, as they'd simplified Anklan'A into Anklan.

    • Prior Ad Regalis

      In 136 ABU he and his apprentice, Augmen, went to the Anklan Isles to aid against General Tsavin, and returned in 137 ABU, when Orsena was liberated.

    137 ABU

    • Anklan Isles

      From about 134 to 137 ABU, the isles were invaded by General Tsavin and freed with the jotuun's help, which left Anklan scarred.

    • Augmen

      He returned in 137 ABU, disregarded General Tsavin's threats against his captives, infiltrated his fortress, and killed him before they could be harmed.

    • General Tsavin

      He held out in his fortress with his garrison and his Abunali captives, threatening their lives if the jotuun marched, until Augmen infiltrated it and killed him in 137 ABU.

    • The Crisis in Anklan

      The invasion of the Anklan Isles by General Tsavin, from about 134 to 137 ABU, and the jotuun's intervention against it.

    • Tsavin's Fortress

      He held out here at the end of The Crisis in Anklan, and Augmen infiltrated it and killed him in 137 ABU.

    138 ABU

    • Augmen

      Around 138 ABU, soon after he returned from Anklan, Ayala was offered a place at the Temple of Isi.

    • Ayala

      Around 138 ABU, when she was about ten, she was presented with the opportunity of a lifetime: she'd excel past the rank of paladin, so long as she trained at the Temple of Isi, on Icos.

    • Prior Ad Regalis

      Around 138 ABU, he warned Augmen against speaking out when his daughter, Ayala, was offered a place on Icos, and Augmen backed down.

    173 ABU

    • Temple of Khada

      Swallowed by the sands of time, the temple is only accessible through a collapsed ceiling that leads to the top-most floor. Feeling very much like a catacombs, these floors house no dead, or at least they weren't intended to.

    186 ABU

    • Mephisto

      In 186 ABU, Mephisto was slain by Mirak who decided to take action against the dra'iq as he saw her laying waste to the villages that once existed where Pa'iq rests.

    196 ABU

    • King Arabah

      Died in Malu Palace

    • King Arabah

      A dignified and honest man, and a longtime friend of Alexium Ariadne, who took his place when he died in the attack of 196 ABU.

    • The Fall of Malu

      The dra'iq attack of 196 ABU that killed King Arabah, and made a king of the smuggler captain who drove it off, Alexium Ariadne.

    • The Fall of Malu

      For some time, the city of Malu was ruled by a dignified and honest man, King Arabah. One evening, the peace was disturbed. A rumble, followed by a crash emitted from the southern city wall.

    203 ABU

    • King Avishni

      Avishni's father, the king of Sukara before him, died of illness when Avishni was a baby, around 203 ABU.

    211 ABU

    • Dunis

      The matriarch, Elder A'Te, maintained her position within the village for many decades before eventually dying peacefully in her sleep at the start of 211 ABU.

    • Elder A'Te

      Died peacefully at the start of 211 ABU; Shaman Ankti, a close friend, was given temporary eldership.

    • Shaman Ankti

      A shaman of the Abunali in Dunis, about sixty-seven years old, who was given temporary eldership over the village when Elder A'Te died at the start of 211 ABU.

    • Shaman Ankti

      When A'Te died at the start of 211 ABU, Ankti was given temporary eldership until a successor could be anointed.

    Without a year

    • Ten-Year War

      Also called the Great Jotuun Purge: the war of extermination waged on the jotuun by Anteo Zuul and the ex-jotuun Augmen, unparalleled in history.

    • The Cremation of Linnenmoire

      A long time ago, around the beginning of the Old Kingdom. Linnenmoire, the first major city on the continent of Icos, housed a stolen Dra'iq egg underneath the keep.

    • The Crowning of Onfroi

      The judgment in Karlaine by which Aethel Zuul refused to recognize Cedric Talvo as king, and made a king of his fifteen-year-old son, Onfroi.

    • The Death of Archon

      The battles against the titans that cost Anteo Zuul his firstborn son, Archon Zuul.

    • The Fall of Azkassar

      The Kingdom of Azkassar was always real, even if some don't believe in it.

    • The Fall of Talvar

      The destruction of Talvar by a dra'iq, and the ruin of the Talvo Royal Family that followed, before the Bicontinental Union was formed.

    • The Founding of Trion

      How Cedric Talvo and Lord Hiro Kuroshi raised a new Talvar in southern Athura, in the years between The Massacre at Wietmoire and The Crowning of Onfroi.

    • The God Wars

      The wars between claimant factions of Azkassar. See The Fall of Azkassar.

    • The Massacre at Wietmoire

      The killing of much of the Talvo Royal Family at Wietmoire Manor, their family home. It's an open secret that it was Anteo Zuul's doing, by then High King.

    • The Peoples of Old Icos

      All of Icos at one point in time was tribal. Long ago, when Azkassar was the largest realm in all of Artisans, the people of Icos knew only a few native realms, and none of them…

    • Vaenera & Azra's Origin

      Once an infamous man, to both civilians and pirates alike, Kade Asher was the captain of a band of vagrants turned pirates.
