    The Death of Archon

    The battles against the titans that cost Anteo Zuul his firstborn son, Archon Zuul.

    Contents
    • The Frozen Cave
    • Vengeance
    • The High King's War

    The Frozen Cave

    Archon rushed into battle to aid his friends in Stade, in their fight to push the forces of Morgar back from their homeland. In the cold north, he and his friend Sabba discovered a regiment of Morgari forces holding out in a frozen cave with titans. Thinking the titans had sided with his enemy, Sabba ambushed the encampment, using plastite to collapse the cave. He killed the atlae, but the titans rose from the debris and turned on the two of them.

    Before they could flee to safety, Sabba was caught by a titan and eaten alive. Archon escaped by the skin of his teeth, but not before witnessing his friend's demise. He told his allies in Stade that the titans were allied with the enemy, which spurred them into intense hatred, as the titans had given their word that they'd never get involved in atlae affairs.

    Vengeance

    After recuperating, Archon and a few allies sought vengeance on the titan that killed Sabba. They killed one, but it wasn't the one they were looking for. This provoked the remaining titans into marching south and invading Morgar, killing a great many civilians.

    Seeing the carnage unfold upon his 'enemy' struck Archon with an unusual, intense feeling. Joras, another of his friends, opted to prevent the titans from reaching Stade. Archon agreed, only to realize they were far outmatched. Throughout his life, his arrogance and pride had led him from one battle to another, winning most, fearing little. It wasn't until he stood before the raging giants that he understood the feeling from earlier was fear. He knew he had no chance of stopping them without help from his father, the High King. He attempted to flee, but a titan grabbed him off of his mount, slammed him to the ground, and stepped on him, killing him instantly.

    The High King's War

    A day later, Anteo and Endelyn caught wind of their son's actions. The informant conveyed it pedantically and chronologically. Furious and impatient, Anteo barked at him to get to the point, and only then learned of his son's demise. Wasting no time, he prepared for war.

    Fearing her husband would meet a similar fate, Endelyn traveled to a jotuun monastery to plead for assistance. Already aware of the battle, the jotuun were planning to intervene. Prior Ad Regalis told her she could accompany him and his apprentice, Augmen.

    Anteo and his infantry battled the titans in the forests between Upror and Morgar, where the titans were slowly but eventually overpowered. Anteo demanded answers, but they remained silent. Before he could give the order to slay them, the jotuun intervened and saved individuals on both sides. Augmen was unlike the other jotuun: he fought with intent to kill, which is why they kept him in the back for most of the conflict. Only when they grew overwhelmed did they allow him to step forward, and Anteo saw it first-hand. Unwilling to stand down, Anteo threatened them to stay out of it or there'd be consequences. The jotuun, equally as stubborn, refused. Suddenly an army of titans revealed itself, rising over a northern ridge. Intimidated, Anteo begrudgingly retreated, while the jotuun stayed to clean up the mess.
