    Athuran Groll Wastings

    The eradication of the native grolls of Abu, begun by Alusha the Vengeful, and the violence that followed their return. As of 211 ABU, few grolls are left on Athura.

    Contents
    • 173 BBU
    • 159 BBU
    • 153 BBU
    • 0 ABU

    173 BBU

    War is waged on the entire groll population in the region of Abu by Alusha the Vengeful after her children were mutilated by members of a groll tribe in the then capital city of Sundiri.

    It didn't take much to convince the rest of Abu to rally to her cause, as there were very very few positive relations with grolls at the time, and as such, it only took a decade to completely eradicate the native groll population. A jungle in central Athura was burned down to cull a massive groll population.

    159 BBU

    A few years after the wasting, and the division of the Athuran Sultanate, the jotuun started advocating on behalf of all groll to be accepted back into Athuran society. This was successful, as the jotuun had learned how to speak the groll's native tongue, Upper Et'lesh & Lower Et'lesh, and it didn't take long for the grolls to learn how to speak pumesh as well.

    153 BBU

    Alusha the Vengeful would rise once again, reminding the nations of "what monsters the grolls truly were" in staged events that baited several groll tribes to attack her pawns. She began by plotting for the few Athuran leaders who sided with her to be taken by her closest followers, only to instigate contempt between the rulers and the grolls by making their comments and actions taken against the grolls publicly known. She set up staged, public executions by allowing the agitated grolls to mutilate the Athuran rulers as horrified onlookers watched the primal display. And as harsh as this was, it was exceedingly effective in twisting the narrative that the grolls were monsters and nothing more. Afterward, the local jotuun were left with no choice but to defend the few groll tribes who swore to peace, leaving the others to their fates at the hands of rogue atlae militia.

    0 ABU

    At the time of the Bicontinental Union, the grolls on Athura are few and far between, and the public opinion about them hasn't changed much. The few surviving tribes being safeguarded by the jotuun still remain active, though the pacifist mindset seems to be ever-quickly declining. Some grolls even become jotuun themselves, though the message spoken by Alusha and her followers remains echoing in the heads of both atlae and groll alike.
