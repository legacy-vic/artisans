    An ex-jotuun who became Anteo Zuul's enforcer, and broke the order's temples for him in the Ten-Year War. Once apprentice to Prior Ad Regalis, and father of Ayala, he died at the war's end. As of 211 ABU his stone spires still stand in Ancrage, and the zealots of Neru'Augma mean to bring him back from the dead.

    Appearance & Character

    Augmen had short, curly, tawny hair, orange eyes lined with kohl, and jagged stripes on both cheeks. He was carefree and cocky, with a short fuse, and cared far too much about how he was seen. Once he got going, his determination was relentless.

    History

    A father alone

    Augmen and Alibet had a daughter, Ayala, who was born with white hair. Alibet didn't want to keep her, but Augmen loved her regardless. A few days later, he awoke to find that Alibet had run off, leaving the two of them alone with nothing. Aniketh, his friend since childhood, came to visit and found him caring for the child by himself. They decided it was best to take her to Aniketh's family farm, where they felt she'd have a great upbringing.

    Neophyte

    After speaking with Aniketh's mother, the two men joined the jotuun, both in desperate need of purpose. They'd wanted to be jotuun since childhood, and the farm was large, with plenty of people to look after Ayala, so they felt justified in leaving her there. They went on several missions as neophytes, and returned when she was four. Augmen found his daughter socially inept for her age, and quickly learned why (see Ayala). He decided to take her with him, believing that the jotuun could offer her a better upbringing.

    He struggled the whole way through his training, while she surpassed all of her peers.

    Apprentice

    In 136 ABU he went with Prior Ad Regalis to the Anklan Isles. By then he was well comfortable as a jotuun. He and Ad Regalis were about on par in reputation, with Augmen the more proficient with kinmatter, and the less conflicted about questionable strategies to reach desirable outcomes. His impatience led to the liberation of Oricos (today Lorence), and several priors and the grand uthisha chastised him for it and sent him away for a time as punishment. He returned in 137 ABU, disregarded General Tsavin's threats against his captives, infiltrated his fortress, and killed him before they could be harmed. See The Crisis in Anklan.

    The rift

    Around 138 ABU, soon after he returned from Anklan, Ayala was offered a place at the Temple of Isi. Augmen disapproved, and made it very vocal, despite the warnings of Prior Ad Regalis and the other priors. He was forced to back down.

    He was still Ad Regalis's apprentice when Endelyn Larrensire accompanied them to the battle with the titans. Unlike the other jotuun, he fought with intent to kill, so they kept him in the back for most of the conflict, and let him step forward only when they grew overwhelmed. Anteo saw it first-hand. He was furious that the jotuun intervened, but Augmen's part in it earned his subtle respect. See The Death of Archon.

    In the years after the offer, he discovered and studied forbidden jotuun texts detailing an ancient form of kinmatter, quintecrakin. It led to his expulsion from the order, and a rift between him and Ayala. He killed Aniketh, his oldest friend, the night he was expelled (see Aniketh). He continued to practice and perfect it in secret, becoming more powerful, but also more corrupt. He became a feared and powerful figure in the underground world of Artisans, using his abilities for personal gain and manipulation.

    The Ten-Year War

    After his expulsion, Anteo sought him out: the jotuun he'd watched kill titans, now an exile. Some time during the war, Augmen let slip that he'd also been the jotuun who ended The Crisis in Anklan. He aided the Union in destroying the jotuun's temples and any who supported them. At Ancrage, he shattered the battlements with massive stone spires, and his battle with the jotuun inside left the city in ruins. He used quintecrakin throughout the war, always unseen. It's how one man broke temples for ten years. He was spending the lives of everyone he'd killed, and nobody lived to say so.

    Death

    He held back from the Temple of Isi for ten years, in exchange for his daughter's safety, and was killed by her hand there at the war's end. See Ten-Year War.

    Legacy

    The spires he threw are part of Ancrage now, the largest a monument to the families lost. Neru'Augma, a group of zealots, means to bring him back from the dead.
