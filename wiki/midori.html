    Also known as Mother Midori, Yaya

    The keeper of an orphanage in Kama, known to the children she raises as "Yaya." Artemis, Afina and Azra are among them. When she's introduced, the orphanage has fallen on hard times, and she's struggling to provide for those who depend on her.

    Appearance & Character

    Midori hums to herself as she works. She's gentle with children and stern when she has to be, and once faced down an angry baker with nothing but a washboard. She was treated differently herself at a young age, and believes mistakes are lessons best learned early.

    History

    Little Helpers

    Her orphanage stood near Kama's lake, far from the market road, beneath a hand-crafted sign in pumesh that read "Little Helpers," and below it, "Mother Midori's Orphanage." She would gather the children for nights of storytelling. The older ones earned money around town, and she wrote letters to the new king for aid. Though she didn't trust Tibs, she relied on him to look after the children and bring them food.

    Leaving Kama

    Once Kama fell under Talvar, the mayor could no longer fund the orphanage. Midori quarreled with Tibs over the errands he had been sending her children on, turned down his offer of money, and left Kama with Artemis, Afina and Azra.

    Khamu

    Elder Antekano let them stay in Khamu, where Midori taught the villagers and asked for nothing in return. Her lessons drew on a map of the known world, written in hyenesh, which her family had passed down and revised for generations. At Antekano's urging, she let the children enter the Kashif Trials, so long as they looked after one another.

    Story

    Spoiler: Episode 1A

    She was nowhere in the crowd when the kashif were anointed. Tibs told the elders that she'd had things to do, and had left the children with him. Two years later, the orphanage was boarded up and overgrown.
