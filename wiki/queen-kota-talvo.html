    The queen of the Kingdom of Talvar, seventy-three years old, and wife of King Onfroi Talvo. Born a Kuroshi, she's the daughter of Lord Akrio Kuroshi and Lady Sakuri Kuroshi, and granddaughter of Lord Hiro Kuroshi, who fled Oprax with his family. She keeps order in Trion and provides for Koto, the last of her children left to her, while her grandfather's people in Novon have come to hate her.

    Appearance & Character

    Kota has long, black, flowing hair and grey eyes. She's intelligent and lighthearted, yet authoritarian when it comes to order in Trion. When it's absolutely necessary to speak up, she fails to. Her hair and attire are in the care of her handmaiden, Cho Abbot.

    History

    Betrothal

    She was betrothed to Onfroi as a child, at her grandfather's request. Cedric turned down the High King's own daughter to keep his word. See The Crowning of Onfroi.

    Novon

    The year the taxes rose, the people of Novon, her grandfather's refugees, came to the gate of Trion and called for her by her family's name, not the king's. She stood at a window and couldn't make herself go down. The guards cleared the gate, and they've never called for her since.

    She still knows what's right, even when she can't bring herself to confront Onfroi to make the lives of those under the crown easier. What she can't say, she sends. It's split between Cho, her father, and a merchant of Trion who's paid not to wonder, as more than one name looks less suspicious than one bulk expenditure on a ledger. Her father gives his part out as his own, and takes the thanks. Novon doesn't know that she pays for its winter grain, and hates her all the same. Onfroi found it years ago, and has the treasury round the numbers off. Neither has ever said a word about it to the other.

    Story

    Spoiler: Episode 2

    She received Artemis and Azlan warmly on her return to Trion, and served them a soup passed down her family for generations. When Onfroi and Artemis came to words over Novon, she said nothing.
