    Suran

    Suran

    Show on the map

    A heavily fortified city located on the western coast of Athura. The city is known for its immense walls which are lined with armaments meant to defend against the large and hostile fauna of the Bali Desert, as well as to protect the city from sieges.

    Within the city, the population is primarily made up of aristocrats and middle-class individuals. With grand buildings, lush foliage lining the elegant boulevards and squares, and fountains dotted around the city. Beneath the streets lie a system of large canals and tunnels connecting the city's moat to the sea, built to protect the city from floods and also used to cache goods and equipment.

    Just outside the city walls is a shanty town, albeit not officially recognized by the kingdom. This shanty town is home to many of Suran's original families, who have been forced out of the city due to the ever increasing high cost-of-living and the many businesses favoring the wealthy.

    Presently, as of 211 ABU, Suran has fallen under the control of the Keiran Clan, a highly elite criminal organization based in Bali. The clan came to power after a brutal battle took place all over Suran between the Keiran's and the Dueval's, another criminal organization. During this battle, King Arvaan was killed by the Keiran's while trying to free his city from the warfare. Unfortunately, this enabled the Keiran's to take control of the city, capturing Queen Ai and Prince Amersi in the process. After the Knights of Suran fell, the Keiran's had all the power necessary to achieve victory over the Dueval Clan. Their reward being Suran, and the entire Bali region.
