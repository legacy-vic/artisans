    The head of the Talvo Royal Family before The Massacre at Wietmoire, husband of Queen Kaelyn Skyfall, and father of King Cedric Talvo. He went to face Anteo Zuul alone, and was killed. In 211 ABU his grandson, Onfroi, rules the Talvar raised by those he'd sent away.

    Appearance & Character

    Gideon was quite tall and strong, with a gruff voice. He was known for his distinctive level-headedness.

    History

    The secret crown

    Gideon was the son of Edmund Talvo, who never saw the west. His grandfather, Aldric Talvo, had seen Talvar as a boy, and lived long enough to tell Gideon about it, the knee included. A lord at most by law, Gideon went west and apologized to the hilltribes as no Talvo had, and they chose him as their king in secret. It took years. He walked alone to each tribe that had been warred on, sat with its dead, and was told every name. At each he said which of it was his family's doing, asked for nothing, and went on to the next. At the end he swore that no Talvo would kneel again, for anything, unless the tribes said so. Tormod Crannoch let him through the moors, and the tribes brought him their feuds at Moirlander's Respite. He settled them so well that the quiet in the west gave him away. See Talvo Royal Family.

    The refugees

    With Cedric and Lord Hiro Kuroshi, Gideon brought Opraxi refugees across the sea to Athura when they weren't safe anywhere in Icos, so that they might live peacefully in new lands. See Internal Struggle.

    Death

    He was away in Athura when his family was killed, and came home to the aftermath. He ordered Hiro to flee with Cedric and his grandson, believing the rest must be faced alone. He walked into High Sonham unannounced to face Anteo, who offered him Kaelyn's life if he knelt. He began to go down, and she told him not to, holding him to his oath. He stood. Kneeling would have undone everything he'd worked to rebuild, though nobody in that room would understand it. Anteo had him forced to his knees and beheaded him. See The Massacre at Wietmoire.

    Legacy

    The survivors raised a new Talvar, where his grandson, Onfroi, is king.

    Quotes
