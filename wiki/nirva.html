    Nirva

    Nirva

    Range
    Abunala Sea, Azka Sea (Suspected)

    A relatively new and extremely territorial sea Dra'iq that took a more serpentine physiology, Nirva lives in the warm waters of Abunala Sea. She lies dormant for months, sometimes even years, until something disturbs her frighteningly light slumber. Specifically, a ship sailing on the water's above. With relentless ferocity, she attacks by launching out of the water and wrapping her serpentine body around whatever she catches, constricting it and wrapping her deathly maw around it.

    Nirva used to roam onto the shores of both Icos and Athura some time ago, which allowed ships to pass through her sea undisturbed. That is, until Rurok swooped down sunk its talons into her, lifting Nirva into the air and carrying her above the seas. She was only able to escape its grip after spraying its face with lakes of water, spooking the creature. Rurok released its grasp and continued off, leaving Nirva plummeting into the sea. Since then, Nirva has never been seen leaving the water, although several plans have been hatched to lure Nirva out and find a method of leading Rurok back to the sea to once again fight and potentially kill Nirva so that naval trade in the sea may resume.

    Among the first ships she took was that of Tybal Zuul, the High King's eldest son, on his way to Trion.
