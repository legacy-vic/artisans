    The Opraxi lord who fled Oprax during its internal struggle, brought its refugees across the sea, and founded Novon. He helped King Cedric Talvo raise Trion, and his granddaughter, Kota, is queen of Talvar. He was the husband of Lady Emi Kuroshi, and father of Lord Akrio Kuroshi.

    History

    Flight from Oprax

    The advisor to the old Tennō gave Hiro's name to clan Honda, which had an old feud against his house. He succeeded in saving his family, and refused ever to return.

    The refugees

    Opraxi weren't safe anywhere in Icos. With Gideon and King Cedric Talvo, Hiro brought refugees across the sea to Athura, so that they might live peacefully in new lands. They were there when the Talvos were killed, and came home to the dead. On Gideon's order, Hiro fled to Athura with Cedric and Onfroi. See The Massacre at Wietmoire.

    Novon

    Hiro brought the refugees who built Novon and Greystone, and they lived under his protection and lordship. He asked only one thing of Cedric, that his granddaughter, Kota, be married to Cedric's son. See The Founding of Trion. He stood beside the boy in Karlaine, and said nothing when Aethel Zuul called his family lowborn. See The Crowning of Onfroi.

    Legacy

    His granddaughter, Kota, married King Onfroi Talvo and became queen of Talvar.
