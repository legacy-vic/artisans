    Talvo Royal Family

    Also known as House Talvo, The Talvos

    The royal family of the Kingdom of Talvar, and once kings in the far west of Icos.

    The Crown in the West

    The Talvos began as vassals of the Kalmeres, sent to a frontier nobody else wanted, with a title and no one to rule. For generations they cut roads, kept granaries open through winter, and settled feuds between the hilltribes without taking a side. They buried their own dead beside the tribes'.

    The hilltribes kneel to no one, and have no word for king. They have feuds, and now and then a person whose judgment even the losing side obeys. When the last tribe brings that person its feud, they're what the east would call a king. It can't be asked for or inherited, and it ends when the feuds stop coming. In this way a Talvo became king of Tuvar, in the west of Upror. Each Talvo after had to prove worthy again, and some didn't. Their seat was Talvar, a city-state and the only powerhouse on that side of the continent. The Talvos and the Crannochs had made peace with the hilltribes by the time Oswulf Talvo founded it, and the tribes allowed it to be built quietly, with no conflict.

    The Fall

    A colossal dra'iq destroyed Talvar, and before the Talvos could recover, Tarris Kalmere declared them guilty of treason, for raising High Sonham in hiding. The last king in the west, Conrad Talvo, bowed to save his family, and his crown was annulled. Afterward, the Kalmeres and Zuuls warred with the hilltribes to keep them weak. See The Fall of Talvar.

    Wietmoire

    The Talvos later built Wietmoire Manor, where much of the family was massacred (see The Massacre at Wietmoire). Anteo Zuul seized it afterward. Gideon, Cedric, and Lord Hiro Kuroshi were away in Athura at the time, bringing Opraxi refugees across the sea (see Internal Struggle). Gideon and Hiro were also storing part of the family's wealth there, which Cedric didn't know of until Hiro showed him (see The Founding of Trion).

    By law Gideon was a lord at most. The hilltribes chose him as king in secret, the first Talvo they'd chosen in well over a century. He went west himself and apologized as no Talvo had: his forebear knelt without asking them, and shouldn't have. It took years: he walked alone to each tribe that had been warred on, sat with its dead, was told every name, and said which of it was his family's doing. At the end he swore that no Talvo would kneel again, unless the tribes said so. Tormod Crannoch let him through the moors. The apology got him heard, and then the tribes began bringing him their feuds, in secret, at Moirlander's Respite. The Crannochs never relayed it to the Kalmeres. They found out from the quiet. The Merrowicks reported, as good news, that there hadn't been one feud in the west for years, and at Karlaine, somebody asked who was settling them. Anteo noticed on his own. He held the title of Duke of Tuvar, and no hilltribe had ever brought him a feud. He'd taken it for wildness, until he heard they'd all been brought to someone. His crown is why he's called king, and why Anteo couldn't let the family live.

    Gideon stayed to face Anteo alone. He sent the others away and went to High Sonham, which his family had built. He walked in unannounced, and refused to kneel. Anteo killed him and Kaelyn there (see The Massacre at Wietmoire). Since then, monarchs have been escorted there so that none can retrace the way.

    Gideon had told Cedric how to find High Sonham, and Cedric passed it on to Onfroi, who knows it still.

    The Crown in Athura

    The survivors raised a new Talvar in Athura (see The Founding of Trion), and Cedric returned to Icos to legitimize it. The same year Anteo Zuul died and Aethel Zuul took his place, Aethel granted kingship to Cedric's fifteen-year-old son, Onfroi (see The Crowning of Onfroi).
