    Jotu'un

    Also known as Jotuun

    The Jotu'un, or simply jotuun, are a religious order that dates back as far as the start of recorded history, of which, is as rich and detailed as both Icos and Athura combined.

    The jotuun were eradicated by Anteo Zuul in the Ten-Year War, and some of their knowledge and traditions went with them. They still kept written pieces of literature in their temples, and this knowledge was hoarded after their eradication.

    The massive cities and megastructures of both continents were raised with the jotuun's help, using kinmatter in their construction.

    Neophyte

    The entry-level rank for newly inducted members. They are in the process of learning kinmatter at the various temples, isolated from society so as to not taint their view of the world around them while in such an impressionable position.

    Paladin

    A higher rank where members undergo an apprenticeship with a jotuun prior. Occasionally, paladins educate neophytes at the temples on everything they've learned during their training.

    Prior

    The highest rank most average jotuun will attain. Priors are experienced in kinmatter and are responsible for carrying out missions with their apprentices to help civilians or solve environmental issues.

    Wanderer

    A rank for jotuun who have chosen to stay out of political affairs, but still protect the balance of nature and help civilians wherever possible. They may be unaffiliated with any particular temple, but still possess a deep knowledge of kinmatter and use it to serve the greater good.

    Uthisha

    The master rank, attained by jotuun who have exceeded average feats and talents. Uthisha have mastery over kinmatter and take on the most demanding missions.

    Grand Uthisha

    A very rare rank that is only attainable by a select few. Grand Uthisha possess great wisdom and are often called upon for guidance. They also conduct conferences with one another and decree what the entire jotuun order does.

    Precepts

    I
    Thou will detach oneself from a life of vanity and otherwise dark desires.

    II
    Thou will be tolerant and accepting regardless of creed, race, or religion.

    III
    Thou shall always offer your service to those in need.

    IV
    Thou shall not envy.

    V
    Thou shall not judge in place of the gods.

    VI
    Thou shall not kill unjustly.

    VII
    Thou shall always act in favor of balance, remembering that all life is interconnected.

    VIII

    Thou shall seek knowledge and wisdom, always striving to expand your understanding of kinmatter and the world around you.

    IX
    Thou shall remain modest and respect the will of the gods.
