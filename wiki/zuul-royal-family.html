    Zuul Royal Family

    Also known as House Zuul, The Zuuls

    The family that has held the High Throne of the Bicontinental Union since Anteo Zuul. They hold High Sonham and Wietmoire Manor. They're Uprorian by blood.

    Rise

    The Zuuls were nobles who lived in Endlemoire, the city of the Larrensires, in Stade. They rose to the Kalmeres' court as a reputable courtier's family, and seized titles when they had an opportunity, in steps: Lord when they came to court, Lord Paramount through a convenient death, and Duke of Tuvar when Talvar fell (see The Fall of Talvar). Aevor Zuul was head of the house then, and found High Sonham. They still hold Tuvar as of 211 ABU, as Archdukes, sworn directly to the king of Stade (see Titles and Ranks). They also hold the township of Wietmoire, which Anteo took from the Talvos with the manor, by right of conquest. For Wietmoire they owe fealty to the Larrensires, the Princes of Moirach, a sore point between the two houses. They were rivals to the Talvos.

    Appearance

    The Zuuls have pale skin, and a strong seed for black hair and blue eyes. Endelyn, being of Azkassari descent, was also pale, with red eyes and black hair streaked with bronze. Her children with Anteo had a mixture of their features.
