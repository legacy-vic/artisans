    Theara the Jotuun

    One of the last surviving jotuun of her day, and the first guardian of Kiro, whom she raised in hiding. She's dead by 211 ABU.

    History

    Seclusion

    Hidden away in a temple, Theara discovered a nascent form of kinmatter, elektrokin. Kiro was entrusted to her as an infant by his mother, an opraxi refugee in Upror who'd heard of someone kind in the ruins. He began learning elektrokin in Theara's care.

    Death

    She used her lightning once, to protect Kiro. It may not even have been seen, but lightning is loud, and Inquisitors are always on the prowl. People talk, and rumors spread. Alfor discovered her whereabouts and sought her out, discreetly accompanied by his Inquisitors. He confronted her on her loyalties, and upon confirming her unwillingness to cooperate, ordered her execution. See Kiro.
