    A late general in the service of the Zuul Union, whose castle, Tsavin's Fortress, still stands in the Anklan Isles.

    History

    The Crisis in Anklan

    In late 134 ABU, while Anteo Zuul was High King, Tsavin invaded the Anklan Isles on his own, not on Anteo's orders, meaning to carve out a realm of his own. He took Mesui first and renamed it Oricos (today Lorence), and by 135 ABU had taken Orsena, the last major stronghold. When the jotuun intervened, his last move was to send his army against them and the natives, and it was destroyed. He held out in his fortress with his garrison and his Abunali captives, threatening their lives if the jotuun marched, until Augmen infiltrated it and killed him in 137 ABU. See The Crisis in Anklan.
