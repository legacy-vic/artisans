    A thief, about sixty-two years old, who has the children of Midori's orphanage call him "Uncle Tibs." He leads a gang made up largely of those same children, grown older, and means to establish a crime syndicate and become wealthy.

    Appearance & Character

    Tibs is jovial in company, and drops it once he's alone with the person he wants something from. He flatters, deflects, and answers questions with questions.

    History

    Midori's Orphanage

    He came and went from Kama, bringing the orphans meals too fine to have been prepared locally, and Midori left them in his care despite her distrust. He persuaded Artemis to work jobs for him out of town by telling him it would help her, and asked only that he not tell her. The two traveled to Sukara, Kilan, Shur'iq, Pa'iq and Wapiti.

    Azra

    He rescued Azra when Morowai was raided, and brought her to Kama. See Vaenera & Azra's Origin.

    The quarrel

    When the orphanage fell on hard times, he returned after four months away, offering money. Midori had learned of the errands he sent her children on, and left Kama without taking it.

    The gang

    He took in Artemis and Azra after the Kashif Trials, telling the elders of the Abunali that he had a place in Trion. His gang made its home in the ruins of an old temple, where he called the two his favorite prodigies.

    Quotes

    Story

    Spoiler: Episode 2

    He sent Azra and her mercenaries to bring Artemis back to him. According to her, he truly needs him this time.
