    History

    The match

    Anteo Zuul wanted a Kalmere for his heir, Archon. Oriane was the only choice, and she was twelve years younger than him. Archon married Dendrilla Ariadne instead.

    Stade

    She was an only child until she was about eighteen, when Lanis was born, and she was passed over for him. As of 211 ABU, she still holds the castle, and is the one person Lanis can't order to say what she thinks. Her line comes after his daughters, Thea and Reine. She has a grandson, Marrec, and has never once put him forward. Houses have been writing to him for years, and she reads the letters first, and burns them. She and Lanis are close. He tells her what he tells nobody else, and she's the only one who knows what he'd say if he had to name an heir. See Houses of Stade.
