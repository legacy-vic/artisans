    Barrentyne

    Also known as House Barrentyne

    An Azkassari noble house, seated at Norvania.

    The Barrentynes want Reine crowned and grateful, but she was raised to be neither proud nor usable. They would absolutely hate to see her marry a Larrensire, which would be stealing their victory from them, but the need for men would give them very little choice. See Houses of Stade.
