    Morgar

    Also known as Kingdom of Morgar

    Morgar

    Show on the map

    The realm above Stade, in the land the Morgari called Morgai.

    History

    The realm takes its name from its first seat, Morgar, raised in imitation of the rulers down south in Linnenmoire. It took a long while to complete: a walled city atop a hill near the open plains, some miles south of where Ancrage sits today (see The Seat in the North). The threat of Morgar is what drove the Three Great Princes to form the Commonwealth of Stade.
