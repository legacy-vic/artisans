    The Orsena Civic Guard

    The Orsena Civic Guard is the primary law enforcement agency within the city of Orsena, renowned for its well-organized structure and highly disciplined personnel. Tasked with maintaining public order, safeguarding the city's citizens, and enforcing municipal law, the Civic Guard is a respected institution. However, despite their skill in handling civilian matters, they are notably inexperienced when it comes to military conflict, as their primary focus has always been urban security rather than battlefield engagement.

    Each regiment is led by a Regimental Commander, chosen for their exceptional leadership and tactical prowess. These commanders report directly to the Lord Commander, the supreme authority of the Civic Guard, who is appointed by the ruling sovereignty of Orsena. The Lord Commander coordinates the activities of the entire guard, ensuring that the regiments work in harmony to protect the city.

    Organization

    The Civic Guard is divided into four specialized regiments, each responsible for different sectors of the city. The regiments are distinguished by both function and district, ensuring that the city is protected in its entirety.

    Regiment of the Shields

    This regiment is primarily responsible for the heart of Orsena, including the sovereign’s palace, government buildings, and merchant districts. They are the most prestigious of the four regiments, entrusted with the protection of the city’s elite.

    Regiment of the Sky Watchers

    Tasked with overseeing the towering, thin spires and higher districts of Orsena, this regiment is known for its expertise in urban navigation and rooftop surveillance. They employ a range of advanced tactics to monitor criminal activity from above, giving them a unique vantage point.

    Regiment of the Blades

    Operating in the shadows, the Blades are responsible for covert operations and intelligence gathering. While not assassins, they are skilled in espionage and stealth, investigating potential threats before they escalate. This regiment deals with organized crime, smuggling, and subversive elements within the city.

    Regiment of the Iron Wardens

    The primary law enforcement regiment within the Orsena Civic Guard, tasked with handling all manner of civil disputes and maintaining public order throughout the city. Whether it be responding to petty crimes, mediating neighborhood conflicts, or dealing with more serious offenses such as theft and assault, the Iron Wardens are the most frequently encountered regiment by the citizens of Orsena. Trained to defuse situations peacefully whenever possible, but well-equipped and capable of using force if necessary. Due to their generalist nature, the Iron Wardens can be found patrolling the streets, investigating incidents, and responding to emergency calls. Their versatility makes them the backbone of the Civic Guard.
