    Trion

    Trion

    Show on the map

    Trion serves as the capital of the Kingdom of Talvar, one of the youngest Atlae-founded realms on Athura. The city was established by King Cedric Talvo, an Ashelan-born noble exiled from Icos for treason. Derided by his contemporaries as 'The Illegitimate King', Cedric’s reign nonetheless cemented Trion as a haven for immigrants from Oprax and Ashela seeking refuge from the ongoing war between their nations. It was named after the tallest keep in the Talvos' fallen city of old (see The Founding of Trion).

    Trion’s architecture and customs bear the imprint of two kingdoms. With Opraxi terraces rising above the coastal cliffs and Ashelan courtyards that open toward mist-laden horizons.

    Elaboration

    Culture & Society

    The city’s population is almost entirely Atlae, and while the Ashelan and Opraxi descendants still hold some contempt for the other, many families have made peace, which can be felt by the merging of their dialects, traditions, and bloodlines. Icosians view the people of Trion as peculiar, if not downright abhorrent. With their ancestry mixed, foreign customs, and questionable loyalties.

    Trion is known for blending Opraxi spices with Ashelan dishes — savory flavors combined with fermented grains and sweet fruit marinades. Song and poetry are big cultural exports, and many are performed throughout Athura. Trion once prospered through trade along Blackrock Bay, exporting silks, oils, and steel. However, this prosperity has waned dramatically in recent years.

    Crisis of the Serpent, Nirva

    For nearly four decades, trade and travel across Blackrock Bay have been crippled by Nirva, a dra’iq of serpentine ilk. She preyed upon merchant vessels and naval patrols, severing Talvar’s connection to trade partners and leaving Trion Port silent ever since.

    The city’s economy is slowly collapsing; more and more merchants flee inland every year, fishing guilds have all but dissolved. This has forced Talvar to rely on its mainland settlements, putting its weight onto many businesses and families that can hardly bare the taxation and culture shock. Nirva has become a living curse upon the kingdom.

    Political Climate

    King Onfroi Talvo has spent years appealing to the High King, Alfor Zuul for aid. Though his pleas are dismissed, Alfor viewing Talvar’s suffering as the consequence of its founder’s defiance. Resentment festers among the populace. The people of Trion whisper that the Zuul Union intends to starve Talvar into submission, forcing the once-proud kingdom back under Icosian control.
