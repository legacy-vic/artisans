    The Lady of Wietmoire, and sister of the High King, Alfor Zuul. Daughter of Aethel Zuul and Catalina Valentyre, she never married, and keeps Wietmoire Manor, where the monarchs gather every year on their way to the Royal Conclave. King Onfroi Talvo, whom she was once offered to in marriage, has become her friend.

    Appearance & Character

    Aeleina has the black hair and blue eyes of her house, as her brother Tybal and her grandfather Anteo did.

    History

    The offer

    Her father offered her in marriage to Onfroi Talvo, and was refused. See The Crowning of Onfroi.

    Wietmoire

    Aethel gave her Wietmoire Manor, putting his kindest child where the Talvos would have to pass every year. It's the last of his mending still standing. With it she holds the township of Wietmoire, and for it she owes fealty to her kinsman Severin Larrensire, the Prince of Moirach, a sore point between their houses. She never married, and keeps the manor, where the monarchs gather every year for the Royal Conclave. Onfroi sleeps under the roof where his family was killed, and the woman he was offered pours his wine. The two have become friends, carefully. One year, she told him that her father had waited for a letter from him, asking for help against Nirva, and would've sent everything he had. She didn't know what she was telling him.
