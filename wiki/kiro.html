    Appearance & Character

    Fiercely loyal, and driven by a desperate need to prove himself, Kiro strives to fulfill every command given to him diligently and without question, believing it his duty to repay the generosity shown to him. His crude yet potent elektrokin and proficient spearmanship make him formidable, though he lacks the experience and ferocity of his peers, and has significant room to grow.

    Raised and nurtured by the High King throughout almost the entirety of his life, he has pledged himself fully towards repaying his perceived debt. Due to this, he has uncontested faith in the High King and refuses to question any orders given to him, no matter how grim they may be.

    History

    Theara

    As an infant, Kiro was entrusted to Theara, one of the last surviving jotuun. His mother was an opraxi refugee in Upror, who couldn't keep a child there. She's never known whose house he grew up in. In Theara's care, he began learning elektrokin, which she'd discovered in her seclusion. Their quiet life came to an abrupt end when Alfor, having discovered her whereabouts, sought her out. She'd used her lightning to protect the boy, and the rumor of it spread. Discreetly accompanied by his Inquisitors, he confronted her on her loyalties, and upon confirming her unwillingness to cooperate, ordered her execution. Before departing, he stumbled upon the boy hidden within the temple. One among his Inquisitors prepared to kill the child, but Alfor spared him, intending to mold him into a powerful and compliant instrument of his will.

    The Hand

    Unaware of the truth, Kiro was raised within Zuul's Hand, where he underwent unrelenting training in combat, and was conditioned to obey without hesitation. Eager to prove himself worthy of the High King's trust and time, he's become a skilled assassin, and is the youngest and most zealous of the Inquisitors.

    Training

    Educated personally by the High King and mentored by Kiyana in wielding a spear, he has a comprehensive education and proficiency with his spear. On top of this, he has some grasp of elektrokin, courtesy of his deceased Jotu'un master. He can't use it reliably, and barely produces a spark on his own.

    The device

    To channel his elektrokin into a directed bolt, he uses a device that Alfor gifted him after taking him in and watching him train with the other Inquisitors. It's a stun baton, out of place because it's not of this world. Nobody knows its purpose or name, but clicking a button on it produces a shock. It was found in a crashed spaceship in the far north (see The Vessel). None of the people of Artisans can explain it, and it's pretty much a secret between Alfor and his immediate confidants. His goal is to shape Kiro into a weapon, not too dissimilar from what Augmen was to Anteo.
