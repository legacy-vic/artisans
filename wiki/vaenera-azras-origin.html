    Vaenera & Azra's Origin

    Once an infamous man, to both civilians and pirates alike, Kade Asher was the captain of a band of vagrants turned pirates. He fell out of a life of piracy when he and his crew made landfall on Tabla Pise, enabling him to meet a young Aiore; a gentle yet very headstrong woman. Here, within the Anklan Isles, she was royalty of sorts, held with high esteem among the Abunali Islanders. She was desired by many men, including the likes of Alexium Ariadne, who wanted her hand in marriage. Kade soon put the foolhardy lifestyle behind him in his quest for love. But it would be too late, as by the time he had fully left his crew, Aiore had already accepted Alexium's proposal and had a child with him. Kade was distraught, seeing as he had lost his crew and his woman by the likes of a much more successful man, and he chose to remain on Tabla Pise as Aiore and Alexium went off to pursue a life together.

    A couple years later, Aiore would return back to her hometown of Morowai, where she would spend time with her family and old friends. To her surprise, she saw that Kade had been working hard to take care of her elderly mother and support the sprouting villages within the community. This seemed to be an entirely different man to the one she once knew, much to her delight. Where Alexium wouldn't bother with getting to know her family, Kade, even through his hardships, went out of his way to stick with them through the thick of it. As much as she loved Alexium, she found herself falling in love with Kade all over again.

    After having an affair, she became pregnant with a child that Alexium would go on to raise, assuming it was his. For five years, Azra would grow up with her sister Vaenera in Morowai before moving to Phylomina, living a sound life until her guilt-ridden mother eventually came clean to Alexium about the true nature of their situation. Disgusted and full of contempt, Alexium gave Aiore an ultimatum. To either give him the whereabouts of Kade Asher, or to leave Phylomina with Azra entirely. Much to his surprise, Aiore chose to leave. But not expecting her to actually consider this outcome, he refused to let her walk out of their lives, so instead, he cast Azra out of the capital, back to the island from which she was born. After being informed by Aiore that Alexium would be watching their relations closely, Kade knew he couldn't stay in Morowai with Azra and her grandmother. So he made the difficult choice to leave them alone, not being heard from since.

    Not long after being shipped away from her family in Phylomina, Azra found it rather easy to make new friends in Morowai. And given that she lived with her grandmother, she had a loving family there too. Now being ten years of age, Azra had been allowed to visit her family in Phylomina for the past two years, growing closer and closer to her sister Vaenera. However despite enjoying each other's company, here in Phylomina, Azra had always felt like a guest rather than a part of the family.

    The Burning of Morowai

    That very year after Azra had returned home from visitation, Morowai was raided by a band of pirates in an unforeseeable tragedy. Azra's grandmother spied the flags that could be seen from shore several miles out, a familiar sigil, these pirates were associated with Kade Asher. What nobody expected however were the flaming arrows that rained down upon the settlement. As people were slaughtered and buildings burned, Azra was led by her grandmother to hide beneath the floor of their hut, though only she could fit. Azra begged and pleaded for her grandmother to try and squeeze her way into safety too, but Azra was closed in, and told to remain silent. Hours of almost nothing but screams and chaos go by until Azra, in a panic, leaps out of the hideaway. Unable to keep herself composed, she screams for her grandmother, running out into the open while doing so. Her eyes dart erratically as she watches the settlement around her crumble into ashes, the jungle around it catching ablaze and charred corpses lie in the roads. It seems as though an entire culture is being stomped out, but for what reason? The pirates who made landfall are ransacking huts as they go, looking for someone, and killing who they didn't need. A couple of pirates grab Azra and alert the others that they found her. Could they really mean...? One of them pulls out a knife, about to plunge it into her chest before an arrow pierces his head. The other pirate pulls out a sword and is about to charge after the attacker, but he's soon shot dead too. Azra, who now is trembling uncontrollably due to fear stands helplessly as Tibs, holding a crossbow, rushes over to pull her behind a hut. He assures her that he will get her someplace safe, and leads her to a small dinghy before proceeding to set course toward mainland Athura.

    Kade, having seen the sails of his old crew, had returned to Morowai. He made his stand against Joss Harrow, his old first mate and the captain who'd replaced him, and lost. He was left for the fire, and the ships of the house of Polys, who came late, found him just barely alive. He believed that everyone there was dead, Azra included.

    In the proceeding months after the raid, Vaenera and the rest of her family assume Azra to be dead, alongside the rest of Morowai's residents. This fueled Vaenera to resent any and all pirates and scoundrels alike, which persists to this day.

    Present Day

    Presently, Azra still struggles with the events that unfolded on that day, keeping herself busy and refusing to take a moments respite as those memories will only resurface with complacency.

    From time to time, Vaenera hears rumors that fuel her suspicions that Azra may still be alive after the burning of Morowai. Tell of a young woman with certain distinguishing characteristics being sighted in parts of the continent where such traits are most uncommon. Though, Vaenera can get very emotional whenever an informant brings up the subject of her lost sister, almost to the point of blind rage. She always insists they check if there's a mole on this mystery woman's chin, but as of now, there are always conflicting stories.

    Azra harbors some resentment toward Vaenera as she truly believed that Vaenera had forgotten about her in favor of living her privileged life of a princess turned queen. And although Azra is aware that everyone carries their own plights and tries to empathize, she struggles with doing so. As a result, she opts to act as if she never even had a sister. Although, after she met Artemis and vaguely told him about Vaenera, he made it a point to remind her during their time in Tibs' Gang.

    When young, Azra felt unworthy to be around the Phylominan royal family after Alexium learned of the truth. Even Azra was unaware that she was not his child. This feeling of worthlessness has only amplified over the years as she's been living as a nobody for ten years. She feels as if she's too far gone from what her family knew of her all those years ago to show her face again, and fears that they have changed as well.
