    Ten-Year War

    Also known as The Great Jotuun Purge, The Ten-Year War

    Also called the Great Jotuun Purge: the war of extermination waged on the jotuun by Anteo Zuul and the ex-jotuun Augmen, unparalleled in history.

    The Pact

    Unable to move openly against the powerful jotuun, Anteo met with Augmen in secret, and together they devised the war. The campaign began a year later. Anteo kept the Temple of Isi, where Augmen's daughter, Ayala, had gone to train, for last, knowing that once Augmen had her, he'd have no use for the war. Augmen was much more determined to find her than Anteo would've liked, so he bargained with him. Isi would be spared, and Ayala with it, for as long as Augmen did as he was told. Augmen held to it until he couldn't.

    Campaign of Fire

    Augmen aided the Zuul Union in dispatching multiple jotuun temples, and destroying any who supported them.

    The Siege of Ancrage

    Revered for its loyalty to the jotuun, Ancrage hid monks in a temple beneath the city. In retaliation, Anteo and Augmen launched a brutal siege. Augmen shattered the battlements by hurling massive stone spires from the surrounding mountains, breaching the walls and devastating the city's infrastructure. The city lay in ruins.

    Ten Years of War

    The purge raged for a decade, devastating Athura. Countless temples fell, and by its end, an estimated 98% of the jotuun populace was destroyed. It ended with the siege of the Temple of Isi.

    The Temple of Isi

    By the end, there was nothing left to burn. The survivors had fled to the one place Anteo wouldn't touch, and Augmen had driven the whole order into his daughter's house. Anteo told him so before giving the word. Augmen went in first, to be the one to reach her, and found Ayala among the refugees. Believing he could 'rescue' her, he slaughtered the defenders without mercy, and killed Prior Ad Regalis, once his master. Ayala pleaded with her father to stop. He tried to talk to her, and told her of the bargain, that she'd been safe for ten years because of him. She understood then who had paid for it. Realizing he was lost, she waited for her chance. When other jotuun distracted him, Augmen faltered, and Ayala launched a stone spire into his back, killing him instantly.

    Anteo, though deprived of his enforcer, reveled in a near-total victory as the order collapsed. Ayala survived the final culling and disappeared into obscurity with seven others (see Qis), one of whom she later took as her secret apprentice: Mirak, a ringo youth who'd been in the order since before the war, at the Temple of Khada.

    Legacy of Ancrage

    Though ruined in the purge, Ancrage was later rebuilt as a symbol of resilience. The city flourished, incorporating the very spires once used to destroy it into its design. The largest now stands as a monument to the families lost in the war.
