    Temple of Khada

    Temple of Khada

    Show on the map

    Temple of Khada | 3 BBU | - Built about a dozen years ago, this temple stands to be one of the largest in all of Athura, although from the surface you wouldn't get this impression. All around the temple you'll find wild yris behave in a domesticated manner as they've grown comfortable around the jotuun who worship and feed them. There are five subterranean levels beneath the four that stand above ground, the bottom-most three are dedicated tombs for any fallen jotuun. Coming in through the western gateway, you'll see a large garden with a sun dial in the middle and shallow pools of water on both north and southern sides. This area is where you'll most likely see neophytes training. Coming closer to the temple there's a large flight of steps leading into the main hall, where you'll see elaborate murals painted by the local ringo populous, depicting the gods giving the jotuun their power to help protect those who can't protect themselves. (...)

    Temple of Khada | 173 ABU | - Swallowed by the sands of time, the temple is only accessible through a collapsed ceiling that leads to the top-most floor. Feeling very much like a catacombs, these floors house no dead, or at least they weren't intended to. Littered around the halls and rooms you'll be able to see what became of the jotuun who thought it'd be best to stay and protect their home. The yris population surprisingly decided to stay despite the area having no real prey for them to catch, may their reasoning be spiritual? The west gate is largely flooded with sand covering the pools, sun dial and lush gardens, the steps are only halfway covered with the entrance leading into the main hall being sealed shut by the tall granite door. It seems whatever had killed these jotuun had a strong ire, and they locked themselves in with it. Was that intentional? (...)

    The temple fell during the Ten-Year War. Mirak, who belonged to it, was away at the time, and came back to the sealed door.
