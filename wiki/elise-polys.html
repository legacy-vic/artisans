    The wife of Leander Polys, lord of the house of Polys in Lorence, and the younger sister of the pirate Kade Asher, who once rescued her from the men who killed their parents.

    History

    Youth

    She was born to an aristocratic family of Arlene. On the way to Lorence, pirates killed her parents and took her. Kade and his allies, Joss Harrow among them, later killed those responsible and rescued her, and she married Leander in Lorence.

    The capture

    When Leander captured Kade, she begged for the choice he was given, which was the only mercy he'd get. His crew had never touched a ship of the Polys, for her sake. See Capture.
