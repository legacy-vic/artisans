    Languages

    The languages of Artisans as of 211 ABU. Hyenesh is the most dominant, while the older tongues live on among the peoples who kept them.

    Pumesh

    The traditional language of the Atlae. Originates far beyond recorded history. As of 211 ABU, it remains in use by the Abunali and a small fraction of Athura.

    Dholesh

    An old tongue commonly used by the atlae of Icos. Dates back to when Icos was first being colonized by atlae. It remains in use by the many Icosian villages.

    Hyenesh

    A relatively modern language. Merges elements of both Pumesh and Dholesh while introducing newer elements to simplify speech and spelling. It's the current most dominant language in all of Artisans.

    Foomesh

    The ancestral language spoken by the Opraxi. Despite being considered a dated language by most scholars, it remains the dominant language spoken as of present within the Oprax region, as any other language is not tolerated within its borders.

    Ringlesh

    The traditional language of the Ringo. It's closely linked to both Upper and Lower Et'lesh, and ringo who speak it are the only ones who can understand grolls speaking Upper Et'lesh.

    Upper Et'lesh

    An evolved version of the traditional language of the grolls that infuses elements of Pumesh. Though, still largely its own separate language. Since the eradication of the jotuun, nobody can truly communicate with most grolls, and those who speak Upper Et'lesh don't want to go anywhere near one due to their nasty reputation.

    Lower Et'lesh

    The traditional language of the grolls. Originates far beyond recorded history. It's the more dominant and basal form of Et'lesh, still spoken by older groll populations. No one else alive can understand a single word of it.
