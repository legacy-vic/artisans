    A shaman of the Abunali in Dunis, about sixty-seven years old, who was given temporary eldership over the village when Elder A'Te died at the start of 211 ABU. Once a kashif, she seeks to give people guidance through the gods. She's the twin sister of Abasse The Seer, whom she doesn't acknowledge unless she has to.

    Appearance & Character

    Ankti has long, shaggy, bronze hair, and her kashif markings have faded beside her shaman tattoos. She's dignified and honest, if petty, and ignores personal connections for fear of losing her spiritual one. She seeks to give people guidance through the gods.

    History

    Early life

    She grew cold and distant toward her mother, choosing to pursue her ambitions of becoming a kashif. After her mother's passing, she felt an overwhelming guilt, and attempted to reconnect with Abasse. It didn't last, and the two don't acknowledge each other unless they have to. See Abasse The Seer.

    Eldership

    She was the closest friend of Elder A'Te, Dunis's matriarch. When A'Te died at the start of 211 ABU, Ankti was given temporary eldership until a successor could be anointed.

    Story

    Spoiler: Episode 2

    Ankti held the memorial feast of Elder A'Te, for which she'd sent Artemis and Azlan to fetch khovis eggs. When Azra took Artemis captive, she told her that the way of the Abunali comes before any authority in the Tan'we, and had the two settle it by Nalusi Ba'et. She let Artemis go with his demand honored, and kept Azra in her tent long enough for him to slip away.
