    Karlaine

    Karlaine

    Show on the map

    Originally a humbly sized town, Karlaine rose to prominence following its acquisition of the Jaraq remains, killed just outside its borders. Within these creatures existed an oil secreted from their piro glands that could burn flesh within a mere instant, and so too melt even the hardest of metals. Demand for its oil soared and Karlaine was the sole proprietor. The booming success was in large part due to the Merchant Guild, who aided in transport of this resource, particularly so when Karlaine had no vessels of its own. Karlaine's sovereignty grew fat and wealthy and declared that his modest residence in the center of the now city was no longer suitable for a king of his stature, and decreed that a grand castle be built in his name. Thus the Eryas Citadel was born.

    Karlaine was one of the holdings granted away from Linnenmoire before its fall, and its rulers expected that fall before it came. Its dynasty and nobles weren't of Azkassari descent, but a mixture of Ibyan and Moirish. After The Cremation of Linnenmoire, it grew into the largest city on Icos, as people migrated there and traffic increased. It's the heart of the Principality of Karlem (see Titles and Ranks).
