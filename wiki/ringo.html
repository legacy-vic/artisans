    Ringo

    The second most dominant sentient species on the planet Artisans. Originating from Athura, the ringo, without their tools, clothing, and other defining characteristics, would appear no different from any other wild animal on Artisans. They have an average height of 3'11'' and resemble large, bipedal rodents, specifically mice and rats. Their average lifespan is 192 years.

    Ringo are brilliant creatures, often outsmarting atlae, but due to their physical disadvantages, they haven't had an easy time reigning dominant over the atlae in recent history. There are several distinct subspecies of ringo, each with their own varying characteristics.

    Ethnic Groups

    Pisewi

    Pisewi 'Islander' Ringo are the smallest and rarest ringo ethnic group, native to Namona Pise and beyond The Southern Isles. These ringo are so isolated from the rest of the world that they still practice much of old ringo culture. Some traditions several thousands of years old, and with that much time passing by for this island-dwelling subspecies, some physical changes were sure to appear. Aside from the obvious smaller stature, the fur coat of the Pisewi Ringo is dense and shiny. Most commonly possessing muted browns and beiges with minimal patterning, their fur can also come in shades of black and blonde. The color of their eyes is scantly seen, as their iris is quite small due to the large size of their pupils, giving them a doe-eyed appearance. They're able to swim much better than their mainland cousins, and thanks to their thick, hydrophobic fur, are also able to avoid being drenched and survive more extreme weather conditions.

    Sudr'iq

    Ringo native to lands that are hot, dry, or humid. These ringo are found all over Athura and tend to have short, thin fur, with beautiful patterning and vibrant colors. Sudr'iq ringo are believed to be the most intelligent, with many notable ringo artisans, inventors, and scholars being sudr'iq. But this is likely due to social bias, with atlae giving them more opportunities than other ethnicities. Their ears, unlike the average ringo, are pointed and usually hold tufts on the tips. Their tails are hairless, usually with another tuft on the tip. They're born blind and entirely hairless. Their eyes are brightly colored like gemstones, which inspired the tradition for ringo to carry a gemstone on their persons the same color as their eyes, as it's believed to bring good fortune.

    Sun'om

    Ringo native to lands that are cold. Primarily only found on the continent of Icos; these ringo carry the worst reputation out of the few, so you're not likely to see many roaming anywhere near known civilization. These ringo have dense matted fur and are the second smallest ethnic group. Their eyes are brightly colored, but the range is mostly but not entirely limited to red, blue, turquoise, and beige. These ringo have the least impressive patterns, and you'll very seldom find a darker color on their coat. They can be seen having blonde, white, grey, and blue fur most commonly. Their negative reputation largely stems from the days of old, when atlae first started to expand their reach onto Icos. Explorers would wander into the wilderness, charting unknown regions, and stumble into sun'om ringo territory. Unknowing and afraid, the ringo acted with aggression, and the atlae did the same. Souring relations for generations to come.

    Sutu'un

    Ringo native to lands in the middle. Lands that are neither hot, or cold. You'll find these ringo in pretty much every city on both continents, despite carrying a negative reputation. Unlike the sun'om, whose reputation is built off of their aggression. The sutu'uns reputation is built upon trust. Or lack thereof. The list of notable sutu'un ringo is comprised primarily of outlaws and pirates most notorious for cheating and murdering their way to infamy. Able to possess both long and short fur with decent patterning and varied colors, these ringo have an easy time masking their identity as a sutu'un, where most typically try to pass as the much more trusted sudr'iq. As a result, many people today on artisans are skeptical of all ringo. Some places even go so far as to ban ringo entirely just because of the stigma. The tails of sutu'un can be either skinned or bushy, but their eyes, much unlike both sudr'iq and sun'om, are usually dull in coloration.
