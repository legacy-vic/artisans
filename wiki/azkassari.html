    Azkassari

    The Azkassari are the atlae of fallen Azkassar. Most noble families from Linnenmoire and Endlemoire descend from Azkassari ancestors who fled Azkassar before the calamity. See The Fall of Azkassar.

    Their blood survives to this day in those noble families because, after fleeing, they considered themselves of purer blood than the other atlae around them. They engaged in avunculate incest, and married between other Azkassari families, for many generations.

    The surviving families like to spread the notion that their blood is more pure because they claim heritage to Lucan and Millera. The Azkassari aren't descended from either.

    In Icos

    When Azkassar fell, some of its noble families fled to Ibya, where they lived as refugees among people they considered beneath them. Many found the Ibyans' simple governance easy to understand, and grew wealthy as advisors, commanders, and even landholders. Quietly, they gained influence and gathered soldiers. Knowing they stood no chance of challenging Ibya so soon, they went west to build a feudal realm by subjugating weaker peoples. The houses that had allied with one another founded Linnenmoire, the capital of Icos and the first city-state, and with it the beginning of feudalism on Icos. See The Azkassari in Ibya. After The Cremation of Linnenmoire, Endlemoire was built upon its ruins, and the Larrensires came to rule there, as they still do. The Barrentyne are another Azkassari noble house. The Valentyres, who are merchants, never established an estate in Linnenmoire with the rest, and their blood has been diluted.

    Appearance

    Those of Azkassari blood are easily identifiable from their pale skin, tall and elegant figures, dark hair, and red eyes. Their nose bridge is straight, and their profile is flat and linear from the forehead to the tip. Their long faces help them stand even further apart from other atlae ethnic groups.
