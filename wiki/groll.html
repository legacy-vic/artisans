    Groll

    Large, bipedal predators that resemble foxes or hyenas, Grolls are formidable creatures found throughout the world of Artisans. Standing at the imposing height of 8 feet on average, some individuals can reach up to 10 feet. Their average lifespan is 92 years. Grolls possess immense strength and agility, making them fearsome fighters, and when in groups, they're lethal threats to any and everything they encounter.

    Grolls lead a nomadic lifestyle, making them highly unpredictable and feared across all regions. Originating in the Northwestern hemisphere, they typically have a spotted coat of matted fur that provides insulation against the cold. Their communication consists of grunts, barks, howls, and yips that unsettlingly resemble laughter. Despite being more intelligent than most beasts, being capable of using tools, they're often underestimated by both atlae and ringo, who consider them unintelligent due to their higher wit. However, Grolls are extremely tough to outmaneuver and should never be taken lightly.

    Snow Groll

    Native to arctic regions, Snow Grolls have significantly more body mass compared to other Grolls and possess thicker fur coverage to withstand extreme cold. They lead a more isolated lifestyle, often found in the icy expanses of Icos. Their coloration ranges from piercing white to black, but their patterning is usually very muted, almost not even being visible.

    Savanna Groll

    Savanna Grolls inhabit the warmer grasslands and savannas as their name suggests. But it isn't uncommon to find them roaming cooler regions as they're acclimated to many climates. They have shorter fur and are leaner compared to their northern counterparts, adapted for speed and endurance in open terrains. Their coloration can be all manner of hues, but white and black specimens are rare for Savanna Grolls. Their patterning is very prominent, boasting black spots all over their body, and some specimens have an uncommon mutation that turns those spots into thick stripes.

    Jungle Groll

    Jungle Grolls are adapted to dense, tropical forests. They have a slightly smaller stature but are incredibly agile and adept at navigating through thick vegetation. As their name suggests, they're sparsely found in the more tropical regions of Athura, where they were able to remain unnoticed during the wastings. A jungle in central Athura was burned down in 173 BBU to cull a massive groll population, and those that slipped through the wastings simply slipped beneath Alusha's proverbial radar. Although, they actively avoid the Tan'we Jungle due to them being unable to compete with the natural predators there. Specifically Tirvaan, who seem to actively hunt them down whenever their scent is picked up. The fur of Jungle Grolls is often darker to blend in with their environment, with similar spotting as Savanna Groll, however instead of solid spots, they're rosettes. Their fur coloration ranges from brown to black, but the rare orange or yellow mutation is still possible.

    Cave Groll

    Cave Grolls dwell primarily in mountainous regions. Having a stockier, robust build to navigate rocky terrains and tight spaces. Their fur is coarse and commonly a variation of grey to match the stone surroundings.
