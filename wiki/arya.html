    Arya

    Arya

    Gender
    Female
    Allegiance
    Southern Abunali
    Sibling
    Aurel
    Traits
    Fair-minded, curious, outspoken.

    A girl of the southern Abunali tribes, and the younger sister of Aurel. When she's introduced, the two have come to Khamu to take part in the Kashif Trials.

    Appearance & Character

    Arya would sooner see a thing for herself than take another's word for it. She knows her gods, which is more than can be said for her brother.

    History

    The Kashif Trials

    When the groups of the Kashif Trials were reformed, it was Arya who asked Artemis and Afina to join hers, over Ano's objections. She had heard the rumors about Artemis and asked Afina whether they were true. Afterward, she defended him to Ano and Aurel, and stepped between them when they fought.

    Quotes

    "Our marshal is in danger, and all you three can do is fight one another?"
    — to Artemis, Ano and Aurel

    Story

    Spoiler: Episode 1A

    She begged Aurel not to go back for Marshal Akkennoten, and collapsed screaming when he did. Artemis dragged her away from the gully, into the jungle.
