    Bicontinental Union

    A binding pact between the realms of both continents, which Oprax refused to join, ensuring military assistance during dra’iq events and forbidding the predatory seizure of weakened territory. The Kalmeres stood among its founding houses, working alongside Athuran rulers. This alliance rewrote political norms across two continents and became significant enough to define time itself: history was now measured in BBU and ABU.

    High Sovereignty

    When the Bicontinental Union was formed, its founders understood a harsh truth. You cannot coordinate two continents by committee alone. So they created The Royal Conclave, wherein an elected, temporary arbiter whose authority superseded kings only in matters of dra’iq response, international law within the Union, and sanctions against rule-breaking nations.

    The High Sovereign was never meant to rule. They were meant to organize, judge, and stabilize. The founders were explicit in ensuring that the role wasn't hereditary, tied to any specific kingdom, and passed only by election when a term naturally ended.

    The title was created with Tarris Kalmere, the first High King. Anteo Zuul was voted in after him and granted emergency powers by the rulers who joined the Union, which he heavily abused, securing succession rights for his children. His eldest, Archon, died in one of the major conflicts (see The Death of Archon), and Anteo never abdicated, so upon his death the High Throne passed to his youngest, Aethel, and then to Alfor. The High Sovereign was never made to relinquish those powers. Even with them, Anteo tried and failed to force Oprax into the Union. See Anteo's Failure.

    Courtiers of the High Crown

    The High Sovereign has courtiers, though not as many as a traditional ruler would. Alfor has about four, who can travel at his behest to any ruler's court, where they are held as esteemed guests. They are representatives of the High Crown.

    Name

    Initially proposed as the Bicontinental Union, the name the year notations (BBU and ABU) still use. Once Anteo ascended to High Sovereign, he influenced those beneath him to refer to it as the Zuul Union, especially after his children were born and set to inherit it. As of 211 ABU, people refer to it as the Union or the Zuul Union in everyday speech, as a Zuul still sits on the High Throne.
