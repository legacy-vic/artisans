    The nineteen-year-old son of a zarbuck rancher from the outskirts of Kama, who travels with his closest friend, Artemis. Earnest and easily worried, he sees himself as a cowardly follower. He means to get out of the slums by hard work and virtue, and one day settle down with someone.

    Appearance & Character

    Azlan is earnest and friendly to a fault. He rambles when nervous, and apologizes even for apologizing.

    History

    Early Life

    He grew up on a homestead just outside of Kama, where his family kept zarbuck in stables and wide pastures. Every harvest season, the whole family would gather for a feast, with stories and music, and his grandmother's zarbuck stew. No matter how busy they were, it was the one time of year when everyone made an effort to be together.

    Meeting Artemis

    He met Artemis by running into him with a basket of fruit, near Kama's abandoned orphanage. He called him "Arty" at once, apologized for it, hugged him without being asked to, and invited him to the feast.

    Quotes

    "A family is like a tree. The branches may grow in different directions, but the roots keep them all together."

    — repeating his grandfather's words to Artemis

    Story

    Spoiler: Episode 1A

    On the night after they met, Artemis woke him at his window to warn him that bad people were coming. The two leapt from the roof as young bandits stole the zarbuck and set the house alight. Azlan's father carried his pregnant mother out of the fire and held the bandits off, but turned when he heard Azlan cry out, and was cut down from behind. Azlan and Artemis fled into the woods with the bandits in pursuit.

    Spoiler: Episode 2

    About five years on, he and Artemis lived off whatever work they could find. He lost one khovis egg to the flock it came from, and threw the other at the mercenaries who came for Artemis. In Dunis, Abasse The Seer told him to keep Artemis on his path, and that his sacrifices would not go unnoticed. In Novon, he spoke of what he wanted: to be a knight or a chef, with a farmstead to breed zarbuck on, one wife, and children. Princess Koto Talvo took him along to Trion because Artemis wouldn't leave without him. At the palace, King Onfroi Talvo remembered his father's ranch, and told him he was sorry.

    Spoiler: Episode 4

    He crossed Bali with Artemis and the king's shipment. When grolls raided Pa'iq, it was his plan to startle a herd of tulkai through their camp, and he led it with a mace and a shield. He reached the bound Tanzu alongside a knight who turned out to be Koto, and drove the wagon to Sulala. He was let in wearing her armor. When thieves took the wagon, he drew a sword on them and couldn't bring himself to use it, and was pinned until a stranger frightened them off.
