    The late wife of the second High King, Anteo Zuul, and mother of Archon and Aethel. She was of the Larrensires, who rule in Endlemoire, the city the Zuuls had lived in as nobles. Long dead by 211 ABU, she lies entombed beneath the Varrow's Tree in the garden she kept at High Sonham, where her grandson, Alfor Zuul, still goes to quiet his mind.

    Appearance & Character

    Endelyn was tall and pale, with long, wavy, black hair streaked with bronze, and red eyes. She was compassionate and emotional, and wanted nothing more than to live happily with her family. Stress threw her into a deep depression.

    History

    Her sons' marriages

    She chose the wives of both her sons, Dendrilla Ariadne for Archon and Catalina Valentyre for Aethel. Anteo never liked either choice.

    The third child

    Endelyn gave birth to a third child at High Sonham, while a dra'iq screamed in the distance. Anteo held her hand throughout, and brightly smiled: "We've finally done it." "This had better be a girl," she answered. "Then we could finally be at peace."

    The child was a boy with white hair (see White Hair). Anteo's smile faded the moment he unwrapped the cloth. He handed the infant back, gave the midwives a distinct look, and stormed out, and they nodded in compliance. When Endelyn was shown him, she covered her face and waved them away. They lowered the infant into the washtub and left.

    Aethel, who was ten, approached the washtub and watched the infant struggle to breathe. Endelyn told him not to look, and he ran from the room, crying.

    The loss of Archon

    When Archon was killed and Anteo prepared for war, she feared her husband would meet a similar fate. She traveled to a jotuun monastery to plead for assistance, only to find they were already planning to intervene, and Prior Ad Regalis told her she could accompany him and his apprentice, Augmen. See The Death of Archon.

    The garden

    Anteo granted her a room in High Sonham for a garden, and she planted a Varrow's Tree at its center. She found the dead buried beneath it and told no one, and made it her tradition to kneel before the tree and pray quietly, which she kept to her last day (see High Sonham). She was kneeling there when she heard Kaelyn scream (see The Massacre at Wietmoire).

    Archon's death left her in a deep depression that nothing seemed to rectify. She spent her few remaining years frequently visiting her garden, where she consumed the tree's leaf. She wanted only to be happy, but no amount of poisonous petals would suffice. Before long, she fell into an eternal slumber, and her servants found her moments later.

    Her family frequently stood by her bedside in her last remaining days, wishing they'd been there to comfort her. Anteo pressured the caretakers to try and save her, but there was very little they could do, and before long, her soul was Oies' to take. Anteo hated the garden for what it caused, but loved it as Endelyn once had. He had her mummified and entombed underneath the Varrow's Tree, where she remained untouched, and he, too, started to visit the garden frequently.

    Legacy

    The Larrensires gave the High Throne a queen in her. The Prince of Moirach, Severin Larrensire, is her nephew.
