    Alora 'Tanzu' Rhea

    Also known as Tanzu, Tanzu the Great

    Alora 'Tanzu' Rhea

    Race
    Atlae
    Gender
    Female
    Ethnicity
    Balian, Ashelan
    Age
    12
    Hair
    Brown, very messy bob cut.
    Eyes
    Hazel.
    Blemishes
    Freckles
    Traits
    Sarcastic, trustworthy, courageous, eccentric.
    Goals
    Live free; Leave home.
    Conflict
    Terribly impressionable; extremely bullish.

    A twelve-year-old who works the stables of Pa'iq, which the child's father, Amesh, owns, goes by Tanzu the Great, and wants to leave home and live free. The name is taken from the Kashif Marshal T'Anzu, a hero to the child since he deterred an occi away from the town. Akir is never far off. He's sworn to Tanzu's father and family, and protects Tanzu above all. Amesh is Athuran, and Athurans don't have surnames. Rhea was the mother's name, a minor house of Icos with a name and little money, which married her to Amesh for his coin. He made it in the stock trade, and not only in animals: he sold people actively in the past (see Slavery). By 211 ABU he does it only on the side, seeing more profit in tjarka, and Akir is his only slave. He owes a sizeable debt to the Crimson Shroud, the culmination of what they invested in him while he was actively trading slaves. When his profits declined, he sold them off, bought tjarka, and raised the stable. The Shroud has paid him a monthly visit ever since to collect their returns, and each visit, the returns have turned into debts with excuses attached. He frequently threatens Tanzu with it: "I should sell you and be done with this... You're of no use to me, anyway." Tanzu has heard it often enough to know that people can be sold, but not that Amesh once sold them. She died giving birth to Tanzu, before Akir came.

    Story

    Spoiler: Episode 4

    Tanzu caught Artemis and Azlan breaking into a stall at the stables of Pa'iq, held them up with a bow that couldn't shoot, and offered to sell them a tjarka. When the owner came, Tanzu hid behind Akir. The grolls who raided the town took Tanzu along with the cargo, and bound the child to a post at a ruined monastery. Azlan and Koto reached the post, and Tanzu left with them for Sulala, saying that those back home were dead. There, Tanzu chased down the thieves who stole the wagon, and fought them tooth and nail.

    Spoiler: Later

    She later comes to admire Artemis more than Marshal T'Anzu after she hears that he slayed an occi while on a hunt with Azlan and Azra.

    When she first meets Artemis, she's slow to recognize the markings on his face as kashif markings, to which she then asks if he knows Kashif Marshal T'Anzu.
