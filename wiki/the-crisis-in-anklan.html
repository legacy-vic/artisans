    The Crisis in Anklan

    Also known as Crisis in Anklan, The Anklan Crisis

    The invasion of the Anklan Isles by General Tsavin, from about 134 to 137 ABU, and the jotuun's intervention against it. Anteo Zuul was the sitting High King, but Tsavin acted on his own, not on Anteo's orders. He meant to carve out a realm of his own, and his service to the Union was only cover. Anteo did nothing about it, glad to see a realm weakened, and watched the jotuun take years to end it. The struggle was resolved, but Anklan was left scarred.

    The invasion (134 ABU)

    General Tsavin launched his invasion on Orsena in late 134 ABU. Mesui was the first settlement to fall. Tsavin renamed it Oricos. After his death, the isles' Icosi overlords renamed it again, and it's Lorence today. It couldn't prepare a naval blockade in time: the invasion was already underway, and his troops made landfall before support could arrive.

    Demeia was erected atop a vanquished Abunali settlement. That settlement had been akin to Khamu, the heart of the Northern Abunali network, and a hub between the M'Atause and M'Awai peoples.

    The fall of Orsena (135 ABU)

    Orsena was the last major stronghold, and it fell after much resistance.

    The jotuun responded to the crisis during the tail end of Tsavin's campaign, after much deliberation over whether they should intervene. They liberated Demeia from his rule, but refused to storm the other cities, as there would be many collateral casualties. The Temple of Yolotl was erected in Demeia as a shelter for refugees and jotuun.

    Negotiations (136 ABU)

    A year of unsuccessful negotiation attempts went by. The natives grew impatient with the jotuun, who turned away assistance from nearby rulers.

    Prior Ad Regalis and his apprentice, Augmen, ventured to Anklan to aid. Augmen's impatience led to the liberation of Oricos, though several priors and the grand uthisha chastised him for it, and he was sent away for some time as punishment. Negotiations resumed.

    The liberation (137 ABU)

    Ad Regalis and Augmen returned, and Orsena was liberated. Tsavin made one last move, sending his army to attack the jotuun and the natives, which led to the destruction of his fighting force. He remained in Tsavin's Fortress with his garrison and his Abunali captives, threatening their lives if the jotuun marched. Augmen disregarded his threats, infiltrated the fortress, and killed General Tsavin before the captives could be harmed.

    Anteo heard only that a rowdy jotuun had ended it, and that the order reprimanded him. He didn't learn Augmen's name, and thought it typical of the jotuun to make an example of someone who gets things done efficiently.
