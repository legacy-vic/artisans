    Kingdom of Stade

    Also known as Commonwealth of Stade, Stadian Commonwealth

    Kingdom of Stade

    Show on the map

    The realm above Oprax, encompassing what used to be Ashela, and the region of Upror in the west, where the Kingdom of Tuvar once stood. Its current ruler is King Lanis Kalmere, of the Kalmere Royal Family. See Houses of Stade.

    The people of Western Icos, within the region that used to be Upror, are comprised of primitive hilltribes and wildmen compared to the more developed east. They technically aren't part of the kingdom, but their lands are technically owned by the king of Stade by stadian law.

    History

    After The Cremation of Linnenmoire, the surviving rulers of Icos made petty kingdoms and princedoms of their realms, each claiming the primacy of Linnenmoire (see The Petty Realms). Eventually, faced with the threat of Morgar to the north, the Three Great Princes banded together and formed the Commonwealth of Stade, named for the high hill where they negotiated its creation.

    Vowing to prevent the past from repeating itself, they enshrined in law that supreme authority would never be vested in a single bloodline or seat of power. So the station of Grand Prince was born, elected by the newly made Lord-Electors, a title held and inherited by the rulers of the founding states. Over time, Stade grew to encompass much of the territory once ruled by the Kingdom of Linnenmoire, by diplomatically integrating its neighbouring realms.

    Titles and Ranks

    As of 211 ABU:

    • King.
    • Owing direct allegiance to the king, many of them with sworn vassals of their own:
      • Archduke of Tuvar, held by the Zuuls.
      • Prince, one for each principality:
        • Moirach, held by the Larrensires, the region around Endlemoire, encompassing Endletonn, Lortonn, Norvania, Bogwatch, Moirlander's Respite and Wietmoire.
        • Karlem, held by the Kalmeres, the region around Karlaine, encompassing every settlement not within the others or the king's counties.
        • Ibya, held by the Damaris of Pavilia; Prince Theron Damaris as of 211 ABU.
      • Duke. Only one exists, holding Ōnari and Kabejō Garrison. A duke is sworn only to the king, never to the archduke or a prince.
      • Counts of Dremmel, Rell and Haric, each holding a county of the same name, sworn to the king rather than to a prince. The Temple of Nolvere is within Rell's borders.
    • Count, a modest number, about five or six to a principality.
    • Lord-Elector is a separate office, which may be held by any of the above. Had King Lanis Kalmere been born in the electoral era and been elected, he'd have been styled Grand Prince of the Commonwealth, Lord-Elector of the Realm, and Prince of Karlem.
