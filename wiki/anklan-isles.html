    Anklan Isles

    Also known as Kingdom of the Anklan Isles, Anklan

    Anklan Isles

    Show on the map

    Culturally still Anklan'A, as the Abunali of the isles still refer to it, but their Icosi overlords simplified it into Anklan.

    From about 134 to 137 ABU, the isles were invaded by General Tsavin and freed with the jotuun's help, which left Anklan scarred. See The Crisis in Anklan.
