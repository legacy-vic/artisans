    Elder Amunti

    Elder Amunti

    Gender
    Male
    Titles
    Elder
    Allegiance
    Abunali, Kashif
    Traits
    Eccentric, theatrical, cautious.

    One of the three elders of the kashif, and the one who presents the opening challenge of the Kashif Trials. He's eccentric and theatrical, but the most cautious of the three.

    Appearance & Character

    Amunti stands with a slouch, clasping and rubbing his hands together. He has a flair for the dramatic, and his own words for things.

    History

    The Kashif Trials

    He presented the opening challenge of the Kashif Trials, an obstacle course he described as "jumpy balance-y swingy puzzles," and had to be reminded to warn the children that it wouldn't be as easy as they thought.

    Story

    Spoiler: Episode 1A

    He had wanted the trials cut short, and said as much to Elder Azhotep when Artemis returned alone: "Perhaps then, it is time to forego tradition."
