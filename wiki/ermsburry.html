    Ermsburry

    Also known as House Ermsburry, The Ermsburrys

    A lesser noble house of Stade, holding Endletonn under the Larrensires of Endlemoire. King Onfroi Talvo is their kin through his mother, Lady Haileen Ermsburry, who was kept with them after the massacre at Wietmoire Manor. They'd have hanged if she left, and she stayed. Onfroi spends a night with them every year, on the way to the Royal Conclave, in her room. See Houses of Stade.
