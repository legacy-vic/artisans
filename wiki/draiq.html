    Dra'iq

    Dra'iq are draconic organisms too

    Contents
    • Ember
    • Tempest
    • Terra
    • Apteral

    Ember

    • The most common type of dra'iq.
    • Always carnivorous in diet with ferocious temperamental tendencies.
    • The heat of their breath varies from specimen to specimen.
    • Flame is created by two large glands called 'piro glands' in the lower jaw that project oil when contracted. The heat of the breath is enough to ignite the oil and depending how large of a breath they exhale, can reach up to 208 meters in the largest individuals. Although at such a distance, most of the oil is burned up so the flames are less likely to stick onto you.

    Tempest

    • A rare type.
    • Omnivorous in diet, they're usually content to stay in forests rather than hunting for animals outside of their habitat.
    • They don't grow very large, usually capping out at about 40 feet in length.
    • They're exceptional flyers, having a wingspan usually doubling that of dra'iq in their same size range.
    • Their breath isn't kept in their lungs but rather in tube-like sacks spanning the length of their necks. They inhale air, trapping in in these sacks until they need to use it. Mind you they're still able to breath and emit sounds. Whenever they eventually release the air, it's in short barks, or bursts of air that emit gusts of wind of up to 233 mph capable of sending most creatures flying.

    Terra

    • Fairly common.
    • Carnivorous in diet.
    • These dra'iq usually have the toughest skin out of the entire order, being able to deflect ballista bolts and shake off intense pirokin attacks.
    • They don't like to fly as often as the other types since they can defend themselves best on land. Utilizing large solid objects like boulders or trees against attackers.

    Apteral

    • Uncommon.
    • Carnivorous, Herbivorous, Omnivorous, Piscivorous.
    • Thick scaled skin similar in texture to chainmail.
    • Most commonly unable to harness the power of any element. However there have been select cases of apteral dra'iq using each except for fire.
