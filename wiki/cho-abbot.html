    As the personal handmaiden and stylist to Queen Kota and Princess Koto in Trion, she is responsible for their hair, attire, and overall public presentation. Mute since childhood and devoted to service, she's both confidant and caretaker to Koto. Niles Abbot is her half-brother.

    Appearance & Character

    Mute since childhood, Cho communicates through gesture, serving as both her language and her discipline.

    Unlike her half-brother Niles, whose pride and verbosity embody the excesses of nobility, Cho teaches through action—her silence a study in restraint and purpose. Her craft reflects her values: careful, deliberate, and deeply personal. To Koto, she is both confidant and caretaker; to the rest of the palace, she is an invisible constant whose quiet diligence keeps the royal image intact.

    Cho’s inability to speak does not stem from weakness but from a vow of service, a remnant of Opraxi vestal tradition where one’s hands and deeds convey devotion more truthfully than words. Though she cannot voice her thoughts, her expression lies in her art—the dresses she tailors, the hairstyles she arranges, and the calm she brings to the storm of courtly life.

    History

    Novon

    Cho is trusted with part of the winter grain that Kota sends to Novon in secret. Kota has never had to say aloud what she's asking for.

    Story

    Spoiler: Episode 2

    She met Artemis and Azlan when Koto brought them to the palace, oversaw the preparations for the king and queen's return, and saw the two dressed and led to the main hall to be received.
