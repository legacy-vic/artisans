    The prince who would have followed his father, Anteo Zuul, to the High Throne, had he not died fighting the titans. First born son of Anteo and Endelyn Larrensire, and husband of Dendrilla Ariadne, he's remembered for the arrogance that led him from one battle to another, and for the war his death set off.

    Appearance & Character

    Archon had black hair streaked with bronze, and his father's blue eyes. He grew stubble earlier than most of his family, and many women considered him handsome.

    Throughout his life, Archon's arrogance and pride led him from one battle to another, winning most and fearing little.

    History

    Marriage

    His father wanted him to marry a Kalmere, but at the time, the only choice was a girl twelve years younger than him, Oriane Kalmere. Archon married his mother's choice, Dendrilla Ariadne, who stayed at Wietmoire Manor after it was taken from the Talvos (see The Massacre at Wietmoire). Their firstborn son, Haylor, was a stillbirth.

    Death

    He rushed into battle to aid his friends in Stade, and lost one of them, Sabba, to a titan. He sought vengeance on it and killed the wrong one. With Joras, he set out to stop the titans his act had provoked from reaching Stade, and only then learned what fear was. He was killed trying to flee. See The Death of Archon.

    Legacy

    His wife was pregnant when he died, and people did not believe the child, Alexium, was his. With Alexium disinherited, the succession fell to his brother, Aethel.
