    Varrow's Tree

    Also known as Varrow's Leaf

    A tree with white, flaky bark and thin, blue, near-translucent leaves. The one in Endelyn Larrensire's garden in High Sonham shimmers in various colors at noon, when the sunlight shines through the painted glass and onto the tree.

    It's poisonous, though there's no struggle and no choking when one consumes its leaf. Endelyn ate of it, wanting only to be happy, and died. She's entombed beneath it.
