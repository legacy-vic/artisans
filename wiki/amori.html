    Amori

    A child of the Abunali in Khamu who hopes to become a kashif, and Azra's childhood friend.

    Contents
    • Appearance & Character
    • History
    • The Kashif Trials
    • Story
    • Quotes

    Appearance & Character

    Amori is gentle, and quick to smooth over a quarrel. Since the Kashif Trials, he bears the mark of Isi: a horizontal stripe on each cheek, and a more detailed line from his forehead to the bridge of his nose.

    History

    The Kashif Trials

    A child of the Abunali, he befriended Azra during her stay in Khamu. He had no partner he trusted for the trials, so she promised to hunt with him, and the two were grouped with Artemis and Afina. Marshal T'Akoteh knew him by name, much to his delight. Theirs was the first group to finish the opening trial. When the groups were reformed, he and Azra joined another, and he told Artemis and Afina it wasn't goodbye. Elder Azhotep anointed him for his comradery and compassion.
