    The Founding of Trion

    How Cedric Talvo and Lord Hiro Kuroshi raised a new Talvar in southern Athura, in the years between The Massacre at Wietmoire and The Crowning of Onfroi.

    Contents
    • The Cave
    • Hiro's Request
    • Novon and Greystone
    • Trion
    • Whispers

    The Cave

    Several weeks after they arrived, Hiro took Cedric to a cave at the base of the cliff that overlooks Blackrock Bay. Nestled within was the accumulated fortune of the Talvos, or a hefty portion of it. Gideon had foreseen the challenges that lay ahead and hidden it there with Hiro, while Cedric saw to the refugees. Cedric hadn't known of it. Talvar was built with it.

    Hiro's Request

    Cedric felt that he was forever indebted to Hiro, who wanted no part of the fortune for himself. Hiro asked only one thing of him: to marry Onfroi to his granddaughter, Kota. Recognizing the genuine connection between Onfroi and Hiro's grandchildren, Cedric found the proposition to be in the best interest of both families, and gave his word.

    Novon and Greystone

    With the succession of his family secured, Cedric's next goal was a stronghold for them to live within for the foreseeable future. For months, Hiro brought Opraxi refugees across the sea (see Internal Struggle) to live in the region and aid in the formation of Novon and Greystone, a keep constructed from the limestone deposits of the region that could last centuries.

    Trion

    Even after Greystone's construction, the surplus resources facilitated a small town around the keep, and eventually a formidable wall, stretching from the southernmost to the westernmost cliff. This second, more noble settlement was named Trion, after the tallest keep in Talvar, his bloodline's fallen city of old.

    With Trion and Novon showing promise, Cedric's vision of a new kingdom began to take shape. A harbor was underway, the crops and livestock were ever-growing, and there was a busy mining operation along the shores of Blackrock Bay.

    Whispers

    In time, whispers of Talvar's existence reached Icosian shores, and the ears of those loyal to the High Crown. Cedric realized that political legitimacy was paramount if he wanted to secure a future for his family and their new home. See The Crowning of Onfroi.
