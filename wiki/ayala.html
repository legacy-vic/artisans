    A prodigy among the jotuun, and the daughter of Augmen and Alibet, known to people as the demon's spawn. She ended the Ten-Year War by killing her father at the Temple of Isi, and went into hiding with the last of her order. She's dead by 211 ABU.

    Appearance & Character

    Ayala was of Abunali descent, with white hair (see White Hair) and yellow eyes. People know a woman of her description as the demon's spawn.

    History

    The farm

    Her mother ran off when she was only days old, and Augmen left her with the family of his friend, Aniketh, when the two of them went to join the jotuun. When she was brought there, the family looked upon her at once with grave concern: "The hidden ones watch this one closely." Everyone on the farm and in town refused to interact with her because they feared Oiyas, and the farm's rapid decline in crops and increasingly frequent raids reinforced it. When she was four, her father took her along, as the jotuun were very open-minded in comparison.

    Prodigy

    Ayala trained as a neophyte under the supervision of several priors, all of whom witnessed her excel through the many trainings and exercises they put her through.

    Icos

    She was presented with the opportunity of a lifetime: she'd excel past the rank of paladin, so long as she trained at the Temple of Isi, on Icos. She was hesitant to leave her father behind, but she accepted, against his very vocal disapproval.

    The rift

    Augmen's study of quintecrakin had him expelled, and the two of them fell out. As he grew feared, Ayala became a respected and powerful jotuun, who used her abilities for the betterment of others and upheld the principles of the order.

    The jotuun told her that Augmen had killed Aniketh, but not what for, only that it occurred the night of his expulsion (see Aniketh). She was distraught, and believed that her father must have had a good reason. After much contemplation, and hearing of his further exploits, she withdrew from that notion.

    The Ten-Year War

    For ten years, the war went around the Temple of Isi. When it finally reached her, she killed her father. See Ten-Year War.

    Qis

    After the war, she hid from the world in Qis with seven other jotuun, Mirak among them, until Aethel Zuul found them. She was defensive, and asked why she shouldn't kill him where he stood. He told her he wasn't his father. He earned her trust, and they grew much closer over several years, always meeting in private. She fell in love with him, and he with her, though their love could never have been pursued. It ended when Qis was found. Late in their relationship, she heavily mistrusted and actively hated him.

    Death

    She died protecting Mirak, along the eastern cliffs near Medjet. She was training him when the hair on the back of her neck began to stand. Someone was watching. All seven Inquisitors of Zuul's Hand had to dispatch her, and she went down fighting. In the end, she killed all but one, and they had to be replaced over time. Alfor Zuul, still a prince, had sent them.
