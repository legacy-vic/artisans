    The Massacre at Wietmoire

    Also known as The Wietmoire Massacre

    The killing of much of the Talvo Royal Family at Wietmoire Manor, their family home. It's an open secret that it was Anteo Zuul's doing, by then High King. When Talvar fell, the Zuuls had needed a king's law, and the Kalmeres' soldiers, to bring the Talvos down. This time they were the law, and needed nobody. The High King sought not only dominion over Icos, but the eradication of any threat to his power.

    Away in Athura

    King Gideon Talvo, his son Cedric, and Lord Hiro Kuroshi weren't there when it happened. They were in Athura, bringing Opraxi refugees across the sea (see Internal Struggle).

    The Return

    Assassins had come in the night, and left no mercy in their wake. Gideon, Cedric, and Hiro came home to the aftermath. As they moved through the bloodstained halls, Gideon's and Cedric's hearts shattered with each familiar face that stared lifelessly back at them. Women, children, pets, and mere servants all lay dead.

    The Gardens

    Needing to escape it, they hurried out through one of the back doors and into the gardens. There, Cedric spotted a dying servant. With his last breath, the servant told him, "Your mother, they t-took her... Zuul sent his regards." Cedric's mother, Queen Kaelyn Skyfall, would be killed by Anteo.

    Gideon, known for his level-headedness, succumbed to the overwhelming rage that consumed him. Cedric stood and asked to fight by his father's side, before a small child approached from the back of the gardens. It was his son, Onfroi. The servants had hidden him from what was unfolding within the manor. Cedric rushed over to embrace him.

    Gideon's Stand

    Gideon saw the love Cedric had for his son, and understood the feeling. He ordered Hiro to flee with the two of them to Athura. Cedric was torn between staying to fight and preserving his family's line, and pleaded with his father to face Anteo together. Gideon refused. It was a burden he believed must be faced alone. He stayed to face Anteo.

    The Knee

    Gideon walked into High Sonham unannounced. Anteo had Kaelyn brought in, and offered what the Talvos had taken once before: kneel, and she'd live (see The Fall of Talvar). Gideon had apologized to the hilltribes for that knee, and sworn that no Talvo would kneel again unless they said so. He began to go down, and Kaelyn cried out, telling him to refuse. He stood back up. Bending the knee to Anteo would have meant undoing everything he'd worked so hard to rebuild. Nobody in that room would walk out understanding that, but he held true to an oath, and it cost him. His wife made sure he remembered, even at the cost of her life.

    Anteo was visibly upset to hear her, and to see Gideon listen. He shouted, doubling down, wanting to revel in his victory. Gideon simply shook his head, silent, his face as still as stone. Anteo reiterated everything Gideon had just lost, and asked what more it would take for him to kneel. "...Watching your wife's head roll?"

    Gideon said one sentence, in his gruff voice. "You are no king."

    Anteo drew his blade, huffing with anger. Gideon knew that if it came to a duel, he'd win. Anteo knew it as well, and ordered his men to force him onto his knees. Gideon was tall and strong, and they had to kick the bend of his knees in order to drop him. Kaelyn kicked and screamed the entire time, restrained by two men. Anteo approached, blade in hand, and Gideon simply lowered his head, already knowing the outcome. The sword rose, and it dropped. Kaelyn's blood-curdling scream rang throughout the walls of High Sonham, followed by the thud of Gideon's body dropping to the cold stones.

    Afterward, Anteo took his anger out on Kaelyn. He struck her with the pommel of the sword before tossing it aside to beat her with his bare hands, and strangled her to death in the very same room. He had them thrown from the walls into the ravine, and nothing of them was ever found. He wasn't the honorable type to bury his dead enemies.

    Aethel was in the room, and did nothing. His mother, Endelyn, heard it from her garden, where she was kneeling for the Talvos' dead. That night there were two more to pray for. The two of them never spoke of it.

    Aftermath

    Anteo seized the manor, and the Zuuls have held it since. Dendrilla Ariadne lived there as Archon Zuul's wife. Lady Haileen Ermsburry, Onfroi's mother, was with her family, the Ermsburrys, in Endletonn that night. Anteo's order reached them before Cedric could, and she was kept there. The survivors raised a new Talvar in Athura, where Onfroi would be king. See The Founding of Trion.
