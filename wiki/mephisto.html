    Mephisto

    Mephisto

    Range
    Abu, Bali

    Timroquet's final child, and the first to be slain. Mephisto was a raging terra dra'iq that terrorized the westernmost regions of Athura. Known for using her powerful jaws to chuck boulders at settlements and causing sinkholes that swallowed temples whole, Mephisto demonstrated to all of Athura why all dra'iq are a threat, even if they don't spew fire or fly above the clouds. In 186 ABU, Mephisto was slain by Mirak who decided to take action against the dra'iq as he saw her laying waste to the villages that once existed where Pa'iq rests. Using a combination of aerokin and jeokin, he attempted to force her to submit. But being a dra'iq her temper only grew, and in turn so did her strength, and she eventually escaped his grasp and tore through what little was left of the settlements she destroyed. Unable to keep the beast under control, Mirak had decided that she had to be put down, and so he used his kinmatter to finally kill her. Her remains rest along the Banta Gorge portion of Mandor Canyon, and her skull now hangs in the throne room of Yidji Palace in Sulala.
