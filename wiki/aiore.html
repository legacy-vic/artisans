    The late queen of Phylomina, wife of King Alexium Ariadne and mother of Queen Vaenera and Azra. Born in the Anklan Isles and held in high esteem among the Abunali Islanders, she died about a year after her husband, and by 211 ABU her elder daughter rules in their place.

    Appearance & Character

    Aiore was gentle, yet very headstrong. She was royalty of sorts in the Anklan Isles, held with high esteem among the Abunali Islanders, and desired by many men.

    History

    Two suitors

    She met Kade Asher when he made landfall on Tabla Pise, but had accepted Alexium's proposal and borne a child by the time he'd left his crew. A couple of years later, she returned to Morowai, found Kade caring for her elderly mother, and fell in love with him all over again. Azra was born of the affair.

    The confession

    Guilt-ridden, she eventually told Alexium the truth, and chose to leave with Azra rather than give up Kade's whereabouts. Alexium refused to let her go, and sent Azra away. See Vaenera & Azra's Origin.

    Foraging

    In the years after Malu was renamed, she made a hobby of foraging from the local jungles with Vaenera. On one of those days she reached into a bush, and took hold of something she shouldn't have brought home. See The Fall of Malu.

    Morowai

    After Morowai was burned, Aiore went looking for Azra and her mother. She did not find Azra, but she found her mother in what little was left of her abode, curled in the fetal position, ash and bone, clutching Azra's toy. She came back with it, gave it to Alexium without a word, and never spoke to him again.

    Death

    She outlived Alexium by about a year, and hers is among the losses that Vaenera blames herself for. It's when Vaenera first began to lean on Alfor Zuul.
