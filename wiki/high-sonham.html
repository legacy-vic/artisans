    High Sonham

    High Sonham

    Show on the map

    A place only heard of, as no one in the realms knows its location. It was built in the mountains by the Talvos, and taken by the Zuuls when Talvar fell (see The Fall of Talvar). Home of the Highguard, who escort the monarchs of the Bicontinental Union here for the yearly Royal Conclave.

    This article is a stub. More may be written here in time.

    Endelyn's Garden

    The garden of Endelyn Larrensire lies within a large room that originally meant something sacred to the Talvos. Anteo Zuul allowed it to be used as a garden space. It has painted glass panes and a skylight, where the blinding white light from the Sonham Mountains reflects off of the colored glass, illuminating the room in a beautiful manner. Statues of mourning priestesses line the sides of the room. At the center is a Varrow's Tree, still young, but no less beautiful.

    The floor of the room is unfinished, with tile stopping just shy of the entrance. This allows the plants to take root indoors, and the room is kept warmer by the subtle heat rising through the ground. Paired with the sunlight and the plants, it has a very faint greenhouse effect. Greenish-blue grass has grown in the sun-kissed portions of the room.

    It was Endelyn's tradition to kneel before the tree and pray quietly, from the time the garden was granted to her last day. She's entombed underneath it, where she remains untouched.

    After her death, Anteo visited the garden frequently. Aethel, in his grief, refused to enter the room, and ordered servants to keep it maintained for the memory of his mother. Alfor visits seldomly, to quiet his mind, though he doesn't tend its plants.

    The Dead

    The room is where the dead lay. When High Sonham was a refuge (see The Fall of Talvar), the Talvos buried their own there beside the hilltribes' dead, and the floor was never finished because it was never meant to be walked on. Endelyn's tree grows out of them, and she was laid among them.

    Anteo didn't know. He figured the Talvos were never able to finish flooring it, and thought the statues peculiar decor. Endelyn was mortified to find the dead beneath the dirt when she first planted the tree. Instead of screaming and running to tell Anteo, she knelt. It's them she prayed for.

    The servants who dug her tomb found the dead, and understood what she'd been kneeling to. They buried her among them and kept quiet, as she had.
