    Atlae

    The most dominant sentient species on the planet Artisans are the atlae, who resemble modern humans to a degree. Averaging a height of 5'5", they possess large cat-like ears that vary in shape and size depending on their race. Their tails, much like their ears, also differ in shape, length, and patterning based on their race. The biodiversity among the atlae suggests that several distinct species once existed across the globe. Over time, these species evolved into the modern atlae, now primarily inhabiting two main land masses. Their average lifespan is 123 years.

    General Physiology

    Within their mouths, atlae don't differ much from humans other than the fact that their tongues are 40% flatter, and that their canines develop quicker, being the first few teeth to grow during infancy, and larger, providing a significantly more wounding bite than a human could.

    Adorning the tips of their toes and fingers rest thick claws that vary in shape and length from individual to individual. This seems largely unaffected by ethnic differences. Their claws sheathe a bone that helps strengthen the structure, making them effective tools for climbing and prying shelled foods open with ease.

    An atlaes pupils are reflective and their eyeballs are larger than human eyes by 20%, allowing them to capture more light and see in low-light conditions far better. The ability to contract their pupils into cat-like slits seemingly at will is indicative of low-to-the-ground predatory hunting during their prehistory.

    Atlae have tails that vary in length depending on the individual, and within Icosian tradition they're typically removed at birth as it's seen as 'primitive' by many noble families. Those too old to clip their tails without permanently injuring themselves prefer hiding it to appear more fashionable. However, most cultures around the known world of Artisans embrace their tails, and some adorn them with jewelry, fashioning them beautifully.

    The atlae of Athura have a higher likelihood of being born with patterns on their hair and skin resembling the big cats of Earth. Conversely, the atlae of Icos are more likely to be born with more body hair and bulkier physiques.

    White Hair

    White hair is a rare genetic anomaly, and a bad omen. People believe it brings death, as white is the color most commonly associated with Oies, the God of Death. In Icos, those born with it are treated far more harshly, with many punishments seeming almost disproportionate.

    Ethnic Groups

    Opraxi

    • Opraxi atlae typically have pale skin with jet-black hair and grey, monolid eyes. Their eyebrows are round, almost circle spots instead of the long, horizontal eyebrows you might expect. They typically come in about a foot shorter than the average height for an atlae, and their tails vary a bunch. Some opraxi have long, sleek, pantherine tails. While others have wide, yet stubby cotton balls.

    Uprorian

    • Uprorian atlae have historically been outcasted time and time again due to their inclination to associate with barbaric cultures. Their physical appearance doesn't quite help with that reputation either. They have a taller, more muscular build, with rounded ears and more hair coverage. Needless to say, your average atlae would be intimidated just being in the presence of an uprorian. Especially paired with the exceptionally rare genetic mutation resulting in white eyes. Their hair colors range from ginger to beige, and their skin from pale to... well, pale. Given that Upror in the present day has largely been vacant, aside from the odd "feral" men that'll show up every now and again, it's getting increasingly harder to find a pureblood uprorian.

    Morgari

    • Morgari atlae share a lineage with uprorians but embrace a passive and nomadic lifestyle. The majority of Morgar's inhabitants don't live in or around any major settlements, and choose to live off the land the old fashioned way. They have physical characteristics similar to uprorians such as the massive height and muscle mass, as well as the thicker body hair, but can be distinguished by their thick northern accent.

    Ashelan

    • Ashelan atlae are culturally diverse but can be identified by their fair skin, blonde or brown hair, and an accent reminiscent of New Zealanders with a mix of British. The atlae on the eastern coast of Stade commonly have an olive complexion, with wavy hair and brown eyes. These atlae are as average as they come. Not too tall, not too short, not too thin, not too bulky. Their claws are usually shaven and tails trimmed since that's what passes as 'proper' in their culture.

    Anklanae

    • Anklanae atlae are shorter than average, and are acclimated to the seas, hunting and gathering from coral reefs and traveling via fishing boats. These atlae are of an umber complexion with rich brown hair. Their hair's texture is wavy to curly. Traditionally, the atlae of Anklan'A need to be fair swimmers if they're to be taken seriously, so despite being smaller than average, natural selection favored atlae with a healthier set of lungs and a knack for athleticism.

    Matause

    • Matause atlae have dark skin, thick eyelashes, black hair, and brown eyes. Their ears are smaller and rounded, and some have panthera-like spots on their hair and skin. Historically, the atlae of Matau were slaves and slavers, so a mix of various ethnic groups and dead cultures make up what matause people are now. Matause atlae are taller than average, and are generally known for their bad tempers. Pair this with their knack for physicality and things can get bloody quick. Though, this isn't all bad, as physical touch is also their love language. They're inclined to be very touchy when introducing themselves to familiarize themselves with others.

    Balian

    • Balian atlae are, very similarly to matause atlae, a diverse group shaped by slave trades, immigration, and wars within Bali. They have clay skin, brown to black hair, and brown to orange eyes with pointed, tufted ears. Their noses are somewhat downward curved, and their eyes are typically monolid like the opraxi. They possess lynx-like spots on their hands and forearms, feet and shins, along their spine and on their tails, and around their necks.

    Abuun

    • Abuun atlae are born with beige or orange hair, with a honey complexion and bright eye colors like green, yellow, and orange. Atlae of Abu have larger, pointed tipped ears with a rare chance of being born with tiger-like stripes on their skin and hair.
