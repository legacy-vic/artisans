    The founder of Trion and of the Kingdom of Talvar, derided by his contemporaries as 'The Illegitimate King.' Son of King Gideon Talvo and Queen Kaelyn Skyfall, husband of Lady Haileen Ermsburry, and father of King Onfroi Talvo, who wears the crown he was refused. Dead by 211 ABU, he's entombed beneath Greystone.

    History

    The refugees

    With his father and Lord Hiro Kuroshi, Cedric brought Opraxi refugees across the sea to Athura (see Internal Struggle). They came home to find their family killed, and Onfroi alive in the gardens. Cedric pleaded to face Anteo Zuul at his father's side, but was sent away with his son. Haileen, who'd been with her family in Endletonn, was left in Icos. See The Massacre at Wietmoire.

    Trion

    Exiled from Icos for treason, Cedric raised Greystone and then Trion with Hiro's help, and promised his son to Hiro's granddaughter. See The Founding of Trion. His reign cemented the city as a haven for immigrants from both Oprax and Ashela.

    Return to Icos

    The same year Anteo Zuul died, he returned to Icos to legitimize his realm, where the court of Karlaine whispered of 'the Prince Overseas.' He turned down the daughter of Aethel Zuul for Onfroi, having already promised him to Kota, and left without the crown he'd asked for. His son had it instead. See The Crowning of Onfroi. Aethel then told him, alone, how his parents had died. He was the only Zuul who'd ever told Cedric the truth. Afterward, Cedric took his son to see Haileen, who'd heard of his landfall and didn't expect him to show up. She sailed to Trion with them.

    Beside the throne

    Cedric ran the realm from beside the throne, and never once sat in it. In court he knelt to his son like anyone else, and meant it. When Onfroi came of age he stepped back the same day, and that night told him the way to High Sonham. He never told him what Aethel had said, only that his grandfather hadn't knelt.

    Later life

    He lived with Haileen for a long while, and lived to see the ships stop when Nirva closed Blackrock Bay. He told Onfroi to ask Aethel Zuul for help, as one honorable man would another, and died still sure it'd come. Onfroi had never asked. Haileen outlived him.

    Legacy

    He and Haileen are entombed next to each other in the crypt beneath Greystone. Alfor Zuul dismisses Talvar's pleas for aid, viewing its suffering as the consequence of its founder's defiance.
