    The third High King of the Bicontinental Union, dead by 211 ABU. Second born son of Endelyn Larrensire and the second High King, Anteo Zuul, husband of Catalina Valentyre, and father of Tybal, Aeleina, and the current High King, Alfor Zuul. He sought to undo his father's harm where he could, and made a king of Onfroi Talvo, whose family his father had destroyed.

    Appearance & Character

    Aethel took after his mother. He had long, flowing, black hair streaked with bronze, which reached the middle of his back, eyes of a deep violet that was closer to red, and a large burn on his left shoulder and neck. He was always thin, and grew tall with age as she had. His features were lean, and sharper than his father's, who was much more broadly built, as the Uprorians are. Many in the realm referred to him as 'pretty' as opposed to handsome, though he was far from unattractive, and was suspected to have several paramours. It was only rumor, based on the way he acted. His way of speaking was softer than his father's, almost flirtatious, and he'd sooner spar with his words than raise his voice. He enjoyed attending feasts and tourneys, fraternizing with nobles across the realms, exchanging tales, and tasting the local wine and, as people told it, the women, wherever he went.

    He was intelligent, brave, and forgiving, but indecisive. He wanted to earn his father's respect. He took no pleasure in being flattered, but didn't dislike being obeyed.

    History

    The heir

    Aethel wasn't born first in line. His elder brother, Archon, died fighting the titans (see The Death of Archon), and the child Archon's wife bore after it was disinherited.

    Under his father

    As a boy, he'd watched his newborn brother struggle to breathe, and was told not to look (see Endelyn Larrensire). He was in the room when his father killed Gideon and Kaelyn, and did nothing (see The Massacre at Wietmoire).

    His mother's garden

    Out of grief, Aethel refused to enter the garden in High Sonham where Endelyn is entombed, and ordered servants to keep it maintained for her memory.

    High King

    When his father choked, only Aethel was in the hall with him, and he did nothing. A young Alfor heard the panicked rushing of servants, and came in to find his father sitting unbothered, sipping his wine as they tried to save Anteo. Aethel watched through the edge of his brow, quietly humming to himself. Tybal was at court in Karlaine, and Aeleina was with her mother at Wietmoire. Anteo had never abdicated, so the High Throne passed to Aethel.

    The Talvos

    The year of his father's death, Cedric Talvo, whose family had been killed on an order people knew was Anteo's (see The Massacre at Wietmoire), came before him in Karlaine. The two had known one another years before. Aethel meant to mend the feud and offered his daughter to Cedric's son, Onfroi. When Cedric kept an older promise instead, Aethel respected him for it, and made Onfroi a king. See The Crowning of Onfroi. Afterward, alone with Cedric, he told him that Gideon hadn't knelt, and that he himself had stood there. After Nirva took Tybal and closed Talvar's bay, he waited for a letter from Onfroi asking for help, and would've sent everything he had. It never came.

    The burn

    As High King, Aethel wanted to understand how many had survived his father's purge (see Ten-Year War), and had a jotuun survivor brought to him as a prisoner. Believing the Zuuls to want all jotuun gone, the prisoner feared being already dead, and attacked. It was a cornered being's fear that gave Aethel his scar. The jotuun was executed for it, against Aethel's wishes.

    The prisoner was killed by an Inquisitor of Zuul's Hand, but not before giving up where the jotuun holdouts were. Aethel reprimanded the Inquisitor, but couldn't deny the results, and acted to find them.

    Ayala

    He found Ayala, Augmen's daughter, hiding out with seven other jotuun in the ancient ruined city of Qis. She asked why she shouldn't kill him where he stood. "Because I am not my father," he answered. He earned her trust, and over several years they grew much closer. They always met in private, as she'd be recognized at once. The two fell in love, though it could never have been pursued. It ended when Qis was found, through no doing of his, and Ayala came to heavily mistrust and actively hate him. What it cost his wife is told in Catalina Valentyre. She'd confessed to him, and he told no one. He let Ayala hate him rather than his wife, and let Alfor blame Ayala rather than his mother. Alfor had the Hand kill her for it, and Aethel learned of it when only one came back.

    Dendrilla's letter

    He'd read the letter that Dendrilla Ariadne had written to his father, and known that he sat on his nephew's throne. He wrote back to her, telling her it was never her fault, and he would also send money. It's how Alexium came to be well off. His reply ended with a request, that she keep the truth from her son. As long as Alexium didn't know, nobody could make him a claimant. Her letter was among his things when he died.

    Death

    He died by Alfor's hand. Alfor visited him as he sat in front of the hearth, staring into the flame, deep in thought, and spoke to him for several minutes, already having decided his father's fate. Aethel was quiet. He was unhappy with his life, and had already made peace with himself. Though, he apologized to Alfor, never having wanted his son's upbringing to be like his own.

    For as long as he remembered, Alfor had believed his father to be weaker than his grandfather, and he was taken aback. He didn't make it known, but there was a sudden sense of guilt for what he was about to do, and yet, he continued. Finally, he asked his father if he would like some tea, and Aethel accepted. He poured him a cup of Varrow's Leaf tea, and they sat by the hearth, staring at it in silence until Aethel shut his eyes and didn't open them. There was no struggle, and no choking. Aethel succumbed to the same fate as his mother, but unlike her, he died wearing a slight grin. He'd known what was in the cup. He found peace.
