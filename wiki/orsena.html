    Orsena

    Orsena

    Show on the map

    An extravagant city populated by wealthy merchants and noblemen. The city is jointly ruled by the Bank of Pearls and the Orsena sovereignty, which are both in a perpetual struggle against each other for power. The Civic Guard - which report to the Orsena sovereignty - is responsible for maintaining order within the city and likewise protecting it from external threats. Conversely, the Bank of Pearls holds sway over trade and finance. In any case, Orsena is widely known for its grandeur and peerless wealth.

    Besides its evident splendor, however, the city is a hub of talented artisans; innumerous people flock to the city annually in search of employment. Resident merchants embraced the opportunity wholly, employing many of them personally, but have also capitalized on it by forming institutions to organize tradesmen in order to present them to visiting merchants as well.

    The city is organized into two distinctive areas: the Berra District and Elias District. Whilst the Elias District does not compare to its upper class counterpart, it is relatively flawless in comparison to the likes of Suran and other Athuran cities. Its streets are generally well kept and order is maintained by the Civic Guard. The area is notable for its accessibility to the trading port via an elevator hugging the cliff's surface. The Berra District, meanwhile, is responsible for Orsena's reputation, and home to both the Bank of Pearls and Hypatina Palace; in addition, a myriad of wealthy merchants and noblemen reside there inside lavish estates. The Berra District is elevated slightly above the Elias District, and is decorated with a dozen plazas. Foremost amongst these plazas is the Grand Plaza, which stands in front of the Hypatina Palace and is well known for its statues of the first members of the royal family - Orsena's founders.

    Orsena boasts three primary walls: two on the exterior and one on the interior separating the districts. Constructed by the city's resident artisans, the walls are sturdy and feature elaborate murals. The Gates of Shakir are embedded into the two exterior walls and dominate the city's façade. The second exterior wall is deliberately and noticeably taller than that of the first, and this is paralleled in the Gates of Shakir as well. The Gates of Shakir are composed of bruthil and embellished with specks of jade, reminiscent of their namesake's war-hammer. The gates are considered an engineering marvel, crafted by the many talents of Orsena, due to their incorporation of bruthil. The interior gate, on the other hand, is far less impressive in terms of ingenuity but compensates in sheer expenditure being composed of both gold and belvium. Adjacent to the Golden Gate is the Central Barracks which boasts the office of the Lord Commander of the Civic Guard.
