    The Fall of Azkassar

    The Kingdom of Azkassar was always real, even if some don't believe in it. The story of its founders, however, is a fable that has existed from time immemorial, and neither of them is proven to have existed. The Kingdom is said to have been ruled by a line of god-kings, of which all descend from Azkassar's founder, Lucan and his queen, Millera. Both held immense power in their own right, and are said to have ruled together in perfect harmony. Under their reign, the Azkassari were revered, and the realm prospered. Unprecedented levels of peace prevailed throughout.

    In spite of the generations of Azkassari rulers after them, however, neither are confirmed to have produced an heir to inherit their vast realm; further, none knew of their next-to-kin. After their deaths, the problem plagued the kingdom through many of its rulers, who frequently claimed legitimacy through blood, even that no one could prove such claims false.

    After more than eight hundred years of supremacy, the kingdom would eventually succumb to infighting between claimant factions, with the wars now collectively referred to as The God Wars. In its final years, priests and jotuun would warn of a great disaster that will befall the Azkassari Kingdom, and many of whom were punished. Tongues, throats, eyes, and ears were removed. Some were even executed; such an act was seen as a slight against the very gods they worshipped and claimed exalted blood from. At the tail end of these waning years, the land would crack violently and through the fissures, magma rose; from the sky, fire fell; and from the hills, lava spilled. The capital, Azkaria, that had once glittered so vibrantly was no more. The land that once seated the heart of the realm became lost to time, engulfed by the rising waters of Abunala Sea.
