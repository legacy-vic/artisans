    Leander Polys

    The lord of the house of Polys, of Lorence, and once Sea Marshal in the King of Orsena's court, an office he's lost to Doros Meredeon. He's the husband of Elise Polys, and hates her brother, Kade Asher, whom he captured and now has to watch succeed where his fleet couldn't.

    Appearance & Character

    Leander hates Kade for what he is. He believes him to be a good-for-nothing killer, despite the fact that Kade saved Elise, without which Leander would never have married her at all.

    History

    The capture

    As Sea Marshal, he captured Kade and raided his old crew's hideout, and most of them escaped. See Capture.

    The hunt

    After Morowai burned, Kade was set to hunting his old crew down. Leander couldn't take them out with a fleet, and Kade, with next to nothing to his name, is dispatching them one by one. In a way, it's showing Leander up. He's since lost his office to Doros Meredeon.
