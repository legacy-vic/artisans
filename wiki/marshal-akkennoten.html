    Marshal Akkennoten

    Marshal Akkennoten

    Gender
    Male
    Titles
    Kashif Marshal
    Allegiance
    Abunali, Kashif
    Traits
    Stern, patient, perceptive.

    One of the twelve marshals of the kashif, and so admired that the children of Khamu go out of their way to meet him. He takes a group of them through the hunting trials of the Kashif Trials.

    Appearance & Character

    Akkennoten watches and listens before he speaks. He sets traps rather than rush into a hunt, and holds patience to be among the most important skills a kashif can have.

    History

    The Kashif Trials

    He led Artemis, Afina, Ano, Aurel and Arya through the hunting trials. He had them climb to camp by their claws, and tested each of them with the bow before he'd take them into the jungle, telling them what their shot would have meant on a real hunt. He reminded them that companionship would be weighed alongside their kills, and that the highest score alone wouldn't make them fit to be kashif.

    Quotes

    "Hunting isn't just about killing the biggest thing. If that's what you want, then being a kashif isn't for you."

    — to Aurel

    Story

    Spoiler: Episode 1A

    On the final day, he and the children with him drove a tirvaan off Artemis and Afina with arrows and a torch. He sent them all back to camp, stayed to keep it away from them and returned with his left arm covered in blood. He led them toward Khamu, but the beast caught up at a gully. He let the children climb out first, then faced it with a single blade and ordered them to run.
