    Tarris Kalmere

    Tarris Kalmere

    Titles
    First High King, King of Stade
    Allegiance
    Kalmere Royal Family, Bicontinental Union
    Issue
    Mandel Kalmere

    The first High King of the Bicontinental Union, and the king of Stade who took Ōnari and Kabejō Garrison from Oprax and the west from the Talvos. Long dead by 211 ABU, he was the father of Mandel Kalmere, and grandfather of King Lanis Kalmere, who rules Stade now.

    History

    The Talvos

    As a young king, Tarris declared the Talvos guilty of treason in the wake of a dra'iq, and spared them when the last king in the west, Conrad Talvo, knelt. By the fall of their crown, the west became his by law. To do it, he burned the charter that had released them from his house's service. See The Fall of Talvar.

    King of Stade

    During his rule, Stade took Ōnari and Kabejō Garrison from Oprax, which was unable to strategically supply either. It was a victory his successor would never match. See Anteo's Failure.

    High King

    The title of High Sovereign was created with him. The Union he first led forbade the predatory seizure of weakened territory. Anteo Zuul was voted in after him.
