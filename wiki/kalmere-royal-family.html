    Kalmere Royal Family

    Also known as House Kalmere, The Kalmeres

    The royal family of Stade, which stood among the founding houses of the Bicontinental Union. They're the dynasty of Karlaine and Princes of Karlem, of mixed Ibyan and Moirish blood rather than Azkassari (see Titles and Ranks).

    This article is a stub. More may be written here in time.

    Members

    • Tarris Kalmere, grandfather of Lanis
    • Mandel Kalmere, father of Lanis
    • Oriane Kalmere, Mandel's daughter, passed over for her younger brother, who kept the old castle of Stade and began the cadet line there. Anteo Zuul wanted her for his heir, Archon, when she was a child. Her line comes after Lanis's daughters.
    • Marrec Kalmere, grandson of Oriane
    • King Lanis Kalmere, current ruler
    • Nephele, late first wife of Lanis
    • Queen Loranne Kalmere, second wife of Lanis
    • Princess Thea Kalmere, elder daughter of Lanis, by Nephele
    • Princess Reine Kalmere, younger daughter of Lanis, by Loranne
