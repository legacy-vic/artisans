    Elder Azhotep

    Elder Azhotep

    One of the three elders of the kashif and their commander-in-chief, who trained the marshals that serve under him. He presides over the Kashif Trials in Khamu, and holds firmly to their traditions.

    Appearance & Character

    Azhotep is tall and burly, and his large fur cloak and long, greying mane make him all the more imposing. He holds firmly to tradition, but is fair in his judgments.

    History

    The Kashif Trials

    He presided over the Kashif Trials in which Artemis, Azra and Afina took part, and took notice of Artemis the moment he saw him in the crowd. When disqualifications and complications left groups short, he had them reformed so that all might have an equal chance to finish. He anointed those who completed the trials.
