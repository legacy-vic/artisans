    Brightworm

    Brightworm

    Range
    Abu, Oprax, Talvar

    A bioluminescent worm species native to the southern forests of both Icos and Athura. The brightworms produce glowing silk, used primarily for building their webbing nests, where they lay their luminescent eggs. These nests glow indefinitely, especially since brightworms spend a significant portion of their lives within them. Though primarily herbivorous, consuming glowing brightpods within the jungles, they are also known to resort to cannibalism in times of scarcity, which can allow a secluded population to thrive a fair bit longer than most other species of worm.
