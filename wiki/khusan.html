    Khusan

    Also known as Khusan Boryign

    The chief of The Hunto, and the Ruler of the Gurtai behind the rumored unification of their tribes, whose name the world outside doesn't know. Of the Boryign clan, the younger son of Guryeg and Milun, he was offered to Oprax as a prisoner when he was a boy, and has sharpened the Gurtai into a weapon against it since he came home.

    Appearance & Character

    Khusan is imposing, and his goal is to unify the Gurtai tribes. Once a treaty hostage, he speaks fluent Foomesh, was trained in the very organization the Gurtai lost to, and returned with the one weapon his ancestors lacked.

    History

    The hostage

    He grew up in the Emperor's court in Oprax, alongside the girl who would become The Blood Empress. They grew up together, but house arrest is still living as someone's prisoner. Believing he was destined for more, he escaped, fleeing into the Opraxi countryside.

    The return

    For two years he wandered, avoiding recognition and accepting charity from strangers, until he found his way to the other side of the Kintari Mountains. After nearly freezing to death and being hunted by predators, he finally returned to the west, only to find that his father, a chieftain of one of the more powerful tribes, had died years ago, alongside his mother and brother.

    The family

    The Emperor wasn't responsible. Guryeg died during a hunt only a few years after Khusan had been offered as a prisoner. Gartan died attempting to rescue him from Oprax. Milun was taken as a prize by the new chieftain, Nuko, merely a late chieftain's wife turned concubine. She died attempting to perform a blood sacrifice with her unborn child, fathered by Nuko, to bring her family back. Had the ruthless Emperor known Guryeg had already perished, Khusan would've been put to death.

    The chief

    Nuko had risen as the new chieftain before his return. Khusan challenged Nuko and won, earning the title of chief, before uniting the rest of the tribes into The Gurtai Horde.

    The advisor

    Bal, the man whispering in his ear, is plotting, and encourages the young man's ruthless development. It was Bal who hid Guryeg's death from Oprax, which kept Khusan alive. It was an investment: a chieftain's son trained by the enemy was worth more to him than a chieftain. When Khusan wrote home, Bal answered as his father, shaping the boy from a distance and learning everything he confided. Khusan has since worked it out and says nothing, keeping Bal close because the voice that raised him is in there somewhere. Bal thinks his family were all needlessly foolish, but that blood magic is a useful tool when it isn't yourself you're sacrificing.

    The weapon

    Risen to prominence among the Gurtai, he's sharpened them into a weapon, structured and organized to directly fight Oprax, wanting vengeance against the Emperor. But the Emperor has died, and his daughter has taken his place.

    The alliance

    She saw how dangerous he is and what he has accomplished, and secured an alliance, citing their fleeting friendship when they were younger. He accepted warily, and she gained access to Blood Magic through him and his courtiers. His closest advisors warn him not to trust her. Privately, he's clear that he doesn't: he knows that his riders, once past Oprax's defenses, could easily take the basin.
