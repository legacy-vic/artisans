    Appearance & Character

    Kade was a free spirit who didn't allow himself to be controlled. When he was a pirate, he loved it.

    History

    Youth

    Kade's family were aristocratic. They were on their way to Lorence from Arlene, hoping to find more opportunity there, when pirates killed his parents and took his little sister, Elise. He fell into a life of piracy after that. The pirates responsible were later killed by Kade and some allies he'd made along the way, pirates themselves, and Elise was rescued. She married an aristocrat in Lorence, Leander Polys, lord of the house of Polys and Sea Marshal.

    Piracy

    By that point, Kade couldn't find it in himself to settle down, so he continued being a pirate, now a captain of his own crew. He was in his late twenties. They were a band of vagrants turned pirates, and he became infamous to civilians and pirates alike. Over time he fell out with them, and felt stuck among them. When they made landfall on Tabla Pise, he met Aiore, and put the life behind him for her sake. He gave the ship to his first mate and closest friend, Joss Harrow, one of the pirates who'd helped him rescue Elise. By the time he'd fully left his crew, she had accepted Alexium Ariadne's proposal.

    Morowai

    He remained on the island, caring for Aiore's elderly mother and supporting the sprouting villages of the community. When Aiore returned, the two had an affair, and Azra was born.

    Capture

    Kade was living comfortably in Morowai, helping Aiore's mother and trying to be a better man for her and their sprouting family, when he was located by the authorities. It was the house of Polys, acting on behalf of the crown of Orsena, and the Sea Marshal who captured him was his own brother-in-law. They threatened to throw him in a dungeon and have him rot for the rest of his days for the life he'd lived. The only way they wouldn't was if he gave up his old crew. He could lose his family, or give up his old one. The choice was the only mercy he'd get, and Elise had begged for it. He chose to sell his crew out, and they were hit for it. They'd always known whose wife Elise was, and for her sake they'd never touched a ship of the Polys. Then it was her husband who came for them, and Joss, who'd helped save her, lost the most. The authorities came crashing down on their hideout on one of the islands, but weren't efficient, and most of the crew were able to escape.

    Disappearance

    Once Aiore told Alexium the truth, Kade knew he couldn't stay in Morowai with Azra and her grandmother. He left them, and they never heard from him again.

    The final stand

    The pirates who later burned Morowai flew a sigil associated with him. They were his old crew. Kade returned once he saw their sails, as nothing good could come from that image. It was as though they knew what would draw him out of hiding. There was no way the authorities could have known of the hideout unless Kade had ratted, so they struck him as payback. Hard. They wanted Kade and his wife, believing Aiore to be living on the island at the time, and not knowing of her place in Phylomina. Azra was simply collateral to them. He made a final stand against Joss, lost, and just barely survived. See Vaenera & Azra's Origin.

    The hunt

    Kade was rescued by the authorities who'd confronted him in the first place. They'd realized they were playing a pirate's game, and they weren't pirates, so they had him hunt down each member of his old crew for them. He's done it with next to nothing to his name, and has believed all the while that everyone's dead, Azra included. Joss wouldn't see his crew dropping dead and not recruit more, so there are new faces among them, and by 211 ABU Kade has more left to kill than the captain. But his primary target, both personally and by contract, is Joss and his closest mates.
