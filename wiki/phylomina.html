    Phylomina

    Phylomina

    Show on the map

    The third-largest city in Athura, spanning across the entirety of a bay, with Malu Palace resting atop the island in the center. There are four primary shipyards, enabling the construction and maintenance of fleets, which happens to be what the city is known for; widely considered to be the most formidable naval military on the continent. Phylomina is home to some of the most ferocious fighters in all of Athura, thanks to the three large amphitheaters that host bloody tournaments where slaves and enlisted volunteers are pit against one another, as well as any beasts the public pay to see.

    Another distinct landmark within the city is the large citadel. It is the city's primary defensive structure, sitting atop the massive cliff overlooking the city, providing a strategic vantage point for monitoring the sea and the coast, keeping the city safe from any potential invasions. The tall bell towers are scattered throughout the city, chiming as the hours pass so the general populous knows the time at all times. They're also used as watchtowers by the city's soldiers since they provide an excellent view of the surrounding area. The garrison, located just beside the citadel, houses majority of the infantry, and it's situated between the two main entrances from the land so that in case of a land-locked siege, they can respond immediately. The many ports on the bayside are hotspots for the general population, since people can enjoy the warm waters as if they were on a beach due to the sloped ramps that lead into the ocean.

    Social Structure

    The city's economy is based on selling ships to various cities, selling gemstones and ore, selling fruits and other farmed resources, and selling fish.

    Upper Class:

    The upper class is comprised of wealthy merchants, shipowners, and members of the nobility. They own the majority of the city's businesses and control a large portion of the city's wealth. They live in luxurious homes and have access to all the finest amenities that the city has to offer. With wealth, comes opportunity to travel, and with this, comes political opinion. Many upper class individuals know their Queen is still quite young, and as such, assume she's gullible. A few individuals with the wealth to pull it off try to influence the Queen's judgement via bribes and other shady practices with varying success depending on how convincing they are.

    Middle Class:

    The middle class is made up of skilled artisans, shopkeepers, and merchants. They are relatively well-off and own their own businesses, but they do not have as much wealth or influence as the upper class. They rarely get to see the world outside of the city due to travel on foot being quite tedious and travel via boat being quite expensive. As such, many of them are apolitical; harbor no personal opinions on any matter.

    Lower Class:

    The lower class is made up of manual laborers, fishermen, and unskilled workers. They have the least wealth and the least access to the city's amenities. They live in cramped conditions, and many of them struggle to make ends meet. Despite this, they are an important part of the city's economy, and their hard work and dedication are vital to the city's prosperity. Many lower class individuals try and help themselves or their families out of poverty by participating in the fighting pits, where rewards for champions are exceptionally high.

    Slaves & Servants:

    The city has a relatively small population of slaves, mostly from neighboring regions, who are used mostly in the city's shipyards and in the construction of the city's infrastructure. The city's laws have a provision for freeing slaves if they have been living in the city for a certain period of time and have not committed any crimes.
