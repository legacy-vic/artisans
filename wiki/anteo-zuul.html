    The second High King of the Bicontinental Union, and the first Zuul to hold the High Throne. Long dead by 211 ABU, he's remembered as a tyrant, for the Ten-Year War, in which nearly all jotuun were destroyed, and for The Massacre at Wietmoire, which it's an open secret that he was behind. He was the husband of Endelyn Larrensire, and father of Archon and Aethel. The powers he was granted were never relinquished, and his grandson, Alfor Zuul, holds them now.

    Appearance & Character

    Anteo was pale and broadly built, as the Uprorians are, with the black hair and blue eyes of his house. He wore his hair long. He was cold and unforgiving, with little patience for those who took their time in telling him something, and he bore humiliation poorly. He was as petty as he could realistically be. His failure against Oprax agitated him to no end.

    History

    Early life

    Anteo was the son of Tybal Zuul I, who had him late in life and died in battle. He was raised briefly by his grandfather, Aevor Zuul, who had been head of the house when Talvar fell (see The Fall of Talvar), until Aevor himself died of a burst belly.

    The High Sovereignty

    Anteo was voted in after Tarris Kalmere, and was granted emergency powers by the rulers who joined the Union. He heavily abused them, securing succession rights for his children, and influenced those beneath him to call it the Zuul Union.

    Oprax

    He tried to force Oprax into the Union, and it simply waited him out behind its wall. His predecessor had taken Ōnari and Kabejō Garrison from it, which alone was enough to enrage him. See Anteo's Failure.

    The Talvos

    Anteo held the title of Duke of Tuvar, and no hilltribe had ever brought him a feud. He'd taken it for wildness, until he heard that they'd all been brought to someone: Gideon. It's an open secret that Anteo was behind The Massacre at Wietmoire. He had the Talvos killed in their family home and seized it. King Gideon Talvo walked into High Sonham alone to face him. Anteo offered him his wife's life if he knelt, and was refused. Knowing that Gideon would win a duel, he had his men force him down, and beheaded him. He beat and strangled Kaelyn to death in the same room, and had them both thrown into the ravine. He wasn't the honorable type to bury his dead enemies.

    His sons' marriages

    Endelyn chose the wives of both their sons, Dendrilla Ariadne for Archon and Catalina Valentyre for Aethel. Anteo wanted a Kalmere for Archon, and never liked either choice, but he didn't have the patience for it to suggest any further candidates.

    The loss of Archon

    When his eldest was killed by a titan, Anteo prepared for war within the day. He overpowered the titans in the forests between Upror and Morgar, but the jotuun intervened before he could have them slain, and he begrudgingly retreated from an army of them. See The Death of Archon.

    Archon's wife, Dendrilla Ariadne, gave birth after his death. Anteo took one look at the babe, Alexium, and denied that he was a Zuul. He disinherited the child and kicked Dendrilla out of Wietmoire. She wrote to him after, and he kept what she wrote as proof against her.

    The loss of Endelyn

    Archon's death left Endelyn in a depression she never recovered from, and the leaves she ate in her garden in hope of happiness killed her. Anteo pressured her caretakers to save her. He had her entombed beneath the tree that killed her, in a garden he hated for it and loved as she had, and came to visit it frequently. See Endelyn Larrensire.

    The Ten-Year War

    With the ex-jotuun Augmen as his enforcer, Anteo waged a war of extermination on the jotuun, devised by the two of them in secret, from the siege of Ancrage to that of the Temple of Isi. He was deprived of Augmen by the end of it, but reveled in a near-total victory. See Ten-Year War.

    Death

    Anteo never abdicated. Long after Endelyn's death, he choked to death. Servants tried to save him while Aethel sat and sipped his wine, and Alfor came in to see it (see Aethel Zuul). The High Throne passed to Aethel.

    Legacy

    The High Sovereign was never made to relinquish the powers granted to Anteo, and a Zuul still sits on the High Throne as of 211 ABU. Many stadians lost family in his fruitless war with Oprax, and turned on the Opraxi refugees who came after.
