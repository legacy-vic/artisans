    The late king of Phylomina, a smuggler captain who drove a dra'iq from the city when it was still called Malu, and was asked by its dying king to take his place. Son of Dendrilla Ariadne, who was cast out by the Zuuls over his birth, husband of Aiore, and father of Queen Vaenera. He died of a fall from his palace into the bay, which people take for his own doing, and Vaenera rules after him.

    Appearance & Character

    Alexium had reddish brown hair and dark eyes. He'd had to question who his father was his whole life, and it made him exceptionally curious.

    History

    Birth

    The second born son of Dendrilla and Prince Archon Zuul, he was born after Archon's death, with reddish brown hair and dark eyes. People did not believe he was Archon's child, and Anteo Zuul disinherited him. He was Archon's son.

    Aiore

    Alexium was already a successful man, well off because of money that Aethel Zuul sent, when he asked for Aiore's hand on Tabla Pise, and she accepted. He never bothered to get to know her family.

    King

    He and his crew drove a dra'iq from Malu with their vessels, and the dying King Arabah, his longtime friend, pleaded with him to take his place as ruler. He restored the city, gave it a new name, and kept a secret in his palace for its sake. See The Fall of Malu.

    Azra

    He raised Azra as his own until Aiore confessed that Kade Asher was her father. When Aiore chose to leave with her rather than give up Kade, he refused to let his wife go, and cast the child out instead. See Vaenera & Azra's Origin.

    When he heard that Morowai had burned, he felt exceptionally guilty, believing that he'd sent Azra to her doom. She'd grown up thinking that her own family hated her, especially him.

    The truth

    He'd known of his birth in small part, from how his mother reacted whenever there was news of the Zuuls. It was only after Morowai that it was confirmed, when word came from overseas that Dendrilla had died. Her belongings were sent to him, and her family had intentionally stored the proof with the rest of her things: Aethel's reply (see Dendrilla Ariadne).

    It was a quiet and disappointing revelation, both in himself for casting Azra out, and in being related to such a detestable man as Anteo. His guilt was compounded by the parallels to how Anteo had treated him when he was born. Then he questioned why his mother and her family had hidden the truth from him. If rumor had begun to spread, Anteo, notorious for his violent nature, would have acted on it. Turning Dendrilla out had been, in truth, a small mercy. After Anteo's death, she kept it hidden because Aethel had asked her to. Alexium kept the reply in his storage beside Azra's toy (see Aiore).

    Death

    He died of a fall from his palace into the bay. Having seen him grieve after Morowai, people took it for his own doing. Alfor Zuul was behind it. He'd found Dendrilla's letter, and knew that a man with a better claim than his own sat on a throne, and it was one of Zuul's Hand who saw to the fall.

    Legacy

    Vaenera succeeded him, and rules Phylomina as of 211 ABU.
