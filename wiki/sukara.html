    Sukara

    Sukara

    Show on the map

    Sukara is a lush and vibrant city, and similar to its sister city, Sulala, both cities are circular in shape and are divided into rings based on social class and economic value. Sukara however, is divided into three rings instead of four.

    The first and outer ring is broken up into five islands, two of which are located in the firth between Sukara and Sulala. These two islands are heavily fortified and decorated, having two imposing statues standing guard in front of them. One of the god of skirmish, Rhodite, and the other of the goddess of weather, Phera, prompting both forts to be named after them respectively.

    The middle ring is for the city's civilians and is the widest of the three rings. This is where most of the residents live and it's known for its beauty and hospitality. The city's inhabitants enjoy a high standard of living and take pride in their city and its culture. Sukaras leaders are seen as approachable and relatable by the people they serve because, unlike Sulala, the Sukaran monarch lives on the same level as the wealthy.

    The center island, the palace, is open to the civilians with many bridges sprouting from it, connecting it to the middle ring. The palace, while being the home of the monarch, also serves as a monastery for people to conduct prayer and other appropriate activities.

    The city's location, between the lush jungle and the sparkling waters, provides a unique and breathtaking landscape made even more impressive by the statues posing in the Abunala Firth.
