    The Union

    The standard used by the Union, at the Royal Conclave and in its dealings. Each realm keeps its own ladder beneath its crown (see below), in Icos and Athura alike. Only the rulers of each kingdom, and their families, have to adhere to the Union's titles.

    • High King / High Queen
    • King / Queen
    • Duke / Dutchess
    • Lord Paramount / Lady Paramount
    • Lord / Lady

    By realm, as of 211 ABU

    • Stade: King; Archduke of Tuvar, Princes of Moirach, Karlem and Ibya, one Duke; Counts; Lord-Elector as an office.
    • Morgar: Grand Prince, Duke, Lord.
    • Bali: King or Queen; Emir, a city's ruler (such as Amenkhet of Neferopolis).
    • Abu: two Kings or Queens, at Sulala and Sukara (dual rulership); Hatya, ruler of a major city who isn't the top liege; Satrap, a provincial ruler; Ensi, a county ruler.

    Before feudalism

    Azkassar was the first civilization to establish feudalism, and "king" was a title heard only there. Ibya was governed by strategoi, each over a nome, who sat together in a senate. See The Peoples of Old Icos.
