    Joss Harrow

    Joss Harrow

    Gender
    Male

    A pirate captain of the Anklan Isles, once the first mate and closest friend of Kade Asher, and the man who burned Morowai. In 211 ABU, his crew are being hunted down one by one, and he hasn't told them by whom.

    History

    First mate

    Joss was one of the pirates who helped Kade kill those responsible for his parents' deaths, and rescue his sister, Elise. When Kade left for the sake of Aiore, he gave Joss the ship.

    The raid

    Joss and the crew never touched a ship of the Polys, for Elise's sake. Then Kade gave up their hideout, and it was her husband, Leander Polys, who came for them. Most of them escaped, and Joss lost the most.

    Morowai

    He struck back at Morowai, under the sails that Kade would know, and beat him in his final stand. See Vaenera & Azra's Origin.

    The hunt

    Kade, whom he'd left for the fire, has been killing his crew ever since. Joss could tell who it was from the first of them, by the way it was done. He hasn't told his crew, who don't know whom they're dying for, and he recruits more. By 211 ABU he and his closest mates are the ones Kade wants most.
