    Larrensire

    Also known as House Larrensire, The Larrensires

    An Azkassari noble house, the rulers of Endlemoire and Princes of Moirach, sworn directly to the king of Stade. Their principality encompasses Endletonn, Lortonn, Norvania, Bogwatch, Moirlander's Respite and Wietmoire (see Titles and Ranks). They gave the High Throne a queen in Endelyn Larrensire. The Ermsburrys hold Endletonn under them. The Zuuls owe them fealty for Wietmoire, which they hold by conquest, a sore point between the two houses.

    The blood of Azkassar runs thick, even if they don't agree politically with the Barrentynes. They're more than inclined to offer Reine their help, if she agrees to marry a Larrensire. See Houses of Stade.
