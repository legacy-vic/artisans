    An old hermit of Mandor Canyon, in the deserts of Bali, about eighty-nine years old, who keeps a shop of herbs, elixirs and oddities in a cave there. He's one of the last of the jotuun, which he keeps an absolute secret, and he hopes to bring the order back, but has no one to pass his knowledge on to.

    History

    The order

    Mirak is native to Athura. He was in the order by the time the Ten-Year War began, at the Temple of Khada, far from home, and couldn't have known what became of his family. During the war, he left the temple without leave to find them, and it fell while he was gone. He found the house empty, and came back to a sealed granite door. Those who'd stayed to protect their home were behind it. He never learned what became of his family.

    Ayala's apprentice

    By the war's end he'd lost everything, and he became the secret apprentice of Ayala. He was one of the seven jotuun who hid with her in Qis, and was there when Aethel Zuul found them. Ayala died protecting him near Medjet (see Ayala). It's the reason he's alive at all, and why he won't waste it.

    Hermit

    He remains in Mandor Canyon, but relocates every so often. He keeps his past and involvement with the jotuun an absolute secret, as Alfor Zuul carries on his grandfather's legacy.
