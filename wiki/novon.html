    Novon

    Novon

    Show on the map

    A small town located just outside the walls of Trion, formed by Opraxi refugees under the lordship of Lord Hiro Kuroshi (see The Founding of Trion). Despite being so close to one of the wealthiest and most prosperous cities in Athura, Novon is plagued by poverty and social unrest. The town is made up of mostly working-class and lower-income families who have been left behind by Trion's success. Many of Novon's residents feel that Trion's prosperity is built on their backs, and that the wealth of the city is not being shared with the rest of the region. They blame the Talvo Royal Family and the city's elite for their economic struggles and lack of opportunities.

    As a result, Novon has seen frequent protests and riots as residents demand greater economic equality and a fairer distribution of resources. The year the taxes rose, they came to Trion's gate and called for Queen Kota Talvo by her family's name. She didn't come out, and they've never called for her since. Nobody there knows that she pays for the winter grain. The town is also known for its poor living conditions, with many residents living in overcrowded and dilapidated housing. Despite the challenging circumstances, the people of Novon are a resilient and determined community, and they continue to fight for a better future for themselves and their families.
