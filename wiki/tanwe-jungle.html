    Tan'we Jungle

    Also known as Tan'we, Enchanted Forest

    The Tan'we Jungle, also known as the Enchanted Forest, is a massive tropical forest covering the majority of the southeastern fertile belt of Athura. Its range spans across the Starry Strait and into Ashla Sanctuary.

    Long ago, the Tan'we ranged as far north as the middle course of Mandor Canyon, which at the time was a great river called the Mes-Mandor. The jungle is the ancient ancestral home of the Abunali, and every atlae descends from the heart of it.

    The animals which reside in the jungle are greatly diverse, with many species having no other populace outside of the jungle; remaining untouched by the outside world due to the stalwart protection from the traditions of the Abunali and their Kashif.
