    Scylla

    Scylla

    Range
    Sommerset Isles, Oprax, Stade

    A mature female Dra'iq that inhabits the Sommerset Isles. Very little is known about Scylla other than her immense size and blood thinning howls, but thanks to the defeat of Jaraq we now know the insides of these animals are very 'alien', thus making them even more valuable than the largest animals outside of their order. Scylla hasn't been documented attacking any settlements, and the ringo tribes that inhabit the southern islands say this beast is benevolent, going out of her way to avoid attacking ringo flat-boats travelling off-island, while decimating any atlae ships that just so happen to pass by. Due to the strict boundary Scylla has outlined for many atlae travelers, very few have managed to fully explore any landmass that lay south of Icos and Athura. Needless to say, slaying the beast has become one of the most expensive bounties in all of the lands if any were foolish enough to try.
