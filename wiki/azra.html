    A nineteen-year-old who survived the burning of Morowai as a child, was taken in by Midori in Kama, and has since lived among Tibs' gang. Self-sufficient and quick to decide, she means to become a master hunter and seek retribution.

    Appearance & Character

    When she first came to Kama, her clothes resembled those of the nearby Abunali tribes, though something was off about them, and ornate teal tattoos showed where her skin was bare. She's stronger than she looks, makes friends easily, and is quick to brush her feelings aside.

    History

    Early Life

    Azra is the daughter of Aiore and Kade Asher, and the younger sister of Queen Vaenera. After Morowai was raided, Tibs brought her to Kama. For the raid itself, see Vaenera & Azra's Origin.

    Arrival in Kama

    Azra met Artemis at Midori's orphanage, having traveled a good deal farther than he had, and offered to help him carry furniture minutes after telling him her home was gone. Having once argued with a sister of her own, she sent him back to make amends with Afina after the two had fought. When Midori left Kama, she took Azra with her, unwilling to leave another child under Tibs' influence.

    The Kashif Trials

    In Khamu, Azra made friends quickly, Amori among them. She led her group as pathfinder in the first of the Kashif Trials, and they were the first to finish. When the groups were reformed, she joined her friends, who were two members short, under Marshal T'Akoteh. Elder Azhotep anointed her with the mark of Atta, for her strength and independence.

    Tibs' Gang

    She later lived among Tibs' gang, in the ruins of an old temple, where he counted her among his favorites. To her, thieving was a way to survive, and the means to one day live in a city, or even a small town.

    Story

    Spoiler: Episode 2

    She'd been on Artemis' trail for some time when she found him in Dunis. She told him that Tibs truly needed him, and when he wouldn't come, had her mercenaries shackle him. He told her he wished Midori had only seen her for the stray she was, and she struck him for it. Shaman Ankti had the two settle it by Nalusi Ba'et. Azra landed the first two strikes, but lost her temper and her rhythm, and stepped out of the ring. She claimed to be a headhunter, though there's a price on her own head. By the time Ankti was done with her, Artemis had gone.
