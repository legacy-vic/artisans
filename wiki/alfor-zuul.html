    Also known as Zuul, High King

    The fourth and current High King of the Bicontinental Union, seventy-eight years old, and the grandson of Anteo Zuul. He took the High Throne after his father, Aethel Zuul, and has since become a recluse in High Sonham, a seat whose location the realms don't know. He keeps about four courtiers, who travel at his behest to any ruler's court, and hosts the monarchs once a year at the Royal Conclave. He has no heir. He's raised a ward, Kiro, and mentors the young Queen Vaenera of Phylomina. Charming and detached, he plays with the realms from a distance, and lets Talvar suffer Nirva without his aid.

    Appearance & Character

    Alfor has black hair with patches of grey, and eyes of a faded red. Much like his father, he's thin, and when he was younger he was considered to be handsome as well. Late in life, he's become reclusive and let himself go, and his claws and hair grow unkempt. He kept his hair medium-length for a while, before he let it grow down to his legs. He has two extra fingers on his hand, near concealed beneath his sleeve. They're rumored of, and the rumor is true. He's charming, observant, and strategically playful, with a cruelty that comes easily to a man so detached from empathy. He takes after his father's way of speaking, and enjoys sparring with his words rather than raising his voice. He manipulates from a distance, and doesn't let himself be tied to the outcome.

    History

    Anteo's death

    He was young when his grandfather died. He heard the panicked rushing of servants, and entered the hall to find his father sitting unbothered, sipping his wine and quietly humming to himself, as they tried to save Anteo. It was the only time he ever admired his father, and then came Karlaine.

    Karlaine

    He stood beside his father, and watched, when Aethel made a king of Onfroi (see The Crowning of Onfroi). He saw a crown given away for an honest answer, and thought it the weakest thing he'd ever watched a king do. It's where his contempt for Aethel and for Talvar began.

    Tybal

    It was on a mission to mend their family's feud with the Talvos that Nirva took his brother. Aethel had proposed it, and it was discussed among the household. Alfor rolled his eyes, and views the mission as foolish. He probed his brother on it in private, and refused when Tybal invited him along, feeling as though he wasn't being heard by either his brother or his father (see Tybal Zuul).

    Ayala

    He hates Ayala. He believes that his father loved her more than his mother, and that their closeness drove Catalina Valentyre to her death. He's often wondered if Ayala bore an illegitimate child. As a prince, under his father's roof, he sent all seven of Zuul's Hand to kill her.

    His father

    Aethel died by Alfor's hand, of a cup of Varrow's Leaf tea (see Aethel Zuul). Alfor had always believed his father to be weaker than his grandfather, until Aethel apologized to him that night. He felt guilt for what he was about to do, and did it. He suspects that his father knew. The grin has never left him, and he's never been able to decide if his father was forgiving him or had won.

    High King

    His elder brother, Tybal, died before their father, so the High Throne passed to Alfor, and with it the powers once granted to Anteo. He holds the Royal Conclave each year at High Sonham, and keeps about four courtiers, who travel at his behest to any ruler's court. King Lanis Kalmere is outwardly loyal to him.

    Phylomina

    Alexium Ariadne sent word to him for the armaments to defend his city from a dra'iq. See The Fall of Malu.

    Talvar

    King Onfroi Talvo has spent years appealing to him for aid against Nirva. He dismisses the pleas, viewing Talvar's suffering as the consequence of its founder's defiance.

    He knows that Onfroi has done nothing wrong, and isn't trying to villainize him, but rather to protect his family's elite status by refusing to fraternize with a house he believes to be lesser. He believes that the Zuuls owe the Talvos nothing, and that both families earned their places in the world, right of conquest always having been a widely accepted principle on Artisans. At the Royal Conclave, in answer to one of Onfroi's pleas, he coldly jested that perhaps Nirva swallowing his brother whole was an unfortunate sign from the gods.

    The garden

    He seldom visits the garden of his grandmother, Endelyn Larrensire, in High Sonham, but goes there to quiet his mind. He doesn't tend its plants.

    Kiro

    He's raised and nurtured Kiro throughout almost the entirety of the boy's life, and educated him personally. Kiro refuses to question any order he's given. Alfor had the boy's jotuun guardian, Theara, executed, and spared him to mold him, which Kiro doesn't know.

    The Vessel

    He keeps the secret of The Vessel, and intends to learn everything it holds.

    The heir

    Alfor is infertile, and cannot have a legitimate heir. This is why he's taken on a mentor-like relationship with Queen Vaenera, who doesn't know her relation to the Zuul bloodline. He knows of it from a letter that Dendrilla Ariadne wrote to Anteo, which he found among Aethel's things after killing him. He was behind the death of her father, Alexium Ariadne, which left a young queen in need of a mentor.
