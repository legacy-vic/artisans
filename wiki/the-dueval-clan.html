    The Dueval Clan

    Also known as The Crimson Shroud, The Dueval Family, The Duevals

    The Crimson Shroud, known to most as 'the shroud', is an infamous cabal based in Bali. Known for their deep-rooted connections to the various historical royal families of Athura, they have amassed immense wealth through unsavory deals. The cabal is a consortium of begrudged noble bastards who aim to target the dynasties from which they descend. There are several clans of note who hold great power within the Crimson Shroud, but the Duevals are the foremost, being the founding dynasty. Athurans don't culturally carry surnames, so the name Dueval comes from V'Ain, the founder, who took the name of his ship and coined the surname, passing it onto respected crewmates and kin. This clan name has since become synonymous with the dynasty.

    A unique tradition within the Crimson Shroud is the dying of their hair. Within the Dueval family, a member's hair grows redder with each ritualistic dunking, initially intended to symbolize the blood on their hands. For the Duevals, the red hair is a mark of respect and authority, granted based on accomplishing feats for the family.

    Their connections, wealth, and ruthless enforcement has made the Crimson Shroud the most feared and respected syndicate in all of Athura. In spite of their illegitimacy, public executions were commonplace for those who repeatedly disrespected the families, including the seemingly untouchable rulers of Athura if any so happen to act reckless and speak ill of the shroud regardless of their diluted royal blood.

    The leading family of the shroud donned the titles of Icosian nobility as an insult to their Athuran customs. Though, since the bicontinental union, Sultans became Kings. The insult, much like the titles they were forged against, vanished with time.

    The Duevals note the color crimson as their defining standard. The 'Crimson King' or 'Crimson Queen' holds supreme authority, ruling with an iron grip. Many years after its founding, the Duevals came to control most of Athura's sovereignty through coercion, blackmail, and strategic alliances. They even ensured that any fallout from their illicit activities fell onto the royal families they manipulated, preserving the Duevals’ anonymity and protecting their interests.

    The Crimson Shroud maintained supreme power from the shadows.

    The Crimson Shroud specializes in a variety of enterprises such as smuggling, with a network that spans both continents, slave redistribution, ensuring no slaves potential goes wasted, and black market auctions, where rare and forgotten items are sold to the highest bidder. To protect their interests and keep other syndicates at bay, the Duevals offer hired muscle to maintain order in settlements that Athuran rulers wish to keep clean. These operations are meticulously managed, ensuring a steady flow of wealth and influence.

    Despite their formidable power, the reign of the Crimson Shroud inevitably came to an end during The Battle of Suran at the hands of the Keiran Clan. This marked the end of their dominance, shaking the foundations of the underworld they worked so hard to keep in order. The fall of the Crimson Shroud left a power vacuum in the underworld, allowing new crime families and organizations to form.
