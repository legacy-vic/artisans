    Bali

    Also known as Bali Desert

    Bali

    Show on the map

    The region of Bali is known for its arid climate and sprawling sea of sand, and seems almost desolate at first glance. But within the realm of Athura, there's much interest to be found within the white sands. Balians are atlae that are native to Bali, but other races of folk are native here too such as the Sudr'iq and Sutu'un ringo, as well as grolls like Savanna Grolls and Cave Grolls.
