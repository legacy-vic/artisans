    Artisia

    Also known as Goddess of Land & Planets, Shepherd of Worlds

    The stoic and ever-ancient Artisia assumes the role of a shepherd, guiding the movement of the land and celestial bodies over vast spans of time. Depicted as a regal and contemplative figure, she holds the weight of the world and the cosmic order upon her shoulders. Balance and equity are what she's all about, for without it, neither life or death would be possible on a dry, barren world. Artisia's actions influence the geological shifts, the ebb and flow of tides, and the positions of the celestial bodies. Her role as the protector of the planet and its interconnected systems instills a sense of reverence for the delicate balance of the natural world. Often, people tend to seek her blessings for fertile soil, favorable celestial alignments, and stable environments to thrive within. The most notable religious order dedicated to Artisia and her purpose are the Jotuun. However, the longer the jotuun were active, the more they started to slowly shift their focus from the balance of the world itself to the balance of more small-minded civil and political disputes. The colors most commonly associated with Artisia are brown, green, and grey.
