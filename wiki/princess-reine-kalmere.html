    The younger daughter of King Lanis Kalmere of Stade, by Queen Loranne Kalmere, and half-sister of Thea. Of Azkassari descent through her mother, she has Norvania and the queen in her favor, while her father refuses to name an heir.

    Appearance & Character

    Reine has her mother's looks: pale, with black hair streaked with bronze, and red eyes. She and Thea look nothing alike.

    Reine was taught from a young age to be more empathetic than the more traditional members of her mother's family.

    History

    The succession

    Her father refuses to name an heir. Reine has Norvania and the queen in her favor, with the Barrentynes leaning toward her, and the Larrensires at a price. See Houses of Stade.
