    Gurtai

    Gurtai

    Show on the map

    The Gurtai are a nomadic people of southwestern Icos, much older than the bloodlines of the modern stadian and opraxi, and far removed from the icosi that later came to the continent. They're a robust atlae, and as stubborn as they are old.

    History

    The Gurtai once lived along the south of the continent, especially in the fertile basin where modern Oprax resides. There they warred with the proto-Opraxi. Though the more robust of the two, the Gurtai weren't as organized, nor as experienced in fighting the diverse enemies the proto-Opraxi had grown accustomed to. Over a couple of centuries their numbers dwindled, as tribes either migrated west or burned. See The War for the Basin.

    The lands to the southwest weren't newly populated by this. The Gurtai had ventured that way for generations during seasonal migrations, but now they were forced to remain.

    It wasn't all war and chaos. Some interbreeding occurred, whether through peace treaties or prisoners of war, and some Opraxi carry Gurtai blood.

    Present Day

    For the long stretch between then and now, the tribes remained independent. Yet whispers pass through many ears to higher courts, speaking of a great unification of the tribes: a coalition so great that it could threaten the stability of Oprax and Stade alike, should they choose aggression as their first act.

    It's unknown who their mysterious ruler is, but they must be quite an imposing person to force order among a people as stubborn as the Gurtai.

    Shamans

    Blood Magic, forbidden in Oprax for as long as it has existed, is freely practiced among Gurtai shamans. It's rumored that The Blood Empress practices it as well. If so, it's believed she might have secured a pact with the Gurtai.
