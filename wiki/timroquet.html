    Timroquet

    Also known as God of Beasts & Dra'iq, The Primordial Dread

    Timroquet

    Place(s) of Worship
    Abu, Anklan Isles, Bali, Matau, Morgar, Oprax, Stade

    Timroquet originates from the Abunali culture, but his legend spreads as far as Upror on Icos. Revered as the most fearsome and elusive Dra'iq, Timroquet holds dominion over all dra'iq and rare beasts. Mythology describes Timroquet as a primal force, ensuring that dra'iq and beasts remain fierce and powerful, serving as a check against the dominance of other species like grolls, Ringo, Atlae, and even titans. Though never seen by mortals, the existence of Timroquet is etched into the mythology of Athura. This god's influence creates a veil of mystery and a sense of respect for the untamed wilderness of the natural world. Legend tells of a time when Oies was unsatisfied with his inability to create life like his one true love, Adena. Instead, being cursed with the predisposed ability to take life. Wanting more for himself, to acquire a new dominion, Oies approached Cezolis, recognizing her nurturing and transformative powers as the sun's divine embodiment. Pitying him in his plight, Cezolis decided to assist him. Together, they birthed a divine creature, Timroquet, who was not too physically dissimilar from the wonderful creatures Adena had made. It's only flaw being that just like its father, it was predisposed to taking life. However unlike Oies, Timroquet quite enjoyed it. Blessed with the power of both Cezolis and Oies, they both could not keep Timroquet out of the mortal realm, and once it freed itself, it unleashed a new threat upon Artisans--the dra'iq.
