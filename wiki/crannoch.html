    Crannoch

    Also known as House Crannoch

    A noble house of Stade, seated at Bogwatch. Of Moirish blood: short, stubborn, and older here than anyone. Their people lived on these moors before Endlemoire or Linnenmoire, and they keep the only safe paths through the bog.

    This article is a stub. More may be written here in time.

    The Crannochs are a sour bunch who lack the sophistication and etiquette of the more refined houses, but their importance to the realm should not be understated. Bogwatch and Moirlander's Respite are key holdings to keeping the western tribes of Upror away from the more civilized kingdoms in the east, and the same goes vice versa. They receive any guests who wish to speak with them at the Respite, which they raised.

    The Crannochs want to be left alone, but armies that walk the moors have to answer to those who hold and know them, and every army crossing from west to east needs a guide. What's asked of them is circumstantial. It depends on who sits at Bogwatch, who their visitors are, and the reason for staying, among other factors. Generally, they're welcoming people, but there have been Crannochs in the past who've leveraged their importance beyond what is necessary, either for financial or other personal reasons. They don't kiss the ground that kings walk on.

    They and the Talvos had made peace with the hilltribes by the time Talvar was founded, hence why the hilltribes allowed the city to be built quietly (see The Fall of Talvar).

    As of 211 ABU, an old man, Colum Crannoch, sits at Bogwatch, and he's welcoming to certain guests: those who were friends to the Talvos. His father, Tormod Crannoch, let Gideon Talvo west through the moors to apologize to the hilltribes. When the tribes began bringing Gideon their feuds in secret, they brought them to the Respite, where west and east could both sit down without anyone asking why. The old man was a boy then, carrying bowls, and is the last person alive in the east who saw Gideon judge. Onfroi has never met him, and nobody has told him there's a man alive who heard his grandfather speak. Colum hasn't gone to him either. A Crannoch doesn't go to kings, so the door is open and it's Onfroi's to walk through. Colum believes him to be "as stuck up as the Stadian nobles he kisses up to." He's partly right. Onfroi does court the Stadian lords, but because his crown was granted rather than chosen, and he needs every friend he can get. Colum mistakes need for pride. His father never relayed any of it to the Kalmeres, who found out from the quiet in the west (see Talvo Royal Family). See Houses of Stade.
