    A seer of the Abunali in Dunis, about sixty-seven years old, whose visions have driven her all her life. She tells people of their fates bluntly, and her erratic manner has most of the village misjudging her. Her twin sister, Shaman Ankti, holds temporary eldership over Dunis, and the two don't acknowledge each other unless they have to.

    Appearance & Character

    Abasse's head is shaven, her eyes are of two colors, and her white ispaa follow no coherent design. She has visions, and tells people of their fates bluntly, whatever it may make them feel.

    History

    Early life

    Abasse and Ankti grew up in very strained familial circumstances. Their mother was very depressed due to the loss of their father, and often took the girls to an old temple in Ashla Sanctuary where they would engage in ritualistic meditation sessions, often dedicated to finding answers from the gods or otherworldly spirits. While Ankti grew cold and distant toward their mother, choosing to pursue her ambitions of becoming a kashif, Abasse grew closer. Not emotionally, but rather they shared a common interest in divination, and unlike her mother, Abasse possessed a special sight. Visions that they both presumed to be a gift from the gods, for she could see beyond the tangible.

    The sisters

    After the passing of their mother, Ankti felt an overwhelming amount of guilt for not being as present in neither her mother's or sister's lives. She attempted to reconnect with Abasse, and in doing so, the two decided that connecting with their spiritual selves was necessary. Though, this wouldn't last long, as a wedge had been driven between them due to their inability to connect emotionally. While it's true that both sisters weren't the emotional type, Abasse was especially distant in comparison. Talking dispassionately about their mother and seeing no point in feeling sorrow. She often went on scatterbrained tangents regarding the afterlife, bluntly telling people about their fates, regardless of how it may make them feel. While Ankti sought to give people guidance through the gods, Abasse often undermined the gods. In recent years, the two don't ever acknowledge each others existence unless they explicitly have to, despite maintaining similar roles and living within the same village of Dunis.

    Story

    Spoiler: Episode 2

    She foretold the arrival of Artemis, and told the village that he was a gift from the hidden ones. When he came to her yurt, she was painting its walls with his story: a white void, a descent into darkness, a god beyond a cracked wall, trust placed in the untrustworthy, and a reflection of himself. She told him the words he'd later speak at the Nalusi Ba'et, and that a being the whispers were silent about would be the last he saw. She told Azlan to keep him on his path.
