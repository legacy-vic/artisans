    Featured article

    Abasse The Seer

    A seer of the Abunali in Dunis, about sixty-seven years old, whose visions have driven her all her life. She tells people of their fates bluntly, and her erratic manner has most of the village misjudging her. Her twin sister, Shaman Ankti, holds temporary eldership over Dunis, and the two don't acknowledge each other unless they have to.
